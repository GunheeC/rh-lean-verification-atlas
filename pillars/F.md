## What is being assumed?

Let $N(\mathcal C)=\mathrm{Closed}(\mathcal C,(F_m,H)\mapsto\mathrm{LinkN}(F_m))$ and let $D_*=\mathrm{DefiniteSideCondition}$. Write $\mathrm{HypsAlt}(N,D_*)$ for the record that holds the family, selection and proofs of these conditions together. F asserts that at least one such record exists
$$
F\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists w:\mathrm{HypsAlt}(N,D_*),\quad \top .
$$

Its Lean form, transcribed from the argument of `UpperTheory.Top.zero_free_all_of_green_v_win`, is

```lean
Nonempty (_root_.GlobalTheory.Fc.HypsAlt
  (fun 𝒞 => Closed 𝒞 fun Fm _ => LinkN Fm)
  _root_.GlobalTheory.Fc.DefiniteSideCondition)
```

There are no free choices of a character, a point or a different selection for each row in this outer proposition. The family class $\mathcal A$, its raw selection $\sigma$, the function $c_T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ and all the dependent fields are components of $w$. In particular the norm field is $N(D_3(3,\mathcal A))$ and the definite-row field uses $D_*,\mathcal A,\sigma$ together. The underlying family class can range over all $\varepsilon$ and families at that $\varepsilon$.
