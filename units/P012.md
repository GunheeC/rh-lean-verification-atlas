## Statement

Assume $V$ and the record `OpenLiteratureStatements`. Its first and third fields assert $\mathcal R$. Its second asserts that for every number field with algebraic closure, valuation subring of that closure, and finite place below the subring, a local parameter map satisfying `IsRecT` exists. Then $H$ holds.

## Assumptions

- Assume the Selmer window $V$.
- The open-literature record includes both stated copies of the polarized row and existence of compatible local reciprocity/parameter maps at the designated places.
