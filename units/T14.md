# T14 · An averaged slope bound over deep components

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

On a good deep component datum meeting the rank, weight-gap and prime-factor restrictions, one can bound component slopes from below on average with an explicit error. A single constant works across those data, furnishing the averaged estimate used in the covolume comparison.

## Statement

Assume the eight statements T17–T24. There is a real $C\ge0$ uniform in the following data. Let a core $T$ on $D$ satisfy the core laws and $m^2\le k$. Take $1\le j<m$, a deep datum $Y$ of index $j$, an integer $e\ge0$ and a real $o$ with $Y$ good at $(e,o)$. Suppose its integer $M'$ has at most fifteen distinct prime factors. For each admissible weight $u$ with gap $g>0$, there are real numbers $s_Z$, indexed by components, such that

$$
s_Z\le\mathrm{compVolSlope}_{Y,\xi_R,8,\mathrm{mixVals}(B),Z}(u), \qquad \frac{\sum_Z^{\mathrm{fin}}s_Z}{\#\mathrm{Comp}} \ge -C\,\mathrm{errX}(\ell(D),u,g),\qquad B=\mathrm{bndB}(u,g).
$$

Here the weight decreases strictly at each embedding, and differences from each index on the $q$-side to any other index there or on the $p$-side have absolute value at least $g$.

- Assume the eight inputs T17–T24.
