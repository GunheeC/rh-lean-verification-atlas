## Statement

The two-test-prime orbit bound implies Case I: for each primitive character choose two auxiliary primes and an exponent; beyond both primes the log-prime-weighted sum of lengths over every finite set of odd Case-I primes is bounded by (host level times cutoff) to that exponent, and every length is finite.

$$
\mathrm{CorGK}\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{CountI}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume the orbit bound at the two test primes (CorGK).
- Specialize to a primitive character, an eligible host and a finite set of odd Case-I primes beyond the auxiliary primes.
