## Statement

SizeWindows is equivalent to RestSize together with the bound $\mathrm{Err}\le x^{C\varepsilon}$ times the error bracket, the two lower bounds $x\ge3$ and $x^{3\varepsilon/8}\ge26$, and the remaining sign, count and scale inequalities (including $m\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon/2}$ and $xm\le k$).

$$
\mathrm{SizeWindows}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathrm{RestSize}\wedge \mathrm{Err}\le x^{C\varepsilon}\mathrm{Br}\wedge x\ge3\wedge x^{3\varepsilon/8}\ge26\wedge \varepsilon>0\wedge C\ge0\wedge n>0\wedge b>0\wedge a_E\ge0\wedge n_{T,2}\le n\wedge m\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon/2}\wedge xm\le k.
$$
