## Statement

Under $0\le C_t$, $t(n,\epsilon)\le C_t n^3$ for $n\ge1$, both Zuckerman thresholds and In91, WBp holds for every real $C_b$. In its separated-parameter range the weighted minor moment is at most $b^{C_5(2r+2)^5}\max(1,G^{-1})^{40(r+1)^2}jN2$ at every local point with $G>0$, uniformly in the allowed coefficient and derivative order.

## Assumptions

- Use the nonnegative majorant and cubic bound for the thresholds.
- Assume both Zuckerman threshold estimates and the In91 input.
