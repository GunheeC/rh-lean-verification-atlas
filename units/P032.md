# P032 · Integral bar cycles with uniform residue evaluations

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A difference condition on an element of a cyclotomic layer yields one integral bar cycle whose good finite-field reductions have the prescribed evaluations. The cycle and its exceptional factor are chosen before the reduction, character and evaluation family, giving the uniformity needed for counting.

## Statement

Assume functoriality and the boundary identities for chains and prisms, the prism composition law, the specified basic bar cycle and its coefficient relation, the interpolation distribution, and the Steinitz transport hypothesis. In any layer as above take $E=e+1$, $e\ge0$, $m=2E+1<\ell^c$, and $a=\kappa^E$. For any $\alpha\in F^\times$ satisfying the difference condition there are $N$, a degree-$m$ bar cycle $y$ on $\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathcal O_L)$ over $\mathbb Z/q$, and $0\ne b\in\mathcal O_F$ such that the following holds. For every reduction $r:\mathcal O_F\to k$ to a finite field which is onto on $\mathcal O_L$ and has $r(b)\ne0$, for every character $v:k^\times\to\mathbb Z/q$, and for every additive evaluating family $\theta$ satisfying the scalar law for $r(\zeta)$, there is a residue $u\in k^\times$ of $N_a(\alpha)$ for which

$$
\theta\bigl(\operatorname{red}_r y\bigr)= \prod_{\substack{0\le j<m\\j\ne E}}(a-\kappa^j)\,v(u) \quad\text{in }\mathbb Z/q.
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume the chain/prism functoriality, boundary and composition identities, the basic bar cycle with its coefficient relation, interpolation and Steinitz transport.
- In the cyclotomic layer set $E=e+1$, $e\ge0$, $m=2E+1<\ell^c$ and $a=\kappa^E$. The element satisfies the stated divisor difference condition.
