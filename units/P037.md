## Statement

Let $L$ be totally complex and $\ell$ prime. Suppose $\ell^c$ is the largest $\ell$-power order of a root of unity in $L$, $c\ge1$ and $c\ge2$ at $\ell=2$. Fix $E\ge1$. If for every allowed layer $F/L$ the preceding character group, defined with $a=(1+\ell^c)^E$, is finite of order at most $B$, then there exists $s\in\mathbb N$ such that

$$
\operatorname{shaInv}(L,\ell,E)=s,\qquad \ell^s\le B .
$$

## Assumptions

- Use totally complex $L$ with maximal $\ell$-power root order $\ell^c$, $c\ge1$ and $c\ge2$ when $\ell=2$.
- Fix $E\ge1$. The prescribed character groups are finite with the same bound $B$ over every allowed layer.
