## Statement

For a twist-good family whose every sufficiently large member has the Galois normalization preconditions and four ties, the recorded cut and two/three-prime assumptions imply uniform constants for the family link at every eligible lift.

$$
\mathrm{LinkN}(F).
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume the second-layer record, the cut hypothesis and a twist-good family.
- At every specified family index the normalization preconditions and four ties hold.
