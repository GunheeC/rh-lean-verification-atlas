# D123 · Arakelov degree of the normalized flat generator

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A flat generator in a particular CM frame carries prescribed norms at the infinite and finite places. The unit computes their normalized degree from a CM period and the root discriminant. This supplies an exact input to the tower calculation, including the finite contribution that an archimedean period alone would miss.

## Statement

Give an adelic norm to the flat generator. Its degree is $(p-q)/2$ times the real CM period logarithm plus q times twice the logarithm of the square root of d minus the logarithm of the root discriminant.

$$
N\ \text{adelic},\qquad \deg N=\tfrac{p-q}{2}\operatorname{Re}\log\Omega(E,\chi)+q\bigl(2\log\sqrt d-\log\operatorname{rd}(E)\bigr).
$$

## Assumptions

- Use a CM host, the stated Kronecker character, CM type and prescribed embedding of the host square root.
- The section is the specified flat generator and its assigned norms satisfy the norm-comparison condition.
