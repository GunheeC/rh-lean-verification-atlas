## Statement

For the specified embedding and compatible local restriction maps, a continuous cocycle satisfies the Bloch–Kato finite condition after restriction precisely when its pullback to the indicated decomposition subgroup satisfies that condition.

$$
c\circ\psi\in Z^1_f(\varphi,\rho\circ\psi)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ c|_{\mathrm{dec}_j}\in Z^1_f(\mathrm{pr}_2,\rho\circ\mathrm{pr}_1).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix the algebraic closure embedding and an open local embedding compatible with the two restriction maps.
- Use the stated coefficient module, its Galois action and a continuous cocycle.
