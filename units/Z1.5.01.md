# Z1.5.01 · Coefficient representations and the Weyl bound

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Over a fixed totally complex field and in fixed rank, the required coefficients at all embeddings can be realized by one algebraic representation of dimension polynomial in the weight bound. This controls the coefficient dimension as the weights vary.

## Statement

For each totally complex number field $E$ and rank $n$, there are constants $c,A$ such that for every strictly decreasing tuple with $|m_i|\le H$ there is one algebraic coefficient representation with the negative of $\mathrm{infOf}(m)$ at every embedding and dimension at most $c(H+1)^A$.

## Assumptions

- Fix a totally complex number field and a rank.
- The integral weights strictly decrease and are bounded in absolute value by $H$.
