# P096 · Equality of localized class lattices

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A change of representative relating two classes at a chosen place carries their lattices to equal localizations at every height-one prime away from that place. The comparison therefore confines the possible discrepancy to the designated place.

## Statement

Fix standard column data, a word $u$, a stage $i\le m$, a place $v$, classes $c,c'$, and $\gamma\in\mathrm{GL}_{N_i}(E)$ relating $c,c'$ at $v$. For every height-one prime $Q$ of $\mathcal O_E$ whose contraction differs from $v_F$, the localizations in $E^{N_i}$ satisfy

$$
(\gamma L_{c'})_Q=(L_c)_Q .
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix standard column data, a word, a stage $i\le m$, a place and two classes related there by the specified $\gamma$.
- The height-one prime of the coefficient ring contracts to a prime distinct from the designated place below.
