## Statement

For every $N\ge1$ and every primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$, if $L(s,\chi)=0$ with $0<\Re s<1$, then $\Re s=1/2$.

## Assumptions

- Take a primitive Dirichlet character of positive modulus.
- The point is a zero in the open strip $0<\Re s<1$.
