# Z1.4 · Graded cotangent length

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Under the graded-model hypotheses, the degree-minus-one cotangent length lies above the specified module length and below twice a finite sum of orbit valuations. The two comparisons turn orbit evaluations into an upper bound for that module.

## Statement

In the marked graded model satisfying M1–M5, any finite cover $T$ of two-sided point orbits gives $L\le 2\sum_{\xi\in T}v(\xi(a))$ for the fixed element $a$ and the length $L$ of the degree $-1$ cotangent quotient. The integral normalized injection and fixed-weight lifts give the opposite comparison $\mathrm{length}(V)\le L$ for the specified module.

## Assumptions

- The marked graded model satisfies M1–M5 and the point set covers two-sided orbits.
- For the opposite inequality use the normalized injection and fixed-weight derivation lifts.
