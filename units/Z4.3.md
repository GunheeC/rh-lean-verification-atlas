## Statement

For a used tuple $\mu$, at an off-level $p\ne T_u$, $p\notin\{g_0,g_1\}\cup Q(\mu)$, the size and error identities and four prime-case coefficient laws imply $\mathrm{ColGe}(T,u,\mu,w,x,\mathrm{lowS}(u,w))$ for every $x\in S_{\rm ram}(u,w)$. The registered root measures this numerical implication. The selected-row application also uses the shared covariance law, the adjacent kernel expansion and the grouped inputs at the column, inert-series and prime-sum callers.

## Assumptions

- Use a tuple off level, with the prime outside its prescribed excluded set.
- The size and error identities and all four prime-case coefficient laws hold.
