## Statement

On a compact Hausdorff space with a finite holomorphic atlas of complex dimension e+1, let a section of a metrized line datum have a simple normal crossings divisor with multiplicities `m_k` and holomorphic component inclusions. For any smooth test function and e admissible potentials, integration against the log norm equals the sum of the component integrals weighted by `m_k` minus the ambient curvature integral.

$$
I_A((v,\Phi),\log\|s\|)=\sum_k m_k I_{W_k}(\iota_k^*\Phi,\iota_k^*v)-I_A((L,\Phi),v).
$$

## Assumptions

- Use a compact Hausdorff space with a finite holomorphic atlas, an admissible metrized line and section, and a simple normal crossings divisor with holomorphic component inclusions.
- The test function is smooth and each of the $e$ potentials is admissible.
