## Statement

Assume $H'$. Fix an odd prime $\ell$, $e\ge1$ with $\ell-1\nmid2e$, an imaginary quadratic field $K$, an even positive level $N$ coprime to $D_K=|\operatorname{disc}K|$, with $\ell\nmid D_K h_K$, and an algebraic closure $\Omega/\mathbb Q$ containing $K$. Write $\kappa$ for the cyclotomic character and $\theta$ for the quadratic character cutting out $K$. Take $\psi$ and $W:G_{\mathbb Q}\to \mathrm{GL}_2(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ satisfying the source conditions `Used`. Explicitly, $\psi$ factors through level $N$, has prime-to-$\ell$ order, has square one on inertia at $\ell$, and $\kappa^e\psi$ is even. The representation $W$ has level datum $(ND_K;e,-e)$, the ordinary polynomial condition for $(e,-e)$, and determinant $\theta$. On inertia at $\ell$ its trace is $a+a^{-1}$ for $a=\kappa^e\psi$. Globally the residual trace is $\theta a+a^{-1}$. At a chosen place above $\ell$, inertia lies in $\ker\theta$ and the prescribed flag characters restrict to $\kappa^{\pm e}\chi$ for a single character through level $\ell$. The restriction to $G_{K(\mu_{\ell^\infty})}$ is irreducible. Suppose a weight-$2e+1$ newform realizes $W\otimes\kappa^{-e}$ via $\iota$, and on every quadratic CM restriction on which $W$ is irreducible it has the witness `WitA`: local unlinked flags above $\ell$ become power-unlinked, and each attached regular algebraic cusp form has a common real parameter for its local window away from $\ell$ and at the power-unlinked places. For both $W$ and $W\otimes\theta$, every cocycle satisfying the Bloch–Kato local conditions is a coboundary.

## Assumptions

- Assume $H'$, an odd $\ell$, $e\ge1$, $\ell-1\nmid 2e$, and the stipulated imaginary quadratic field, closure and even positive level with the coprimality restrictions.
- Use the Used character, representation and ordinary-polynomial inputs with the given local and irreducibility conditions.
- The weight-$2e+1$ newform realizes the indicated twist; every quadratic CM restriction on which the representation stays irreducible has the specified local flags and common real window parameter.
