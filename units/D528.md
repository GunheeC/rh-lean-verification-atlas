## Statement

Cohomological control, the three algebraic comparison lemmas, Kato input, two rigidity results, level admissibility and unramified automorphy bound a single finite Selmer length by twice a sum of trace-difference valuations for an admissible finite family.

$$
\mathrm{OneCharU}_{s}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Use the cohomological input, three algebraic comparison lemmas, two-twist Kato input and both adjoint rigidity results.
- Impose level admissibility and the unramified-coefficient automorphy input on the finite family.
