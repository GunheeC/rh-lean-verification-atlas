## Statement

For every characteristic-zero field $k$ and reduced separated finite-type $k$-scheme $X$, there is a sequence of blowups $f:X'\to X$ with $X'$ smooth. Its restriction over the smooth locus is an isomorphism. The reduced inverse image of the complement is a simple normal crossings divisor. The formal wrapper uses a closed resolution term independently of its record argument.
