## Statement

For the given endomorphism, the de Rham homology characteristic polynomial is the image of the integral characteristic polynomial for the $\ell$-adic endomorphism.

$$
\operatorname{charpoly}(f_{\rm dR})=\bigl(\operatorname{charpoly}(\varphi)\bigr)_{\mathbb Z\to k}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Use an abelian scheme over an algebraically closed field and a group endomorphism.
- The integral lattice and its power-torsion evaluation maps are compatible with the endomorphism; the prime is invertible in the base field and de Rham homology is finite-dimensional.
