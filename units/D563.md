# D563 · Absorbing the four size-window errors

Under the size-window inequalities, a normalized inequality with four half-power error terms implies the stated bound on the scalar prime-sum quantity. This numerical step explains how the density and column factors can be divided out while retaining a half-power estimate with an explicit epsilon loss.

## Statement

Let $n,n_T,n_{T,2},m$ be natural numbers and $C,\varepsilon,x,b,k,a_E,\mathrm{Err},G_x$ real. Assume the size-window inequalities, including $x\geq3$, $n,b,\varepsilon>0$ and the bounds on $m,k,a_E$ and $\mathrm{Err}$ in the linked definition. If

$$
n_T mb\,\frac{n_{T,2}}{2n}\bigl(G_x-4x^{1/2+\varepsilon}\bigr) \leq \mathrm{Err}+2n_T mb/x,
$$

then $G_x\leq x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}$.

## Assumptions

- Assume SizeWindows on the counts and real parameters, including positive $b,\varepsilon,n$, nonnegative $C$, and the size, count and bracket bounds in D566.
- With $Q=n_Tmb$, $r=n_{T,2}/n$ and $X=x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$, assume $Qr(G_x-4X)/2\le\mathrm{Err}+2Q/x$.
