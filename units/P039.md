# P039 · Uniform discriminant dependence for integral homological torsion

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A single exponent bounds the log of the full homological torsion order in the designated range of ranks and degrees, using the field degree and discriminant. This gives a uniform estimate as the field and twist parameter vary.

## Statement

There exists $C\in\mathbb R$ such that for every totally complex $L$, $e\ge1,\ 1\le N\le4e+6,\ 0\le m\le2e+1$, putting $n=[L:\mathbb Q]$ and $D=|\operatorname{disc} L|$, the full torsion $T$ in $H_m(G_N,\mathbb Z)$ is finite and

$$
\log |T|\le \exp\!\left((2en)^C\log(2D)\right).
$$

## Assumptions

- Take a totally complex number field and a positive integer $e$.
- The matrix rank and homological degree satisfy $1\le N\le4e+6$ and $0\le m\le2e+1$.
