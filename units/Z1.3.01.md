## Statement

For the ordinary normal form, unramified weight-$2e+1$ source and the admissible tower, projection, level, smoothing and lattice data, construct integral continuous twisted cocycles at all layers, compatible under pushdown, with a global specialization whose single $\ell$-power multiple satisfies Sys, IwSys2 and SpLink.

## Assumptions

- Fix the ordinary normal form and an unramified modular source of weight $2e+1$.
- Use admissible tower, projection, level, smoothing and lattice data.
