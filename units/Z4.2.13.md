# Z4.2.13 · Period, lattice and theta-vector laws of the tied base

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a tied entry the period is nonzero, its period lattices obey the stated lattice law, and the normalized theta family satisfies both vector and transformation requirements for the same character. That common character is necessary to use the covariance law for the chosen entry.

## Statement

For every tied kernel entry $t$, its period is nonzero, its admitted period lattices satisfy LatAll, and its normalized theta family satisfies VecP and EqP for the same character.

## Assumptions

- Fix a tied kernel entry with its associated character, period and normalization.
