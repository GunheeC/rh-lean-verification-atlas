## Statement

Let $C$ be algebraically closed with a nonnegative-real-valued valuation and let $R\to C$ identify $R$ as its valuation ring. Every abelian scheme $A/R$ with a relatively ample invertible sheaf admits group homomorphisms $i:A\to P$, $r:P\to A$ with $r i=1_A$. The scheme $P/R$ is abelian, smooth of a specified relative dimension and carries a rigidified symmetric relatively ample invertible sheaf with trivial polarization kernel.
