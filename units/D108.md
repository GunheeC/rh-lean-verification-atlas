## Statement

For an odd prime and a nontrivial character of order prime to it whose reduction differs from cyclotomic, a unit first Bernoulli value for the inverse character forces every residual cocycle satisfying UnrEv to be a coboundary.

$$
\operatorname{UnrEv}(c)\ \Longrightarrow\ [c]=0.
$$

## Assumptions

- The prime is odd and the character is nontrivial, of order prime to it and distinct from cyclotomic after reduction.
- The first Bernoulli value of the inverse character is a unit and the residual cocycle satisfies UnrEv.
