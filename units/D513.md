## Statement

For each $0<\varepsilon<1/52$, simultaneously for all sufficiently large scales and every frame of order in $[x^{2+8\varepsilon},2x^{2+8\varepsilon}]$, there exist ranks inducing exactly that frame, with the prescribed exponent, rank, size and growth inequalities of RankVal.

$$
x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le {\rm ord}(\phi)\le2x^{2+8\varepsilon}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists R,\ R^{\rm frame}=\phi\ \wedge\ \operatorname{RankVal}(s,R).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix $0<\varepsilon<1/52$ and a sufficiently large scale of that exponent.
- Require $x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le\operatorname{ord}(\phi)\le2x^{2+8\varepsilon}$.
