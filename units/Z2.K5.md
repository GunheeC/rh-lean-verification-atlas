If the separated finite families satisfy the prescribed cap, the equivariant character group of a cyclotomic layer is finite with an explicit order bound. The formula keeps the losses from twist valuation, field degree and class number, which matter when comparing different layers.

## Statement

For totally complex $L$, a layer $(F,\zeta,\gamma)$ and $E\ge1$, the hypothesis $\mathrm{Few}$ with bound $B$ implies finiteness of the prescribed equivariant character group $H$ and $|H|(\ell^\nu)^{r_2(L)}\le B\ell^{(b+v_\ell(E))g+c+v_\ell(E)}$, where $g=2[L:\mathbf Q]+\lfloor\log_2 h_L\rfloor+2$.

## Assumptions

- Fix totally complex $L$, the cyclotomic layer, its root and generator, and $E\ge1$.
- The Few assertion on the designated norm classes holds with bound $B$.
