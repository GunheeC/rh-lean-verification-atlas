## Statement

Assume $F$, unramified Howe duality in its two spherical-span directions, and the convergent unitary Siegel–Weil input. For each $\varepsilon$ and $C_F\ge6510$ some $x_0$ works for all size data of exponent $\varepsilon$ at $x\ge x_0$ satisfying the row sizes and the Eisenstein tie. For every compatible finite-group family, real datum with its level character and group record, grid $(1,C_F,b)$, Weil model above the datum, row volume and nonzero smooth vector $w$, there exist a real vector $\rho$ and coefficient functional $\xi$. For every nonzero $t$ in the trivial-character evaluation space there are a column $\Psi$ and grid datum $\Phi$ for which the pairing of the row form of $(w,t)$ converges and is nonzero.

## Assumptions

- Assume the local theta input, both spherical-span implications of unramified Howe duality and convergent unitary Siegel–Weil.
- Use row-size and Eisenstein ties, $C_F\ge6510$, a compatible Weil model, admissible grid and nonzero smooth vector.
