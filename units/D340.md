## Statement

The selected period Gram expression is bounded by the displayed exponential of the sum of boundary logarithms on the polydisc.

$$
c I\preceq G(t)^{-1}\preceq C L(t)I,\qquad \|\mathrm d G_{pq}(t)\|\le C e^{L(t)/2},\qquad L(t)=\exp(1)+\sum_{i<a}\log(1/\lvert t_i\rvert^2).
$$

## Assumptions

- Take log-period data and sections differentiable on the unit ball, linearly independent at the origin.
- The chart radius satisfies $0<\rho\le1$ and the permuted vanishing boundary coordinates obey the prescribed condition.
