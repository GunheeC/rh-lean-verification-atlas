## Statement

On the enriched class D3(3,A), duty, tied real-place second-layer data, the mass-ratio estimate and the genus-mass hypotheses give two uniform implications. Chosen data of exponent 3 give fullness and the prescribed degree/rank relation. Tuple and discriminant-size bounds give the global comparison with constants 2, CH, 3, 2, 0.

$$
\mathrm{ChosenE}(F,3,C_H)\Rightarrow\mathrm{NuFullF}(F)\wedge\mathrm{MuRank}(F),\quad \mathrm{TuSize}(F)\wedge\mathrm{DGamSize}(F)\Rightarrow\mathrm{LinkGLOBAL}(F,2,C_H,3,2,0).
$$

## Assumptions

- For the family class assume duty and closed real-place second-layer data on the enriched class.
- Use the mass-ratio bound and genus-mass hypotheses. The first implication assumes chosen data of exponent three; the second assumes the tuple and discriminant size bounds.
