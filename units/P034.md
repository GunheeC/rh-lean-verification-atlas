# P034 · Odd homology after successive block operations

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

In the stable range for a totally complex field, iterating the specified difference of block repetition and stabilization leaves an integral span of rank at most the number of complex places. This bounds how many independent odd-homology classes can survive the operations.

## Statement

Let $L$ be totally complex, $k\ge0$ even, $q=k+1<r_2(L)$, and $n\ge2q+4$. Fix any integer $t>0$. Starting with $z_0(x)=x$ in $H_q(G_n,\mathbb Z)$, define

$$
z_{j+1}(x)=(\operatorname{diag}_t)_*z_j(x) -t^{j+2}\operatorname{stab}_*z_j(x),
$$

where block repetition and stabilization both land in rank $t^{j+1}n$. The integral span of all $z_k(x)$ has rank at most $r_2(L)$.

## Assumptions

- Take a totally complex field, even $k\ge0$, $q=k+1<r_2(L)$ and $n\ge2q+4$.
- Fix a positive integer $t$ for the diagonal block and stabilization operations.
