## Statement

The Fargues exactness input gives the specified annihilator bound on the Hodge–Tate cokernel and identifies the Hodge–Tate kernel by orthogonality to the kernel of the dual logarithmic differential.

$$
\mathrm{FarguesExact}\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{FarguesHodgeTate}.
$$
