## Statement

For an admitted place and each lift stage $j<m$, the index-zero local differentiated entries satisfy the bounds $g_A$ and $g_B-\mathrm{alEven}$. For $1\le i\le r$ the $p$-weighted column differential satisfies the bound $g_B$ for all allowed integral parameters, selected columns and output monomials. Chapter P also treats preservation of the lattice by the normalized Hecke operator.

## Assumptions

- Use an admitted local place and a lift at stage $j<m$.
- The column, parameter and output monomial lie in the stipulated ranges.
