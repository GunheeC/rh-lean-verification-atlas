# N415 · Relative prime count in progressions

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a reduced progression whose modulus is divisible by eight, sufficiently large scales admit a relative prime-count estimate with an explicit error. A possible exceptional real zero contributes its own main-term correction. The estimate retains the dependence on the modulus when the progression varies.

## Statement

For $M>0$, $8\mid M$, reduced $a\bmod M$, $X\ge2$ and $\log X\ge C_0\log M\log(\log M+2)$, the prime count has main term $\bigl(\mathrm{li}_2(X)-\chi(a)\mathrm{li}_2(X^\beta)\bigr)/\varphi(M)$ with relative error $\ll e^{-c\log X/\log M}+e^{-\sqrt{c\log X}}$. The character term is omitted if the exceptional window is empty. Chapter T derives the relative error and exceptional-window adjustment from Thorner–Zaman.

## Assumptions

- Use a reduced residue class modulo a positive multiple of eight.
- The endpoint satisfies $X\ge2$ and $\log X\ge C_0\log M\log(\log M+2)$.
