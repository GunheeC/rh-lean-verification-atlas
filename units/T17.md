# T17 · Arithmetic intersection bounded by small-section volume

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a Hermitian line satisfying the semipositivity and nefness hypotheses on the given arithmetic presentation, its top intersection is bounded by the volume of small sections in the specified chart lattice. This lets a count of small sections control an intersection number.

## Statement

Take finite blocks of homogeneous coordinates $y$ in a field $\Omega$, their vanishing ideal $P$, a birational presentation of arithmetic dimension $D+1$, and an intersection system $I$ satisfying its defining axioms. Let $A$ be a conjugation-invariant Hermitian line, semipositive relative to some $P_0\subseteq P$, nef on the fibres in its multidegree and nef on the generic fibre in the specified sense. Then

$$
I_P(A^{D+1})\le \mathrm{arVolP}\bigl(\text{small sections for }P,y,A,\, \mathrm{chartLat}(y,\deg A),1,D\bigr).
$$

## Assumptions

- Take a finite multihomogeneous birational presentation of arithmetic dimension $D+1$ and an intersection system satisfying its axioms.
- The Hermitian line is conjugation invariant, relatively semipositive, fibre-nef in its multidegree and nef on the generic fibre.
