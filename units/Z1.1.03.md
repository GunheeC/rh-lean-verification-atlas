## Statement

Over the linked CM field with odd coefficient prime, nonzero $M$ divisible by twice that prime and unramified splitting, an AdInW representation has a cyclotomic inverse twist of its trace-free adjoint that is conjugate-self-dual automorphic and satisfies AllW.

## Assumptions

- Use the linked CM field, an odd coefficient prime and a nonzero level divisible by twice that prime.
- Assume unramified splitting and membership of the representation in the adjoint window.
