## Statement

Let $F$ be a number field and $\ell$ a prime such that $\ell\ne2$ or $F$ has no real place. Assume finite-coefficient Poitou–Tate duality, local finiteness and local Tate duality. For every allowed set of local data $D$ and every $e\in\mathbb Z$, with $A_e=(\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell)(-e)$,

$$
\operatorname{length}H^2(G_{F,S},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1)) +\nu_{\rm Gal}(\ell,F,|e|) =\operatorname{length}\operatorname{Sha}^1(D,A_e) +\sum_{w\in D.S}\operatorname{length}H^0(F_w,A_e).
$$

All lengths are extended $\mathbb Z_\ell$-lengths; $\operatorname{Sha}^1$ is the kernel of the specified localization.

## Assumptions

- Take a number field, a prime $\ell$ odd or with no real places of the field, admissible local data and an integral twist.
- Finite-coefficient Poitou–Tate duality, local finiteness and local Tate duality hold.
