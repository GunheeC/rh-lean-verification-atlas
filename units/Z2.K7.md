# Z2.K7 · Full integral homology torsion

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a totally complex field, integral homology has finite torsion. In the specified range of degree and rank, its logarithmic order satisfies a bound with an explicit exponent in degree, twist parameter and discriminant. This makes the cost of full torsion available for prime-by-prime estimates.

## Statement

For totally complex $L$, the torsion in $H_m(\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathcal O_L),\mathbf Z)$ is finite. With $n=[L:\mathbf Q]$, $D=|\mathrm{disc}\,L|$, $E\ge1$, $1\le N\le4E+6$, and $m\le2E+1$, its log order is at most $(2D)^{(2En)^{24}}$.

## Assumptions

- Fix a totally complex field $L$.
- The quantitative assertion uses $E\ge1$, $1\le N\le4E+6$ and $0\le m\le2E+1$.
