## Statement

For a linked field pair and level $M$ with the unramified-splitting and inertia conditions, an open-irreducible, unlinked Eisenstein seed of weight $m+1$ with PR and the prescribed parity has a character twist over L in the stated adjoint window, $m\ge2$.

## Assumptions

- The linked fields and level satisfy splitting and inertia restrictions.
- Use an open-irreducible, unlinked ordinary seed with PR and the required weight and parity.
