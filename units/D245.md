## Statement

Let epsilon in the tilt have untilt 1 and suppose the untilt of its inverse Frobenius is different from 1. If y in the positive crystalline period ring has zero de Rham theta image after every nonnegative Frobenius iterate, then the Frobenius of y is a multiple of the image of the crystalline period t(epsilon) in that ring.

$$
\bigl(\forall n\ge0,\ \theta_{\rm dR}(\iota(\varphi^n y))=0\bigr)\ \Longrightarrow\ \varphi y\in\bigl(t(\varepsilon)\bigr)B_{\rm cris}^{+}.
$$

## Assumptions

- The untilt of $\varepsilon$ equals one and the untilt of its inverse Frobenius differs from one.
- For $y\in B_{\rm cris}^+$, the de Rham theta image of every nonnegative Frobenius iterate is zero.
