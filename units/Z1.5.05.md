## Statement

Under the automorphic hypotheses, LevelPNAlt and admissibility on restriction, classify the rational systems with any allowed symmetric weight at the chosen prime $p$ away from $M$. For fixed $n$ the size bound is polynomial in $M(H+1)$, independent of the odd coefficient prime distinct from $p$.

## Assumptions

- Assume the automorphic hypotheses, LevelPNAlt and preservation of admissibility on restriction.
- Use the prescribed weight and a prime away from the level.
