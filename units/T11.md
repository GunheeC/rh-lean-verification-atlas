## Statement

Assume the doubling Eisenstein input (right-half-plane summation and meromorphic continuation with moderate local regularizations) and the standard value statement T12. Over every admissible number-field pair and automorphism, each admissible triple of Weil models has real and finite matching data. The unprimed integrals converge on Schwartz vectors. On the prescribed primed test space the theta kernel has rapid decay in one variable with polynomial growth in the other, the integral converges and is moderate. For each matching choice some primed test integral is nonzero and there is a unique nonzero $c$ with $I(\Phi)=c I'(\Phi')$ for every matched pair, as functions on the common group. At good places the kernels of both finite quotient maps are the augmentation spaces on the test vectors, the primed local test spaces surject onto the quotient, and functionals killing the primed augmentation factor on test vectors through the quotient. The primed global test space is stable under the primed action.

There are two independent uniform triples of signs for the tower relations. For two matching models with comparison constants $c,c_1$ and an adapted pair of planes, $C\ne0$ depends on the geometry and real maps. Write $F(z)$ for the signed `stepFac` of the two plane ideals and $L(z)$ for the source's `charL` of $\nu,S_0$. In the $\beta$ step, with ranks $(a,b+2)$ and $(a,b)$ and $h=\lfloor (a-b-2)_+/2\rfloor$, one has $cL(h+1)=u c_1$ for some $u$ such that $uCF(-h)=1$. In the $\alpha$ step with ranks $(b+2,a')$ and $(b,a')$ and $h=\lfloor(b+2-a')_+/2\rfloor$, one has $c=CF(1-h)L(h)c_1$.
