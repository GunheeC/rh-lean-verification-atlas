## Statement

Assume $H'$, the level and Satake–Frobenius correspondence for imaginary quadratic fields, and preservation of admissibility whenever quadratic restriction is irreducible. For each $n\ge2$ there exist $c,A\in\mathbb N$. For all $m_1>\cdots>m_n$, $m_i+m_{n+1-i}=w_0\in\mathbb Z$, $|m_i|\le H$, $M\ge1$, and $p\nmid M$ prime, the rational place above $p$ admits data $D$ of weight $m$, purity $w_0$, and $|D.S|\le c[M(H+1)]^A$. These data classify $\mathrm{AdmRNAlt}(\iota,M,m,w_0)$ representations of $G_{\mathbb Q}$ at this place for every odd $\ell\ne p$, closure and $\iota$.

## Assumptions

- Assume $H'$, the imaginary-quadratic level and Frobenius correspondence, and preservation of admissibility under irreducible quadratic restriction.
- Use rank at least two, the specified symmetry and size of weights, and an allowed level and good prime.
