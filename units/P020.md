## Statement

Assume the local, automorphy, cohomological and CM input records of `LiteratureRankNHypothesis`, the existence of one $C_K\in\mathbb R$ satisfying `LemmaK`, and the four bounds `CountI`, `CountII`, `CountIII`, `CountV` of `HeightAndVanishing/Counts.lean:53–69`. Then the assertion $V$ above holds for every primitive $\psi$. In `LemmaK` the same $C_K$ bounds the log cardinality of the finite group $H^2(\mathrm{zlTw}(D,j+1))$ by $\exp((2j[L:\mathbb Q])^{C_K}\log(2|\operatorname{disc}L|))$ for every prime $\ell$, every admissible totally complex field $L$ with the required root of unity, closure and local datum $D$, and every $j\ge1$ (`LemmaK.lean:9–17`).

## Assumptions

- Assume the local, automorphy, cohomological and CM records of the rank-$n$ literature hypothesis.
- One constant $C_K$ satisfies LemmaK, and the four character-count assertions I, II, III and V hold.
