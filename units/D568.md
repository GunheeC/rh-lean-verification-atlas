## Statement

Under the ten size, division, comparison, counting, slope, height and finite-parameter assumptions, $\mathrm{Ga}(u)$ is at most $\mathrm{Main}(u)+\log|W(u)|+K_{0,v5}+M$ plus $(1-\bar\delta)^{-1}$ times $\left[4\log(1+x^{c_{HH}})(K_3+M+b)+(\bar\eta+4\sqrt{\bar e})x^{c_{HH}}b\right]$, where M is the weighted mean of Lo5.

$$
\mathrm{Ga}(u)\le \mathrm{Main}(u)+\log|W_u|+K_{0,v5}+M+\frac{4\log(1+x^{c_{HH}})(K_3+M+b)+(\bar\eta+4\sqrt{\bar e})\,x^{c_{HH}}b}{1-\bar\delta}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Use a Hida size setting, division, $E$ and $H$ comparison, counting and slope bounds.
- Assume quotient-slope, slope-inequality, height and finite-parameter conditions at the same unit.
