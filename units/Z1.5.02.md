# Z1.5.02 · Counting automorphic eigensystems

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

With the cohomological contribution and injection assumptions, the size of any family of inequivalent automorphic representations at the prescribed level and weight is polynomially bounded. Its constants depend on the CM field and rank before level and weight are chosen.

## Statement

Fix a CM field $K$ and $n\ge2$. Under the cohomological contribution and injection assumptions $\mathrm{cohC}$ and $\mathrm{borelC}$ in the supplied record, every finite pairwise nonisomorphic family of the prescribed weight and principal level has size at most $c[M(H+1)]^A$, with $c,A$ fixed before $m,H,M$.

## Assumptions

- Fix the CM field and a rank at least two.
- The automorphic input record supplies cohC and borelC for these data.
