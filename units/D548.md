## Statement

For x>0, a host explicit-formula record and compatible functional give the zero character in the kept set, band membership and normalization at sgc0, at most 4o band characters, the TT character-share error at most 4o $x^{-1+\varepsilon}$|TT|, the fixed bound on nonzero kept characters, and C log x on the others.

$$
\operatorname{CharsA}\bigl(\ell,A_0,K_{\rm kept},TT,(3o+1)^{-1},4o,K_o,C,\varepsilon,x\bigr).
$$
