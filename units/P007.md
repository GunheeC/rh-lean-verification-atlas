## Statement

For every $N\ge1$ and every primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$, if $L(s,\chi)=0$ with $0<\Re s<1$, then $\Re s=1/2$.

## Assumptions

- The Dirichlet character is primitive and has positive modulus.
- Its L-function vanishes at a point of the open critical strip.
