# D154 · Hyperplane sections of the Chow form

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A hyperplane cuts the original cycle into components with multiplicities. Specializing one block of the Chow form records exactly those components as factors. The scalar ambiguity is harmless for this factorization, but the multiplicities and the requirement that the hyperplane avoid the original prime are essential.

## Statement

Slicing the first block of the Chow form by a hyperplane gives, up to a nonzero scalar, the product of the component Chow forms raised to the intersection multiplicities.

$$
\exists c\in k^\times,\qquad f(u,-)=c\prod_{Q\in\operatorname{supp}(P\cap H_u)}g_Q^{m_Q}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Over a characteristic-zero field use a homogeneous prime ideal with the required dimension and a hyperplane whose form lies outside it.
- Choose a Chow form for the original cycle and a Chow form for each component of its section.
