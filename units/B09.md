# B09 · Finite character conversion of a gap bound

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A bound on the gap at every translate of a finite subgroup gives an upper bound on a weighted sum over the kept characters. The stated width, density and imbalance bounds control the conversion. Discarded characters still contribute an explicit error, which the next estimate must bound.

## Statement

Fix $C\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$, $|c|\le1$ and $C_E,K_o,M\in\mathbb R$. There is $x_1$ such that the implication below holds for every $x\ge x_1$. Take a finite abelian group $H$, its prescribed arithmetic host facts at the finite excluded set $S$, character constants $\ell(\xi)$, and $T_2\subset A_0\le H$, $TT\subset H$. Write $n=|H|$, $n_2=|T_2|$, $n_T=|TT|$, $\delta_2=n_2/n$, and $f=|\Gamma_\Phi|$. For $b,k>0$, $a_E\ge0$ and integers $m,N\ge0$ assume $m\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon/2}$, $xm\le k$, and the following conditions.

- The width parameters satisfy $\eta,\theta\ge0$, $b',X'>0$, $b'X'\le xb$ and $|a_t/b-x|\le1$ on $TT$. Each positive column weight $w_{u,j}(\rho)$ ($u\in A_0$, $\rho\in\Gamma_\Phi$) is in $[b,b+\eta+1]$ on the thick places indexed by $T_2+u$ and at most $2b'$ elsewhere. The cutoff obeys $a_t/w_{u,j}(\rho)\le N$.

- Every frequency satisfies $|G_\xi(y)|\le y\theta$ for $y\ge X'/3$; $2f(x+1)\theta\le1/x$, $6f(x+1)(\eta+1)\le b/x$. Also $a_E\le2xb$, $n_T\ge nx^{-\varepsilon/8}/2$, $\delta_2\ge x^{-\varepsilon}$, $m\ge x^{1/2-\varepsilon}$, and $k\le n^2x^{1/2-\varepsilon}$.

- Write $A_0^\perp$ for the frequencies trivial on the embedded $A_0$. The kept set $K'$ contains the trivial character, lies in $A_0^\perp$, and its characters take value one at the distinguished element $s_0$; $|A_0^\perp|\le M$. For kept characters nontrivial on the embedded $H$, $|\sum_{t\in TT}\xi(t)-cn_T|\le M x^{-1+\varepsilon}n_T$. The bounds on $|\ell(\xi)|$ are $K_o$ for kept nontrivial characters and $C_E\log x$ for discarded members of $A_0^\perp$.

Set

$$
\mathrm{Br}=n_Ta_E(1+m^2/k)+ m\left(2nmbx^\varepsilon+ \frac{n_2kbx^\varepsilon}{n^2x^{-\varepsilon}}\right).
$$

If for every $u\in A_0$ the gap (the sum of $\mathrm{ASK}-\Re\mathrm{MAIN}$ over $TT$ and the $m$ columns) is at most $x^{C\varepsilon}\mathrm{Br}$, then

$$
\sum_{\xi\in K'}\mathrm{wt}_c(\xi)\Re G_\xi(x) \le x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}+\sum_{\xi\in A_0^\perp\setminus K'}|G_\xi(x)|,
$$

where $\mathrm{wt}_c$ is $1$ on characters trivial on the embedded $H$ and $c$ otherwise.

## Assumptions

- Fix $C\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$, $|c|\le1$ and the recorded character constants. Use the finite host, $T_2\subseteq A_0$, positive $b,k$ and nonnegative $a_E$.
- Impose the width, cutoff, frequency, cost and remaining-size conditions, including the upper rank and $xm\le k$ inequalities.
- The kept characters satisfy normalization and share bounds. For every subgroup translate the gap is at most $x^{C\varepsilon}$ times the same size bracket.
