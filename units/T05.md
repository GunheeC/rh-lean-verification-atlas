# T05 · Two uniform bounds on indefinite rows

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Two estimates control the indefinite rows. One bounds the false-side row at sufficiently large parameters on families satisfying a division condition. The other bounds the true-side weight at every parameter once the point is sufficiently far out. The Fourier fit uses the second estimate. The final definite-side assembly uses both.

## Statement

For every $P$, both of the following assertions hold on the admissible families. First, for a family with `DIVinN`, some $C_0\ge0$ works eventually at points $p$ satisfying `More`: for $C\ge C_0$ the false-side row satisfies `ON`. This gives the upper bound on the sum of the two conjugate-place indices for every twisted place, nonnegative row errors there, and the summed weighted-error bound $E_{1,j}$ for each $j$. Second, eventually at points and for all $C$, the true-side row satisfies $N_{L6,j}\le\log\lambda_j(\mu_0,p,v)$ for every $j,p,v$ in the row datum.

## Assumptions

- Fix the admissible family and row data. The first assertion requires its division input and points satisfying the stated More condition.
- The two estimates use their respective false-side and true-side row data, with the thresholds and parameter $C$ in their stated order.
