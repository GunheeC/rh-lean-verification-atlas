## Statement

Assume $F$, the automorphic join, the Hecke–Tate functional equation, both theta comparisons of T11, separation as in T09, and the lower-weight assertion of T05. Then some $C_{F,0}$ works for every $C_F\ge C_{F,0}$. In each admissible family with `DIVinN`, eventually every point having the prescribed field grid of size $C_F$ satisfies, for every $C$, the false-side functional bound $\mu_{\max,RR}\le h_h-\operatorname{meanLog}(\kappa)$ for its numerical data $N(C)$.

## Assumptions

- Take the local theta data and automorphic join, the Hecke–Tate functional equation and the two theta comparisons.
- Assume separating functionals and the lower-weight assertion. The family has the division input and points on the specified field grid.
