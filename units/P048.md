# P048 · One twisted period with simultaneous local bounds

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a fixed admissible column setup with integral rows, one coefficient-field element represents the chosen twisted period and satisfies all the prescribed local lower bounds outside the exceptional primes. Simultaneity allows those bounds to be combined into a common denominator estimate.

## Statement

Take a base and pre-chart, an admissible column record $I$ with its pinned frames and coefficients, its stage laws $l$, later chart data and the level-zero lattice identification. Assume that the reference and twisted rows on this choice are integral. Choose the coefficient and tuple data with $t_p.\xi=I.\xi$, and any divisor datum $Y$. Under the stabilizer-denominator identity, for every word $u$, used nonreference place tuple $\mu$, integral point $x$ of the twisted point lattice and $F$ in the indicated ambient carrier and $V_0(\mu)$, there exists $y\in L_L$ such that its chosen complex image is the twisted period of the embedded $x$ and the identified $F$, and for every $w$ with $p_w\notin Q_Y(\mu)$,

$$
y=0\quad\text{or}\quad v_w(y)\ \ge\ -e_w v_{p_w}(D_{\Gamma,u}).
$$

## Assumptions

- Use the base and pre-chart, pinned frame and coefficients, stage laws, later data and level-zero lattice identification.
- The reference and twisted rows are integral, the coefficient and tuple characters agree, and the stabilizer-denominator identity holds.
- Fix the indicated divisor, used nonreference tuple, integral point and permitted form.
