# Z1.5.03 · Frobenius classifiers over a CM field

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At a good prime of a fixed CM field, a finite list classifies allowed Frobenius data of fixed purity for every eligible odd coefficient prime. Its size bound is polynomial in the level and weight parameters, giving one count across coefficient systems.

## Statement

From the automorphic hypotheses and LevelKNAlt, for each CM field $K$ and rank $n$ at least two, constants $c,A$ work for every strictly decreasing weight $|m_i|\le H$ with $m_i+m_{\mathrm{rev}(i)}=1-n$, every $M\ge1$ and prime $w$ above $p$ with $p$ dividing neither $M$ nor the discriminant of $K$. One classifier for AdmKNAlt has size at most $c[M(H+1)]^A$ for every odd coefficient prime different from $p$.

## Assumptions

- Assume the automorphic input record and LevelKNAlt.
- Use the prescribed pure weight and a prime away from the level and CM discriminant.
