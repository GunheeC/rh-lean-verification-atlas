## Statement

For each ordinary normal form and its unramified modular source, the integral class theorem, the Hodge–Tate law and admissible projection yield one tuple (coefficient ring, $y$, exceptional primes, twist, test characters, layers) satisfying Sys, IwSys2, SpLink and Pin7 simultaneously.

## Assumptions

- Use an ordinary normal form and its unramified modular source.
- Assume the integral class theorem, the Hodge–Tate law and Hecke projection for this source.
