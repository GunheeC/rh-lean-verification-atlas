## Statement

For each axial host on a frame the induced arithmetic data satisfy the host prime conditions with exceptional set consisting of its distinguished q and its given small primes.

$$
\operatorname{HostFactsS}\bigl(\operatorname{arith}(H),\{q_H\}\cup S_{\rm small}(H)\bigr).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix a frame and an axial host on that frame.
- Use its induced arithmetic record and the union of its distinguished prime with its recorded small-prime set.
