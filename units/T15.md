## Statement

Assume the self-intersection bound and injectivity on small sections. Take (i) the toroidal KS, seminegative Lie, Green cuspidal, point separation and Fubini–Study Chow inputs, (ii) the Hodge-bundle record, (iii) integral Siegel toroidal models, charts for fans, toroidal charts and arithmetic degree, (iv) Grothendieck deformation, (v) the eight statements on Kawamata–Viehweg, log-canonical $L^2$ extension, holomorphic sections, nef numerics, Chow forms, their integrality and hyperplane sections, and proper-intersection length, and (vi) the deep estimate T14. Then T13's uniform inequality for all the covolumes holds.

## Assumptions

- Assume the self-intersection estimate, injectivity on small sections and the toroidal, Lie, Green, point-separation, Chow, Hodge-bundle and arithmetic-degree inputs.
- Use Grothendieck deformation, the listed extension, nefness, proper-intersection and section inputs, and the deep component bound T14.
