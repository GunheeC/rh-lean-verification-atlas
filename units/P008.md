## Statement

For every $s\in\mathbb C$, if $\zeta(s)=0$, $s\ne1$, and $s\ne-2(n+1)$ for every $n\in\{0,1,\ldots\}$, then $\Re s=1/2$.

## Assumptions

- Take a complex zero of $\zeta$ distinct from $1$ and from every negative even integer.
