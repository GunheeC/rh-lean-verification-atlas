## Statement

At a valued integral-model point with the stated level, splitting and regularity data, a generating section gives a unit representing its theta scalar. For a general section with the required generator comparison and $8\mid N$, the normalized value $a\kappa^{N/8}$ is a unit. $\mathrm{KeyUnitOnModels}$.

## Assumptions

- Use the stipulated base ring and prime-level structure on the model, including conditions N3, N4 and split fibres.
