## Statement

Assume $P$ and row rationality. For any $h,c,A$ as in T03 with $A$ eventually large, three conclusions hold. For every unramified CM datum and $M\ge3$ the ratio of standard lattice masses in ranks $M$ and $M-1$ equals the prescribed `massRatioRightSide`. At $p_A(c)$ one has the base, first stage, export and second stage of automorphic membership. Finally, there is a large-base slot $R$ such that eventually every constructed point of the good slot intersected with $R$ admits, at every real scale, a bundle with the specified linkage.

## Assumptions

- Assume the third-place unitary hypotheses and rationality of the eligible rows.
- Fix a proof of the existence proposition RecTExists and a real dependence parameter, and take an eventually large retwisting predicate. The lattice-mass clause takes an unramified CM datum of rank at least three.
