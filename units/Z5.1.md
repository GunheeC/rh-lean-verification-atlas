# Z5.1 · Gram determinant and additive index

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At full rank over a totally complex field, archimedean Gram determinants compute the height of a free basis lattice. Enlarging that lattice divides its height by the additive index, giving the normalization needed to compare covolumes of full lattices.

## Statement

For a full finitely generated lattice in an invertible frame over a totally complex field of degree $n$, the real realization has rank $nD$. For the free lattice $B$ of a full basis, its height is the product of its complex Gram determinants; for full $L\supseteq B$, $\mathrm{ht}(L)[L:B]=\mathrm{ht}(B)$.

## Assumptions

- Use a finitely generated full lattice over a totally complex number field.
- The archimedean frame is invertible.
