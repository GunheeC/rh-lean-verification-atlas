## Statement

Integrating the cusp weight on the chosen fundamental set yields the stated bound involving its volume, the root discriminant and the CM-type and rank parameters.

$$
0< V=\int_{\mathcal F}d\mu,\qquad \int_{\mathcal F}w_{\rm cusp}\,d\mu\le|\Psi|j'\bigl(6+\log(2|\Psi|\,\mathrm{rd}(E))+\log(2|\Psi|j'\,\mathrm{rd}(E))\bigr)V.
$$

## Assumptions

- Use a CM host with the arithmetic action of the lattice group and its prescribed fundamental set.
- The indices satisfy $1\le j'$ and $3j'+1\le j+j'$.
