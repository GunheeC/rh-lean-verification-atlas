## Statement

Assume $P$ and row rationality: every datum in the starting row test with the prescribed group, geometric, weight and residue-characteristic conditions passes the rational row test. Fix a proof $h_L$ of the placewise local-map existence proposition `RecTExists`, $c\in\mathbb R$, and an eventually large retwisting predicate $A$. Suppose the small-prime norm bound is linked at $p(h_L,c)$, and the functional inequality of T10 and covolume inequality of T13 are linked in their row versions at $p_A(h_L,c)$. Then the definite-side family, selection and certificates of T02 exist. The two types of parameter have distinct roles in this implication.

## Assumptions

- Assume the third-place hypotheses and rationality of the tested rows.
- Fix a proof hL of RecTExists, a real dependence parameter and an eventually large retwisting predicate. RecTExists asserts that a local map satisfying IsRecT exists at each place.
- The small-prime bound is linked at the dependence parameter. The functional and covolume bounds are linked at the extension parameter.
