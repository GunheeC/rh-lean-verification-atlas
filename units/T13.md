## Statement

Assume resolution, principalisation, integrated Poincaré–Lelong, the three Hilbert–Samuel statements, the arithmetic bigness bound, Hodge–Tate orthogonality, the Zarhin splitting and nef Riemann–Roch (T17–T24). Assume also integral Siegel toroidal models, the toroidal-fan input for KS, seminegativity of the semiabelian Lie bundle, separation of pairs of points, arithmetic degree, the 28 Hodge-bundle inputs, and Grothendieck deformation. There exists $\varepsilon_1>0$ such that for each $0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_1$ there is a largeness parameter $K$. For every family $F$ at $\varepsilon$ and real $H$ with $\mathrm{FamHyp}(F)$ and $F.\mathrm{Large}(K,H)$, there are $C_0\ge0$ and $x_0$ such that, for $x>x_1$ with $x\ge x_0$, every family index and point $p$ with the point conditions and reciprocity tie (`RowsTAlt`), every $C\ge C_0$ and every $j$, the false-side row satisfies

$$
\operatorname{lcv}(XW_j,M_j)/D\ \le\ N(C)_{L3,j}.
$$

The normalization $D$, row space and lattice in this expression belong to $p$.

## Assumptions

- Use T17–T24: arithmetic bigness, Hilbert–Samuel, resolution, principalisation, Poincaré–Lelong, splitting, nef Riemann–Roch and Hodge–Tate orthogonality.
- Assume integral toroidal models and fan/KS, Lie seminegativity, point separation, arithmetic degree, the published Hodge-bundle package and Grothendieck deformation.
- Use a family with its required hypotheses and largeness conditions at the selected exponent and scale.
