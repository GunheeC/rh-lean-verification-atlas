# P026 · An integral ordinary zeta cocycle

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For an integral ordinary representation attached to the specified newform, there is one integral cocycle with local triviality on inertia away from the prime, a nontrivial second coordinate at the chosen place, and the tame norm relations. The same cocycle must satisfy all three demands.

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be odd, $e\ge1$, $A$ a place above $\ell$, and $W$ a continuous integral two-dimensional representation of $G_{\mathbb Q}$. Assume the specified normal form: $W$ is unramified outside $S$ containing the primes over $\ell$, its determinant $\delta$ has square one and takes value $-1$ on complex conjugations, and its restriction to $\ker\chi\cap\ker\delta$ is irreducible. At $A$, $W$ is upper triangular, a positive power of its first diagonal character on inertia equals the same power of $\chi^e$, and that character differs from $\chi$ somewhere on the decomposition group. Let $g$ be a newform of weight $2e+1$, of level prime to $\ell$, with unit $a_\ell(g)$. Suppose there is a character $\eta$ trivial on the subgroup fixing $\mu_\ell$ pointwise such that $g$ is attached (via $\iota$) to $W\otimes\chi^{-e}\delta^{-1}\eta$, and $W(s)_{22}\chi(s)^e\delta(s)^{-1}\eta(s)=1$ on inertia at $A$. There exist a common coefficient ring $O$ for $W$, an $O$-integral continuous cocycle $y$, and a finite set $S'$ with the following properties. On inertia away from $\ell$, $y$ is locally a coboundary. There is no scalar $x$ with $y(s)_2=(W(s)_{22}-1)x$ throughout the decomposition group of $A$. For every $n\ge1,\ w\notin S',\ \ell^n\mid Nw-1$, and every admitted surjective character to $\mathbb Z/\ell^n$ trivial on the subgroup fixing $\mu_{Nw}$ pointwise, the same $y$ satisfies the tame norm relation.

## Assumptions

- Take odd $\ell$, $e\ge1$ and an integral rank-two representation in the specified ordinary normal form at a place over $\ell$.
- Use its ramification, determinant, kernel irreducibility and two local character conditions.
- A weight-$2e+1$ newform of level prime to $\ell$ has unit $a_\ell$. It realizes the indicated twist by $\eta$, with $\eta$ trivial on the subgroup fixing $\mu_\ell$ and with the second diagonal character satisfying the specified inertia identity.
