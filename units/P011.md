## Statement

Assume $\mathcal R$. Then $H$ holds for every character of every positive modulus, with its exception at the principal pole.

## Assumptions

- Assume the full polarized Galois row $\mathcal R$.
- For the half-plane assertion use positive modulus, $\Re s>1/2$, and exclude the principal-character pole.
