## Statement

For each allowed epsilon and C0 at least 1, fix uniform host constants and a lower threshold. An admitted initial host of an admissible rank tuple, with the prescribed conductor and lcm divisibility, good prime pair in its window and field rows up to $\lfloor Y^{\varepsilon/5}\rfloor$, extends to a host at that scale and an auxiliary prime. It has the chosen constants, the four-parameter host bound, and character shares with tolerance $x^{-1+\varepsilon}$.

$$
\exists H,q_0,\quad H^{\rm ax}=H_0,\quad C_{T,2}(H)=C_{T,2},\quad\mathrm{HostGood}(s,C_8,4,c_6,C_b,H,q_0),\quad\mathrm{Shares}_{R}(TT_H,x^{-1+\varepsilon}).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix $0<\varepsilon<1/52$ and $C_0\ge1$. Use an eligible large scale, valid ranks and an admitted initial host.
- Use its size and conductor bounds, the positive lcm divisor, good prime pair in the window and all field rows through $\lfloor Y^{\varepsilon/5}\rfloor$.
