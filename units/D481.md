## Statement

Suppose a family class has a tail of division-kernel data and a closed uniform bound for those kernels whenever the tuple and discriminant-size estimates hold. Under these size estimates the covered-twist and eta-sum bounds imply the division height, frame and slope bounds on a common tail.

$$
\mathrm{TuSize}(F)\wedge\mathrm{DGamSize}(F)\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{LinkDIVt}(F,C_{\rm abs},C_H).
$$

## Assumptions

- Assign division-kernel data on a tail of each family, with the stated closed uniform bound.
- Impose the tuple and discriminant size conditions. For the expanded division-height clause use the covered-twist and eta-sum bounds.
