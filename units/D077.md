## Statement

At split primes satisfying the slope test, every sufficiently large weight of the correct parity gives the specified Eisenstein lift with its local conditions and unlinked adjoint.

$$
\mathrm{HypothesesH}'\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{PropEGW}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume the automorphic input record $H'$.
- Take a split prime satisfying the slope test and a sufficiently large weight of the specified parity.
