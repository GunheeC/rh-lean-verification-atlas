# Z1.1.04 · Rank-three seeds at prescribed weights

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The three preceding inputs produce an open-irreducible rank-three seed at an arbitrarily high allowed weight in a specified congruence class. Its symmetric weights and residual characters give the prescribed starting data rather than just a representation of the right dimension.

## Statement

From the preceding three inputs, for every prescribed lower bound $B$ and admissible field pair with distinct characters, obtain $m\ge B$ in the designated residue class modulo $\ell-1$ and an open-irreducible rank-three seed of weights $(m,0,-m)$, prescribed residual characters and cyclotomic inverse twist.

## Assumptions

- Use a linkable field pair with distinct characters and a lower bound for the weight.
- The three seed propagation inputs hold at the indicated ranks and weights.
