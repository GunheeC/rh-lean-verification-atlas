## Statement

At a normalized point assume the characteristic coefficients belong to the Hecke algebra, the residual matrix span is full, and the idempotent and distinct-eigenvalue corner hypotheses hold. There are normalized ring data, lifting the point, whose congruence valuation equals the quotient length; their block modules and finite display satisfy the specified relations.

## Assumptions

- Take a normalized point with characteristic coefficients in the Hecke algebra and full residual matrix span.
- Assume the stated idempotent and distinct-eigenvalue corner conditions.
