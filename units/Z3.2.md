## Statement

Under G1–G6, the tame-generator, Bloch–Kato and annihilator, continuity, semisimple decomposition, Fontaine, integral Tate finiteness, local compatibility, 5.1 and unramified conditions, the normalized row, lifting-exponent and TB hypotheses yield the global lattice-index inequality with the specified row lengths, lifting exponents and TB constants.

## Assumptions

- Use G1–G6, tame generators, Bloch–Kato and annihilator conditions, family continuity and semisimple decomposition.
- Use Fontaine and Tate local conditions, coefficient compatibility, the unramified hypothesis and the designated row, exponent and TB data.
