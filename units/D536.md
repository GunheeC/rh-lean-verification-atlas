## Statement

For a primitive character, combine the joint window and odd-minus bound at the stipulated cutoff to get an interval from Y to Y squared inside the admitted scale range, with the Selmer bound at each point for every prescribed page function.

$$
C_t\ge1\ \wedge\ \mathrm{JointWin}(C_t)\ \wedge\ \mathrm{ThmBc}(C_t)\ \Longrightarrow\ \forall\psi,\ \mathrm{VWinAt}(\psi,C_t).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix $C_t\ge1$ and a primitive character.
- Assume the joint window and odd-minus bound with this same cutoff $C_t$.
