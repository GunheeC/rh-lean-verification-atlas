## Statement

On a good root tower with positive ranks the full intersection system satisfies the I8 identity for the flag-height intersection expression.

$$
\mathrm{I8}_T\bigl(II,h_{\rm flag}(\mathrm{CMType}(H),p+q,II)\bigr).
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume the comparison, Given and Weil records.
- Fix a good origin on the root tower with positive ranks, the required extra data and a full intersection system.
