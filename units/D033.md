## Statement

The convergent unitary Siegel–Weil input supplies a common nonnegative row-error constant and two largeness thresholds for each set of admissible parameters. Every tied, standard two-word datum satisfying Hyp0 and absolute convergence has the required bound for the normalized sum of real inner products of pairs of nonzero nongeneric terms in the same row with $s_0=1$, at that error scale.

$$
\mathrm{SiegelWeilUnitaryConvergent}\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{RowNGP}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume the convergent unitary Siegel–Weil input.
- For the row estimate use an admissible tied standard two-word datum satisfying Hyp0 and absolute convergence.
