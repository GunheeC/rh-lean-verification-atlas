## Statement

Fix a prime $p$, an algebraically closed characteristic-zero valued field $C$ with $v(p)<1$, a valuation ring $O$ which is a $\mathbb Z_p$-algebra complete for $(p)$, and a $p$-divisible group $G/O$. Choose $\zeta_0=1$, $\zeta_{n+1}^p=\zeta_n$, $\zeta_1\ne1$. Let $B$ pair the two $O$-extended Tate modules of $G,G^\vee$ bilinearly. On pure Tate elements require the Weil pairing to be $a\mu_{\mathrm{Tate}}(\zeta)$ and $B(1\otimes x,1\otimes\chi)=a$ in $O$ for some $a\in\mathbb Z_p$, for every $x,\chi$. Then $\mathrm{HT}_G(x)=0$ if and only if $B(x,y)=0$ for all $y\in\ker(\mathrm{dlog}_G)$ in the dual-side module.
