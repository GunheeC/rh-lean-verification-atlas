# Z1.1.01 · Ordinary Eisenstein seeds

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

## Statement

For a marked complex conjugation, nontrivial splitting character and admissible slope-test data, every sufficiently large weight $k$ of the specified parity admits an Eisenstein rank-two seed with PR and unlinked trace-free adjoint.

## Assumptions

- Fix a marked complex conjugation and a nontrivial splitting character.
- The local slope-test inputs are admissible.
