## Statement

Assume Poitou–Tate duality, local finiteness and local Tate duality. Fix a $C_K$ satisfying W05's cohomological bound and constants as in W12 giving its simultaneous bound at $C_t=401$. Assume W14's minus-part bound, the collective estimate of W08, both individual estimates of W09, the restricted Case V estimate of W10 and the individual estimate of W09's shape for Case Z (odd $\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\mid2e$, $\psi=1$). Then the Selmer window $V$ of [P013](P013.md) holds.

## Assumptions

- Assume Poitou–Tate, local finiteness and local Tate duality. Take a uniform W05 constant and W12 constants at the same cutoff 401.
- Use the minus-part estimate, the collective Case I, individual Cases II and III, restricted Case V, and the indicated exceptional trivial-character Case Z bound.
