## Statement

The normalization furnishes the flat model and universal isogeny of type Tj with the specified local rings and identifications.

$$
\mathrm{EsNorm}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix the datum at a prime $p$ over a characteristic-zero discrete valuation ring with $p$ in its maximal ideal. Use a model of level $N\ge3$ coprime to $p$.
- Take a maximal ideal containing the ideal at $r$, $j\le n$, and $c=p^e$ with $e>0$ if $j>0$. The multiplicity parameter is one.
