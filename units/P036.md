## Statement

Take a layer $F/L$ with $L$ totally complex, $E\ge1$, $a=\kappa^E$, and $b,B\in\mathbb N$. Suppose every finite family of elements of $F^\times$ satisfying the divisor difference condition, with pairwise distinct classes of $N_a(\alpha)^{\ell^b}$ modulo $q$-th powers, has size at most $B$. Let $\mathcal H_q$ consist of the functions on the prime ideals of $F$ with values in $\mathbb Z/q$ that kill principal divisors and satisfy $\phi(\gamma^{-1}P)=a\phi(P)$. Writing $v=v_\ell(E)$ and $g=2[L:\mathbb Q]+\lfloor\log_2 h_L\rfloor+2$, we have

$$
\mathcal H_q\text{ finite},\qquad |\mathcal H_q|q^{r_2(L)} \le B\,\ell^{(b+v)g+c+v}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Take a cyclotomic layer $F/L$ over totally complex $L$, a positive exponent $E$ and nonnegative $b,B$.
- The Few bound $B$ applies to finite families satisfying the divisor-difference condition and pairwise distinct prescribed norm classes.
