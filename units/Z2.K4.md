# Z2.K4 · Odd stable homology and torsion

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Successive block operations bound the rank of the surviving odd homology by the number of complex places in the stated range. Separately, torsion orders stabilize above the given rank threshold. These estimates control the free and torsion contributions needed for finite counting.

## Statement

For totally complex $L$, even $k$, $k+1<r_2(L)$, $\ell>0$ and $n\ge2(k+1)+4$, the span of the $z$-sequence in degree $k+1$ has integral rank at most $r_2(L)$. For $n'\ge n\ge2m+4$, torsion in degree $m$ of $\mathrm{GL}_{n'}(\mathcal O_L)$ is finite and has the same order as at rank $n$.

## Assumptions

- The field is totally complex. For the odd-rank statement use even $k$, $k+1<r_2(L)$, positive block multiplicity and $n\ge2(k+1)+4$.
- For stable torsion use $n'\ge n\ge2m+4$.
