# P027 · Length measured by two-sided orbits

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Under the graded-chart, fibre and tangent-space hypotheses, finitely many two-sided points meet every two-sided orbit. Twice the sum of valuations of a separating function on any such cover bounds the indicated cotangent quotient length. This converts a local algebra length into a finite orbit calculation.

## Statement

Let $O$ be a complete characteristic-zero discrete valuation ring and $K$ an algebraically closed extension of its fraction field with valuation $v$ extending the normalized order. Let $A=\bigoplus_j A_j$ be graded, with marked $O$-point $x$ zero on nonzero weights. Its degree-zero algebra is Noetherian, complete local, with local structure map surjective on residue fields. It generates $A$ together with finitely many elements of weights $-2,-1,1,2$. Let $I$ be generated by lists of weights $(-1,-2,1,2,0)$ and sizes $(r+1,s,r',s',m)$; denote the first list by $F$, and assume the weight-zero equations vanish at $x$. Put

$$
P=\{b\in A:\,tb\in(\ker x)^2\text{ for some }0\ne t\in O\}, \qquad L_j=\operatorname{im}(A_j\cap\ker x\longrightarrow A/P).
$$

Assume $r\ge1$ and the smooth graded chart $M_1$: its variable counts in the five weights are $(r,s,r'+1,s'+1,m)$; individual linear coordinates can be isolated, and vanishing of the linear part on $\ker x$ implies membership in $P$. The negative and weight-zero equation lists span the corresponding cotangent lattices over the fraction field. Assume the image of $A_0$ in $A/I$ is finite over $O$. In what follows take $K$-points of $A/I$. For each point vanishing on both signs other than $x_K$, at least one strictly one-sided sign is absent in its fibre over $A_0$. At every two-sided point (nonzero on some element of each sign), point derivations killing $I$ have dimension at most one. Let $a\in A_0$ vanish at $x$ and be nonzero at all two-sided points. There exists a finite set of two-sided points meeting every such $K^\times$-orbit. For every such cover $T$,

$$
L=\operatorname{length}_O(L_{-1}/\langle\overline F_i\rangle) <\infty,\qquad L\le 2\sum_{\xi\in T}v(\xi(a)).
$$

## Assumptions

- Use a complete characteristic-zero DVR, an algebraically closed valued extension, and the marked graded algebra with the stated finite generators and complete local degree-zero algebra.
- Assume $r\ge1$, the stated equation counts and smooth marked chart, the negative and zero-weight cotangent spanning conditions and finiteness of the degree-zero image.
- The one-sided fibres, two-sided tangent dimensions, chosen function vanishing at the mark, and the finite orbit cover satisfy the conditions in the statement.
