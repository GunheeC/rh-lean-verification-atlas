## Statement

For each degree r of a period algebra at a prime l, every element of Fil0(r) is congruent modulo Fil0(r+1) to a coefficient from W times dxi0(r). Such a multiple is in Fil0(r+1) exactly when its coefficient lies in the ideal generated by xi. For each element z of Fil0(r) some l-power times z equals the image in Acr0 of xi^r times an element of W.

$$
\mathrm{Fil}_r/\mathrm{Fil}_{r+1}= (W/(\xi))\,\overline{d\xi_0(r)},\qquad z\in\mathrm{Fil}_r\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists N\ge0,\ w\in W,\ \ell^N z=\iota(\xi^rw).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix a prime $\ell$, a period algebra at $\ell$ and a nonnegative filtration degree $r$.
- The first and third clauses concern elements of the $r$-th filtered piece; the second takes any coefficient in $W$.
