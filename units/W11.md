# W11 · Bounded class-group ranks in cyclotomic layers

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For an abelian base containing the required roots of unity, one exponent bounds the order of every cyclotomic layer’s class group modulo its subgroup of ℓth powers, where ℓ is the chosen prime. The bound is independent of the layer and its model.

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be any prime and $K/\mathbb Q$ a finite abelian extension containing $\mu_\ell$ for odd $\ell$, or $\mu_4$ for $\ell=2$. There is an integer $r\ge0$ such that for every $n\ge0$ and every model $K'$ of $K(\mu_{\ell^n})$ over $K$,

$$
\big|\operatorname{Cl}(K')/\operatorname{Cl}(K')^\ell\big|\le\ell^r .
$$

## Assumptions

- Take $\ell$ prime and $K/\mathbb Q$ finite abelian.
- The base contains $\mu_\ell$ for odd $\ell$, or $\mu_4$ at two. Use a layer model over that same base.
