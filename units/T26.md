## Statement

Assume an arithmetic intersection system, the three Hilbert–Samuel consequences T18, the bigness bound T17, the resolution statement `ResKStatement`, extension of homomorphisms of semiabelian schemes, the Fargues Hodge–Tate input, GAGA for sections, quotients by finite flat subgroups, the Zarhin splitting T22 and nef Riemann–Roch T23. Then the uniform component-slope estimate in T14 holds with the same restrictions on $D,T,j,Y,e,o$ and the number of prime factors.

## Assumptions

- Assume the intersection system, arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel, bigness, resolution, and extension of semiabelian homomorphisms.
- Use Fargues Hodge–Tate, GAGA for sections, the finite flat quotient, Zarhin splitting and nef Riemann–Roch inputs.
- The component data satisfy the depth, index, goodness and prime-divisor restrictions of T14.
