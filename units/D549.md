## Statement

With x>0, explicit-formula data and compatible character functional, the omitted-character correction SigA on the host subgroup and kept set is at most 4o x^(1/2+epsilon).

$$
\Sigma_A(D,\ell,A_0,K_{\rm kept},x)\le4o\,x^{1/2+\varepsilon}.
$$

## Assumptions

- Take $x>0$ and an axial host at the stated level and test set.
- Assume its explicit-formula record and compatibility of the character functional.
