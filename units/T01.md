## Statement

Assume $P,F$, local matching, agreement of theta integrals, the closed covolume bound of T13, a proof $h_L$ of the local existence proposition `RecTExists`, and the primitive-character window with constant $401$. Then for every $N\ge1$, every complex Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$, and every $s$ with $\Re s>1/2$, one has $L(s,\chi)\ne0$ whenever $\chi\ne\chi_0$ or $s\ne1$. For each number field, algebraic closure and valuation above a finite place, this proposition asserts the existence of a map satisfying `IsRecT`. The two theta assumptions are the two conclusions stated in T11.

## Assumptions

- Assume the third-place unitary hypothesis $P$, local theta inputs $F$, matching and equality of theta integrals.
- Use the closed covolume bound, a proof of the placewise existence proposition RecTExists, and the primitive-character Selmer window with exponent 401.
