## Statement

For every algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, each prime $\ell\ge5$, each allowed coefficient ring $O$, and every even $e\ge2$ such that $\ell-1\mid2e$, the $O$-length of the localization kernel of the trivial-character coefficient module twisted by $\kappa^{-e}$, at modulus $\ell$, is zero. The proof term separates $\ell-1\mid e$ from $\ell-1\nmid e$.

## Assumptions

- Fix an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, a prime $\ell\ge5$ and an allowed coefficient ring.
- Take even $e\ge2$ satisfying $\ell-1\mid 2e$ and the trivial character.
