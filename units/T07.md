## Statement

Assume the two-layer linkage hypotheses, the third-layer published inputs, geometric Galois compatibility, the series input `SerN`, depth-zero support, local finiteness, local Tate duality and the local Euler characteristic. For each $h,c$, the linked norm bound at two and three implies the linked norm condition for $\mathrm{LinkN}$ at the same $p(c)$. On a family $F_m$, the latter conclusion says that some $C,x_0$ work for every $x\ge x_0$, index $i$ with finite odd Tate–Shafarevich part, and lift $j$: the local condition `LinkN_at` holds for its size datum at $j,C$. This unfolds the abbreviation `NLink.Final.mainTheorem`.

## Assumptions

- Assume the two-layer linking and third-layer published hypotheses, geometric Galois comparison, SerN and depth-zero support.
- Use local finiteness, Tate duality and the Euler characteristic; the linked norm bound at two and three must hold at the same dependence parameter.
