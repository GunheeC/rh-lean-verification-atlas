## Statement

Real Howe duality, local theta, the Hecke functional equation, toric and zeta denominator bounds, lattice norm transport, reflection, equality of theta integrals and local matching imply DenSum, ThbSt, OneSqSt, BotSmall, BotNe, FieldDat, TorEx, DenEx, BotEx, LNe, TriEx and Tower simultaneously.

## Assumptions

- Assume real Howe duality, the local theta input and the Hecke functional equation.
- Use the toric and zeta denominator bounds, norm transport and reflection.
- Require equality of the indicated theta integrals and matching of the local models.
