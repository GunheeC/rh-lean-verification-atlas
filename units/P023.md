# P023 · Power windows for a positive character family

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

If the weighted prime sum of a positive character family stays below a fixed multiple of a power on arbitrarily distant whole power intervals, the family is zero-free above that exponent. This supplies the family version of the window criterion used at a level.

## Statement

Let $F$ be a character family, $0<\theta<1$, $\delta>0$ and $C\in\mathbb R$. Assume that for every $Y_0$ there is $Y\ge Y_0$ such that

$$
\mathcal G_F(y)\le C\,y^{\theta}\qquad\text{for all }y\in[Y,Y^{1+\delta}].
$$

Then $F$ is zero-free above $\theta$.

## Assumptions

- Fix a character family, $0<\theta<1$, $\delta>0$ and a real constant $C$.
- For every starting threshold there is a larger $Y$ with $\mathcal G_F(y)\le Cy^\theta$ throughout $[Y,Y^{1+\delta}]$.
