## Statement

For $\mathrm{PTCase}(F,\ell)$ and any integral twist $e$ of the given local data, finite Poitou–Tate, local finiteness and local Tate duality give the length identity $V_{\rm len}+\nu_{\rm Gal}=\mathrm{shaLen}+\sum_w\mathrm{localH0TwistLength}_w$ for the coefficient module and localization conditions of VFormula.

## Assumptions

- Assume PTCase for the number field and prime and use the given twist and local data.
- Use finite Poitou–Tate duality, local finiteness and local Tate duality.
