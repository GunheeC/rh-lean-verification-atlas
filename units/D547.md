## Statement

For a level and host satisfying the explicit-formula record and a compatible additive-character functional, the group-model prime sum over the kept characters, with weight 1/(3o+1), equals the level prime sum at every real y.

$$
G_m^A\bigl(D,\ell,K_{\rm kept},(3o+1)^{-1},y\bigr)=G_m^\Lambda(y)\qquad(y\in\mathbb R).
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix a level, axial host, test set, scale and parameters.
- The host satisfies the explicit-formula record for those data and its additive-character functional is compatible.
