# D562 · Increment bounds for smoothed character sums

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Changing the argument of a smoothed character sum costs at most its displacement times an explicit logarithmic and character-dependent factor. The companion term has a uniform Lipschitz constant. These estimates make it possible to replace nearby arguments while accounting for the resulting error.

## Statement

There is one nonnegative Cd such that for every finite additive group, arithmetic data and character functional, every nontrivial character satisfies the Lipschitz bound $|G_\psi(y)-G_\psi(z)|\le |y-z|(\log(\max\{y,z\})+C_d+|\ell(\psi)|)$, and $|G_z(y)-G_z(z)|\le C_d|y-z|$, for y,z at least 1.

$$
|G_\psi(y)-G_\psi(z)|\le |y-z|\bigl(\log(\max\{y,z\})+C_d+|\ell(\psi)|\bigr),\qquad |G_z(y)-G_z(z)|\le C_d|y-z|.
$$

## Assumptions

- Fix any finite abelian group, arithmetic data and additive-character functional.
- Both arguments are at least one. The first estimate takes a nontrivial character, and the second concerns the zeta term.
