## Statement

Assume a uniform construction extending each admissible field row, with the order, conductor, lcm divisibility and good prime pair stipulated in the source, to a good host with the character-share bound. For primitive psi the exponent-401 Selmer window and admissibility at all large eligible frames then give 3/2-power host windows for the cyclic level with constant 4 and Selmer bound sqrt(b).

$$
\mathrm{HostExW}\bigl(\tfrac32,(s,R,H)\mapsto\mathrm{EAtH}(\mathrm{cyc}(\psi),H,TT_H,x,\varepsilon,4)\wedge\mathrm{VVle}(E_H,t_R,\sqrt b)\bigr).
$$

## Assumptions

- Assume a uniform extension of eligible field rows to good hosts satisfying the required order, conductor, prime pair and character-share conditions.
- For the primitive character assume its Selmer window with exponent 401 and the eventual admissibility of a compatible rank tuple on each eligible frame.
