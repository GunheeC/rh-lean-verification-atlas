# P049 · Gram determinant in the span of the lattice

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a lattice generated by independent vectors over a totally complex field, the product of their archimedean Gram determinants gives its height. Enlarging the lattice without changing its rank divides the height by the index. This lets covolumes be computed in the lattice's own span.

## Statement

Let $K$ be a totally complex number field with its given star structure, $V$ a finite-dimensional $K$-space with the compatible $\mathcal O_K$-action, $b$ a basis and $X_v$ invertible complex frames. For a $K$-independent family $e_1,\ldots,e_j$, set $B=\sum_i\mathcal O_K e_i$. Then

$$
\operatorname{ht}_{b,X}(B)=\prod_{v\mid\infty}\operatorname{Re}\det\operatorname{Gram}_{v,b,X}(e).
$$

For every finitely generated $\mathcal O_K$-module $L\supset B$ of $K$-rank $j$, one has $\operatorname{ht}_{b,X}(L)[L:B]=\operatorname{ht}_{b,X}(B)$.

## Assumptions

- Use a totally complex number field with its involution, a finite-dimensional space with compatible integral action, a basis and invertible archimedean frames.
- Take a linearly independent family. For the index formula the containing integral lattice is finitely generated of the same rank.
