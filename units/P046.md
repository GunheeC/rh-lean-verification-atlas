## Statement

Assume admission for the specified tied entries when the curve, series, character, local-law and comparison data at each stage are supplied, and existence of the indicated Lubin–Tate curve realization for each eligible inert prime. Fix a tied host, lattice system and kernel, comparison input $I$, comparison hypotheses $H$, labels and joint datum. At a place $w$ of inert residue characteristic $p\ge5$ outside the level primes, assume the host nonvanishing condition and $p\nmid T_u$. Suppose $\mu$ is used, the level-zero stabilizer condition holds, and $2r_G\le k$. On the chain $\operatorname{chainAt}(H,h_T)$ the semilinear comparisons satisfy the level-zero identification, kernel, rationality and terminal valuation conditions. The same chain admits a system at the specified place and levels, with its standard coordinates, uniformizer and ramification normalization, and $d_G(0)=0,\ d_G(j)\le v_p(D_{\Gamma,u})$.

## Assumptions

- Assume the entry-admission rule and curve realization at eligible inert primes, with the specified tied host, kernel, comparison, labels and joint.
- Use a nonvanishing inert place of residue characteristic $p\ge5$ outside the level with $p\nmid T_u$, a used tuple, trivial level-zero stabilizer as specified and $2r_G\le k$.
