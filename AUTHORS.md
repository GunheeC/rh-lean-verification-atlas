# Authors and contributions

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Gunhee Cho conceived the atlas, designed its evidence levels and reading tracks, maintains the repository and decides what is released. The Git author field identifies the maintainer.

Gunhee Cho is affiliated with the Department of Mathematics, Texas State University (`wvx17@txstate.edu`), and TranscendQ (`geonhee@transcendq.com`). TranscendQ aims to develop methods for mathematical verification and guarantees in hardware and quantum computing.

Cho posts project discussions and progress updates on the YouTube channel [EnjoyingMath](https://www.youtube.com/@enjoyingmath9346). The discussions draw on his graduate text for background in complex analysis. The views and any errors are the author's. [G. Cho, *Complex Analysis and Riemann Surfaces: A Graduate Path to Algebraic Geometry*, arXiv:2601.06868 (math.CV)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06868)

| AI cross-review | Claude and Codex appear in the project’s review and hand-off records. Edition-specific review files give their scope and verdict. Where an individual report gives no model version, the attribution is **version not recorded**. [Review index](reviews/README.md) · [Grade evidence](data/grade_evidence.json) |
| Named human checks | No human, the author included, has yet checked the arguments. The human-check register contains no submissions. The progress table and checklists derive their counts from the register. [Human check records and intake](signoffs/README.md) · [Progress](PROGRESS.md) |

A maintainer who submits a mathematical check uses the same edition, scope, attestation and verdict fields as every other reader. A contribution to editing or direction does not record a mathematical check.
