# Authors and contributions

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Gunhee Cho conceived the atlas, designed its evidence levels and reading tracks, maintains the repository and decides what is released. The Git author field identifies the maintainer.

| Contribution | Attribution and scope |
|---|---|
| Original mathematical development | Levent Alpöge released the Lean development and its mathematical argument. The atlas organizes exposition, source comparisons and checking infrastructure around the pinned edition. [Development and edition register](sources/README.md) |
| Drafting | Codex drafted atlas pages, data, scripts and reading maps. Claude drafted earlier verification notes. Blueprint editions v13, v13a and v13b and papers v3 through v7 are this project’s AI-written, unpublished drafts and have no human review. Model versions for the individual drafts are not recorded. [Change log](CHANGELOG.md) · [Origins of the texts](NOTICE.md) |
| AI cross-review | Claude and Codex appear in the project’s review and hand-off records. Edition-specific review files give their scope and verdict. Where an individual report gives no model version, the attribution is **version not recorded**. [Review index](reviews/README.md) · [Grade evidence](data/grade_evidence.json) |
| Named human checks | The human-check register currently contains no submissions. The progress table and checklists derive their counts from the register. [Human check records and intake](signoffs/README.md) · [Progress](PROGRESS.md) |

A maintainer who submits a mathematical check uses the same edition, scope, attestation and verdict fields as every other reader. A contribution to editing or direction does not record a mathematical check.
