\begin{formalstatement}[The value of the doubled Eisenstein series]\label{sub:T12}

For every good number-field base $(F,E,\sigma,B)$ and spaces $A,Y$ of
ranks $m,n\ge1$, take opposite Hermitian and skew-Hermitian signs, with the
signature $(r,s)$ of $A$ satisfying $\min(r,s)=0$. Fix a Hecke character
restricting as $\eta^m$, any finite-index Darboux doubling model satisfying
its splitting, rationality, invariance and theta-summation rules, a frame,
standard compact $K$ and height $a$. For every Haar measure with a
fundamental domain $D$, every Schwartz vector $\Phi$ with finite-dimensional
$K$-span, and every $x$, the flat-section Eisenstein sum for
$g\mapsto l(\omega(g)\Phi)$ has a meromorphic continuation from a right
half-plane, holomorphic at $s_0=(m-n)/2$, with value
\[
 E(x,s_0)=
 \begin{cases}1,&n<m,\\2,&m\le n\end{cases}
 \ \mathop{\mathrm{avg}}_{g\in D}\,
       \theta_F\bigl(\omega(x)S(g)\Phi\bigr).
\]
The average is taken with respect to the specified Haar measure.

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T12}
