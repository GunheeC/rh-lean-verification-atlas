## State the equality in its target field

Fix the period data, including the conditions on the chosen tilt element used by the comparison maps, and the local field with its indicated absolute Galois subgroup. Let $x$ in the crystalline ring be fixed by every element of that subgroup. The conclusion asks for a scalar $k$ belonging to the specified maximal unramified set whose image in the de Rham field equals the comparison image of $x$. It does not merely say that this image lies somewhere in the original field; for the latter kind of conclusion compare [D203](../../units/D203.md). `GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.bCris_fixed_mem_maxUnramified` ([FontaineLemmaInvariants.lean:420–426](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/GroundTheory/PAdicHodge/FontaineLemmaInvariants.lean#L420-L426)). `GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.bCrisPlus_fixed` ([BCrisPlusInvariants.lean:340–344](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/GroundTheory/PAdicHodge/BCrisPlusInvariants.lean#L340-L344)).

## Lift the invariant before applying the positive-ring theorem

The localized ring permits powers of the crystalline period in the denominator. Choose $m$ and a positive-ring element clearing such a denominator. The proof obtains a positive representative for an iterate of Frobenius, and uses its compatibility with the original equality and fixedness to produce a positive representative for $x$ which remains fixed under the whole subgroup. Inside this reduction the minimal polynomials of the recovered elements are compared using the iterated Frobenius relation, irreducibility and monicity. This is where controlling the unramified part enters. A choice of a common denominator alone gives neither the fixed representative nor that polynomial comparison. ## Apply the fixed-element theorem and transport the equality

For the fixed positive representative $y$, equivariance makes its image in the de Rham plus ring fixed. The positive-ring fixed-element theorem supplies a scalar in $K$, and the criterion for a scalar in the positive crystalline image supplies its membership in the unramified closure. Finally use the equality taking $y$ to $x$ and compatibility of the positive and localized comparison maps. The concluding equality is in the de Rham field.
