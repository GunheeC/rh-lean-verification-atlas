\begin{formalstatement}[Goal: The Dirichlet half-plane]\label{pn:P003}
For every $N\ge1$, Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$ and $s\in\mathbb C$, $\Re s>1/2$ and $(\chi\ne\chi_0\ \text{or}\ s\ne1)$ imply $L(s,\chi)\ne0$.
\end{formalstatement}
\nodestatus{P003}
Write $H$ for this entire assertion. The Euler product gives nonvanishing for $\sigma>1$ (NIST DLMF, Eq.~25.15.2). The half-plane $\sigma>1/2$ is the target.
