## Quantified hypotheses

**V.** Fix any primitive Dirichlet character $\psi$, any real $0<\varepsilon<1/52$, and any $e:\mathbb R\to\mathbb N$ satisfying $u^{2+8\varepsilon}\le 2^{e(u)}\le 2u^{2+8\varepsilon}$ for every $u\ge1$. For every $Y\le x\le Y^2$ and every number field $E$ of type $(\psi,e(x),q,d)$, put $t=\lfloor x^{401}\rfloor_+$ and $b=\exp(x^\varepsilon/2)$. For every algebraic closure $\Omega/E$ and every allowed system of local data at the rational primes, all the Selmer lengths $L_{\ell,a}$ are finite for $1\le a<t$, and for every finite set $S$ of primes,

$$
\sum_{\ell\in S}\log(\ell)\sum_{1\le a<t} L_{\ell,a}\le b .
$$

## Conclusions and proof outline

For any $s\in\mathbb C$, a zero of $\zeta(s)$ other than $1$ and the negative even integers has $\Re s=1/2$ in both public formulations. For every positive modulus and every character $\chi$ of that modulus, $\Re s>1/2$ and $(\chi\ne\chi_0\ \text{or}\ s\ne1)$ imply $L(s,\chi)\ne0$. Every zero of such a character in $0<\Re s<1$ has $\Re s=1/2$, also upon restriction to primitive characters. The five source declarations are in `Main.lean:4–12`. **1. Put both estimates on a single host.** Fix a primitive $\psi$ and let $\Lambda=\operatorname{cyc}(\psi)$. For any admissible $\varepsilon$, choose the dyadic exponent function $e$ in V and receive its $J,X_0$. Fix the field-frame constant $c\ge1$, set $a=2+8\varepsilon$ and $k=c^{1/a}$. For the requested lower endpoint $y$ and B1 threshold $Y_1$, choose $M\ge\max(1,y,Y_1,k^3,k^{400})$, then $X\ge\max(X_0,M^J)$. B1 selects $q,d$ at $X^{1/J}$. V returns $W\ge X^{1/J}\ge M$. Put $Z=kW$. This is the order in `winPairs`, `CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean:23–37`. At the B2 application the uniform scale threshold also forces $4\psi.N\le\lfloor Y^{\varepsilon/5}\rfloor$, $q\ne3$ and $e\ge e_0$ (`supplyOfAdmOf`, `ShortNorms/supplyOfAdmOf.lean:39–93`). For $Z\le x\le Z^{3/2}$ put $u=x/k$. Then $W\le u\le W^2$, $x^a\le c2^{e(u)}\le2x^a$, $x^{400}\le u^{401}$ and $\exp(u^\varepsilon/2)\le\exp(x^\varepsilon/2)$. The good prime pair persists on the restricted interval. B2–B8 give the field, ranks on that exact frame, admissibility and the good host with its selected character classes. The eventual rank theorem applied to this frame and its RankVal certificate (`supplyOfAdmOf.lean:38,129–135`) shows that its rank $t$ is at most $\lfloor x^{400}\rfloor_+$, so nonnegativity of each summand restricts V to $t$ on that same field. The request for this cyclic level has its two required components at each member scale, the explicit-formula bounds and the finite Selmer-sum bound. This gives $\mathrm{HostExW}(3/2,G_\psi)$ on arbitrarily far windows. See `CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean:5–81` and `CharacterBoxes/SupplyChi.lean:8–16`. **3. Use the tied family on its windows.** Fix a sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$. The request supplies $\mathrm{EFamC}(\Lambda,F_m)$ and arbitrarily far intervals $[Z,Z^{3/2}]$ in the family window. By P017 with list A and the pointwise P021 lattice bound used in B10, there is one eventual threshold, after which the whole interval satisfies

$$
\mathcal G_\Lambda(x)\le x^{1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}=x^{1/2+9\varepsilon}.

**4. Exclude a zero and descend to the public assertions.** Suppose $L(\rho,\psi)=0$ with $\Re\rho>1/2$ away from the principal pole. Choose $1/2<\theta<\min(1,\Re\rho)$, and then an admissible $\varepsilon$ with $9\varepsilon<\theta-1/2$. For windows far enough to exceed the threshold and 1 the sum is at most $x^\theta$. In the open strip a zero of a character in the level contributes its multiplicity to the Mellin residue with a positive level weight. The contributions of coincident zeros have the same scalar factor, hence their sum is nonzero, and at a real zero the residue is positive. This Mellin argument is a sketch. The implication used here is the level criterion `formB` (P018, `PrimitiveCosets/Imports.lean:8–19`), which excludes a level zero to the right of $\theta$ in the strip. The level criterion also uses classical nonvanishing for $\Re s\ge1$ away from the exception. As $\psi$ occurs in its cyclic level, either range contradicts the assumed zero. See v4 Proposition 6.32, p. 83. [P018](../../units/P018.md) · [P022](../../units/P022.md) · [P023](../../units/P023.md)

Inducing a primitive character adds finitely many Euler factors $1-\psi(p)p^{-s}$. Each is nonzero when $\Re s>0$, so change of level gives P003 with its stated principal exception. An open-strip zero left of the line descends to the primitive character and reflects across $s\mapsto1-s$ to a zero of the inverse primitive character right of the line. The gamma factors are finite and nonzero in this strip. The right half is already excluded, proving P004 and its restriction P005. At modulus one the strip assertion applies to $\zeta$. A zero at $\Re s\le0$ away from $0$ and the negative even integers would, by the completed zeta functional equation, give a zero at $1-s$, where $\Re(1-s)\ge1$. Also $\zeta(0)=-1/2$. This gives P002 and its equivalent Mathlib formulation P001. See `CharacterBoxes/Imprimitive.lean:5–35` and `CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean:59–102`.
