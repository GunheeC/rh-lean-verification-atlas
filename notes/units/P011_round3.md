## Fill the middle field

The middle field is supplied by [B04](../../units/B04.md). Start with the local data, its place and coefficient conventions. The construction chooses the local class-field input and a cuspidal parameter. A local extension theorem then produces an assignment on all irreducible representations, together with its restriction on the cuspidal ones. Dimension and twist identities are obtained for that same extension. The remaining fields assert the required compatibilities, including the central character, duals, injectivity, depth and the specified type condition. The type condition itself is discharged after restricting to the indicated cuspidal case. The choices and the final record, including its side conditions, are in `UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.recTExists` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/RecTExists.lean:20–66`).

Thus $\langle h,\mathrm{recTExists},h\rangle$ provides precisely the argument required by the first binder of P012. Local existence is a substantial input to this step. The mere repeated occurrence of $h$ supplies neither the extension of local parameters nor its compatibility fields.

## Produce the right kind of window

The second argument is [P013](../../units/P013.md), used as `UpperTheory.One.vwin_of_row` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/VwinOfRow.lean:4–5`). It constructs the open-image-condition record from $h$ and supplies the named base-change result together with $h$ to `UpperTheory.One.Join.automorphic_window_of_inputs` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/TopMainZj.lean:71–74`). That theorem supplies the CM abelian inputs to the window calculation. The output is a statement quantified over every primitive character $\psi$, with exponent $401$, as fixed by `UpperTheory.Top.VWin` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:49–49`).

To see why a single bound at one field or layer would not fill this argument, first fix the admissible $\varepsilon$ and a layer selector satisfying the page inequalities. The assertion chooses $J\ge1$ and a threshold. For every sufficiently large $X$ and every admissible pair at scale $X^{1/J}$ it must then return a lower endpoint $Y\ge X^{1/J}$ with $Y^2\le X$. On the entire interval $[Y,Y^2]$ the bound must hold at the preselected layers. This order of choices is `UpperTheory.Top.VWinAt` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:44–47`). At a point $x$ it applies to each field realizing the specified character and layer, by `UpperTheory.Top.VAt` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:41–42`). The bound itself includes finiteness of the localization-kernel lengths at every prime and an inequality for the weighted sum over every finite set of primes and all indices from one up to the cutoff. Both clauses appear in `UpperTheory.Top.VVle` (`Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:12–17`). Obtaining them uniformly is the large mathematical obligation inside P013.
