\begin{formalstatement}[An averaged bound over deep components]\label{sub:T14}

Assume the eight statements T17--T24. There is a real $C\ge0$ uniform in
the following data. Let a core $T$ on $D$ satisfy the core laws and
$m^2\le k$. Take $1\le j<m$, a deep datum $Y$ of index $j$, an integer
$e\ge0$ and a real $o$ with $Y$ good at $(e,o)$. Suppose its integer
$M'$ has at most fifteen distinct prime factors. For each admissible
weight $u$ with gap $g>0$, there are real numbers $s_Z$, indexed by
components, such that
\[
  s_Z\le\mathrm{compVolSlope}_{Y,\xi_R,8,\mathrm{mixVals}(B),Z}(u),
  \qquad
  \frac{\sum_Z^{\mathrm{fin}}s_Z}{\#\mathrm{Comp}}
   \ge -C\,\mathrm{errX}(\ell(D),u,g),\qquad B=\mathrm{bndB}(u,g).
\]
Here the weight decreases strictly at each embedding, and differences
from each index on the $q$-side to any other index there or on the
$p$-side have absolute value at least $g$.

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T14}
