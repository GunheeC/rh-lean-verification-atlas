\begin{theorem}[One cyclotomic generator for every layer]\label{pn:P031}
Let \(\ell\) be prime and let \(L_0\) contain a primitive \(\ell\)-th
root if \(\ell\) is odd, or a primitive fourth root if \(\ell=2\).
Write \(c_0\) for the largest \(c\) such that \(L_0\) contains a
primitive \(\ell^c\)-th root. There exists \(\gamma\in G_{L_0}\) whose
cyclotomic image topologically generates the image and satisfies
\(v_\ell(\chi(\gamma)-1)=c_0\). The base contains a primitive
\(\ell^{c_0}\)-th root. For every \(E\ge1\), every \(m\ge0\), and
every degree-\(\ell^m\) cyclotomic layer \(L_m/L_0\),
\[
 \operatorname{shaInv}(L_m,\ell,E)=
 \operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}
 \operatorname{coker}\bigl(x_{\mathrm{Tw}}(E,\gamma)^{\ell^m}-1\bigr).
\]
Here \(x_{\mathrm{Tw}}\) is the twisted action on the dual of the
infinite-layer character module with the specified local conditions.
\end{theorem}\nodestatus{P031}
\begin{proof}
We give the tower comparison of Blueprint Z2.K1~\bploc{lem:P-r3-k1}. A closed cyclotomic
image in \(1+\ell^{c_0}\mathbb Z_\ell\) contains an element \(u\) of
exact depth \(c_0\), by maximality. Binomial expansion gives
\(v_\ell(u^{\ell^j}-1)=c_0+j\), using \(c_0\ge2\) at two.
Its powers therefore generate every finite quotient. We may choose
\(\gamma\) above \(1+\ell^{c_0}\); the generator over the \(m\)-th
layer is its \(\ell^m\)-th power in the cyclotomic quotient.

Let \(L_\infty=L_0(\mu_{\ell^\infty})\), \(L_S\) the maximal
extension unramified outside the places over \(\ell\) and infinity,
and \(H=\operatorname{Gal}(L_S/L_\infty)\). The same \(L_S\) works
over every \(L_m\), by taking normal closures. Let \(B\) be the
continuous characters \(H\to A_E\) trivial on the decomposition groups
above \(\ell\). With \(\Gamma_m=\operatorname{Gal}(L_\infty/L_m)\),
restriction identifies the localization kernel over \(L_m\) with
\(B^{\Gamma_m}\). Indeed inflation--restriction has outside terms
\(H^1(\Gamma_m,A_E)\) and \(H^2(\Gamma_m,A_E)\). The first vanishes
because a generator acts by a scalar different from one and the
difference acts surjectively on \(A_E\). The second vanishes by the
two-term resolution of the trivial module over the completed group
ring of \(\mathbb Z_\ell\). For a place above \(\ell\) the local image
in \(\Gamma_m\) is open; its generator gives the same degree-one
vanishing. Hence a restricted character vanishes on the indicated
local subgroup exactly when the finite-layer class is locally trivial.

Dualize \(B\) into \(\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell\). Since the target
is injective, the dual of the fixed subgroup is the cokernel of the
dual inverse-generator action minus the identity. This action is
\(x_{\mathrm{Tw}}(E,\gamma)^{\ell^m}\). Duality preserves finite
length by a composition series. In the infinite-length case arbitrarily
large finite submodules dualize to quotients of arbitrarily large length.
This gives the claimed equality for extended lengths as well.
\end{proof}

The inflation--restriction input is the continuous exact sequence for
a closed normal subgroup (R.~Sharifi, \emph{Group and Galois Cohomology},
\S2.2, paragraph preceding Proposition~2.2.18). The proposition states the higher-degree version with a vanishing hypothesis; the paragraph explicitly gives the closed-normal-subgroup version used here. The additional comparisons identify its local subgroups and
the action on the particular twisted dual at every layer.
\cite{r21-sharifi}

For the next results write \(q=\ell^\nu,\ \kappa=1+\ell^c\).
A layer consists of a cyclic Galois extension \(F/L\) with a primitive
\(q\)-th root \(\zeta\), and a generator \(\gamma\) of order
\(h=\ell^{\nu-c}\) acting by \(\zeta\mapsto\zeta^\kappa\);
\(1\le c\le\nu\), with \(2\le c\) at \(\ell=2\). For \(a\in\mathbb N\)
put \(N_a(\alpha)=\prod_{i=0}^{h-1}(\gamma^i\alpha)^{a^i}\).
For a divisor \(D\) the difference condition means
\(\operatorname{ord}_P\alpha=D(\gamma P)-aD(P)\) for all primes \(P\).
