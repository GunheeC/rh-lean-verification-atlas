\begin{formalstatement}[Uniform bounds at two and three]\label{sub:T25}

Assume the two-layer linkage hypotheses. For every reciprocity system $h$
and parameter $p$ over $h$, the following closed family assertion satisfies the
linked norm condition at $p$. There exist $C,x_0$ such that at all
$x\ge x_0$, $x>x_1$, and each index, for every compatible local
$HW,F$ with its comparison and every hub $X,C_2$ satisfying the specified
setup, the bound holds at each twisted place $i$ with $l_i=2$ or with
$l_i=3$ and $3$ outside the host's small set. For every good $\pi$ it reads
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 v_{cc}(i,\pi)&\le s_\pi(\rho_i)+\mathrm{midF}(L,C)
       +\mathrm{VVr}(l_i)+v_{hE}(l_i)+\varepsilon_L(l_i),\\
 \mathrm{midF}(L,C)&=C f m b\log x+C x^C\mathrm{large}_2(L)^2 .
 \end{aligned}
\]

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T25}
