**Changing the width and the point of evaluation**

Let $H$ be any finite abelian group, $D$ any arithmetic datum on $E_0\times H$, and $\ell$ any complex-valued function on its characters. For every character $\xi$ and all $A,w,b>0$,

$$

 \big|wG_\xi(A/w)-bG_\xi(A/b)\big|
       \le 3\frac{A}{\min(w,b)}|w-b|.

$$

There is also an absolute $C_d\ge0$ for which, if $y,z\ge1$ and $M=\max(y,z)$,

$$

\begin{aligned}
 |G_0(y)-G_0(z)|&\le C_d|y-z|,\\
 |G_\xi(y)-G_\xi(z)|&\le |y-z|\big(\log M+C_d+|\ell(\xi)|\big)
                 &&(\xi\ne0).
\end{aligned}

$$

`lipschitz_weight_all` and `derivative_integral_all` (D562), `LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:957–981`.

**Proof.**

Write $s_+=\max(s,0)$ and $\Lambda(n)$ for the von Mangoldt function. We first prove the numerical bound that determines the width constant. Put $\Psi(t)=\sum_{1\le n\le t}\Lambda(n)$. For an integer $N\ge2$, let $a=\lfloor N/2\rfloor$ and $b_0=N-a$. The identity $\log N!=\sum_{n\le N}\Lambda(n)\lfloor N/n\rfloor$ follows by summing $\log j=\sum_{n\mid j}\Lambda(n)$, which is just prime factorization. Hence the coefficient of $\Lambda(n)$ in $\log\binom{N}{a}$ is $\lfloor N/n\rfloor-\lfloor a/n\rfloor-\lfloor b_0/n\rfloor\ge0$. It equals one for $b_0<n\le N$. Therefore $\Psi(N)-\Psi(b_0)\le\log\binom{N}{a}\le N\log 2$. Now $N\le3a$ for $N\ge2$. Induction, starting with $\Psi(0)=\Psi(1)=0$, gives $\Psi(N)\le3b_0+N\log 2\le3N$; taking floors gives $\Psi(t)\le3t$ for every $t\ge0$. This is an elementary derivation of the estimate in `AN.psi_le_three`, `AN.sum_vM_le`, `LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:668–694`; for the factorial identity see `sum_log_eq`, `GzLipschitz.lean:28–53`.

For $\xi\ne0$, let $c_n=\Lambda(n)\operatorname{primVal}(D,\xi,n)$. Each factor of $\operatorname{primVal}$ is an average of $\xi$ over a coset of a finite inertia subgroup. Character values have modulus one, so $|c_n|\le\Lambda(n)$. The definitions at all positive $t$ become

$$

 G_\xi(t)=\sum_{n\ge1} c_n(t/n-1)_+ +t\ell(\xi),\qquad
 G_0(t)=\sum_{n\ge1}\Lambda(n)(t/n-1)_+-t\log t+(1+\gamma)t,

$$

where $\gamma$ is Euler's constant. Both sums have finite support. Set $\mu=\min(w,b)$. Multiplying the first equality at $A/w$ by $w$ changes the kernel to $(A/n-w)_+$ and the linear term to $A\ell(\xi)$. At $b$ the linear term is identical. The positive-part function is $1$-Lipschitz, so the difference has modulus at most $|w-b|\Psi(A/\mu)\le3A|w-b|/\mu$. `cosetMean`, `primVal`, `GPsi`, `FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean:61–70`; `Sz`, `Gz`, `StatementOC.lean:11–13`; `AN.norm_coef_le`, `AN.wG_char`, `AN.lip_char`, `LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:745–769,828–875`.

For $\xi=0$, exchange $w,b$ if necessary so that $w\le b$. The difference $wG_0(A/w)-bG_0(A/b)$ is $U-V$, where

$$

 U=\sum_{n\le A/w}\Lambda(n)\big((A/n-w)_+-(A/n-b)_+\big),
 \qquad V=A\log(b/w).

$$

Both have the same sign: $0\le U\le3A(b-w)/w$ and $0\le V\le A(b/w-1)$. Thus $|U-V|\le\max(U,V)\le3A(b-w)/w$. This subtraction preserves the constant $3$ for the principal character, including the case of an empty prime sum. `AN.wGz`, `AN.lip_triv_le`, `AN.lip_triv`, `LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:879–953`.

For the increments put $L(N)=\sum_{1\le n\le N}\Lambda(n)/n$. The factorial identity bounds $\log N!$ between $N L(N)-\Psi(N)$ and $NL(N)$. Also $N\log N-N+1\le\log N!\le N\log N$ for $N\ge1$, by comparison with the integral of $\log t$ and with $N\log N$. Consequently $\log N-1\le L(N)\le\log N+3$. For nonprincipal $\xi$, each kernel difference costs at most $|y-z|/n$, whence the increment is bounded by $|y-z|(L(\lfloor M\rfloor)+|\ell(\xi)|)$. For principal $\xi$, the finite-sum formula is continuous at each integer: the new summand vanishes at its entrance. On $(N,N+1)$ it gives $G'_0(t)=L(N)-\log t+\gamma$. For $t\ge1$, $t/N\le2$, so $-1-\log2\le L(N)-\log t\le3$. Apply the mean-value theorem on the subintervals cut by the integers between $y$ and $z$, then sum their lengths. Both estimates hold with $C_d=3+|\gamma|$. Only existence of a common constant enters D562. `mertens_upper`, `mertens_lower`, `hasDerivAt_P`, `lip_piece`, `GzLipschitz.lean:55–106,127–184`; `AN.deriv_char`, `LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:778–826`.
