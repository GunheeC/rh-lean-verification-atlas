\begin{formalstatement}[Integral orthogonality for Hodge--Tate kernels]\label{sub:T24}

Given the open-literature record, fix a prime $p$, an algebraically closed
characteristic-zero valued field $C$ with $v(p)<1$, a valuation ring $O$
which is a $\mathbb Z_p$-algebra complete for $(p)$, and a $p$-divisible
group $G/O$. Choose $\zeta_0=1$, $\zeta_{n+1}^p=\zeta_n$, $\zeta_1\ne1$.
Let $B$ pair the two $O$-extended Tate modules of $G,G^\vee$ bilinearly.
On pure Tate elements require the Weil pairing to be
$a\mu_{\mathrm{Tate}}(\zeta)$ and $B(1\otimes x,1\otimes\chi)=a$ in $O$
for some $a\in\mathbb Z_p$, for every $x,\chi$.
Then $\mathrm{HT}_G(x)=0$ if and only if $B(x,y)=0$ for all
$y\in\ker(\mathrm{dlog}_G)$ in the dual-side module.

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T24}

\noindent Integral kernels over the chosen valuation ring and the pairing specified using a compatible system of roots require a comparison. \href{https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.2157}{L. Fargues, La filtration canonique des points de torsion des groupes p-divisibles, §5.3}~\cite{r21-fargues}.
