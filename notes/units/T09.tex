\begin{formalstatement}[Separation on the evaluation grid]\label{sub:T09}

Assume $F$, unramified Howe duality in its two spherical-span directions, and
the convergent unitary Siegel--Weil input. For each $\varepsilon$ and
$C_F\ge6510$ some $x_0$ works for all size data of exponent $\varepsilon$
at $x\ge x_0$ satisfying the row sizes and the Eisenstein tie. For every
compatible finite-group family, real datum with its level character and group
record, grid $(1,C_F,b)$, Weil model above the datum, row volume and
nonzero smooth vector $w$, there exist a real vector $\rho$ and coefficient
functional $\xi$. For every nonzero $t$ in the trivial-character evaluation
space there are a column $\Psi$ and grid datum $\Phi$ for which the
pairing of the row form of $(w,t)$ converges and is nonzero.

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T09}

\noindent The uniform grid, convergent test pair and nonzero row pairing require a separate transfer. \href{https://msp.org/pjm/2013/264-1/pjm-v264-n1-p11-p.pdf}{S. Yamana, The Siegel–Weil formula for unitary groups, Theorem 2}~\cite{r21-yamana}.
