\begin{formalstatement}[D562]\label{decomp:D562}
There is one nonnegative Cd such that for every finite additive group, arithmetic data and character functional, every nontrivial character satisfies the Lipschitz bound |Gpsi(y)\ensuremath{-}Gpsi(z)| \ensuremath{\le} |y\ensuremath{-}z|(log(max(y,z))+Cd+|ell(psi)|), and |Gz(y)\ensuremath{-}Gz(z)| \ensuremath{\le} Cd|y\ensuremath{-}z|, for y,z at least 1.
\end{formalstatement}
