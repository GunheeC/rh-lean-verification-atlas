\begin{formalstatement}[Nef growth of the chart lattice]\label{sub:T23}

Given the open-literature record, let $y$ be a birational
multihomogeneous presentation in a characteristic-zero field with
arithmetic variety $P$ of dimension $D+1$, and let the integral
multidegree $a$ be nef on the generic fibre. Put
$V_n=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}(\mathrm{chartLatNm}(y,a,n))$.
Each $V_n$ is finite-dimensional and
\[
 \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{D!\,\dim_{\mathbb Q}V_n}{n^D}
       =\mathrm{genDeg}(P,D,a).
\]

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T23}

\noindent The geometric section asymptotic needs a comparison with the rational span of the specified chart lattice. \href{https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-18808-4}{R. Lazarsfeld, Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I, Corollary 1.4.41}~\cite{r21-laz}.
