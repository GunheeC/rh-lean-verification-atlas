\begin{formalstatement}[All-prime torsion from faces and stabilizers]\label{pn:P038}
For every totally complex number field \(L\) and \(N,m\ge0\), let
\(d=[L:\mathbb Q]N\), \(s=3^{dN}\), and \(A=\mathrm{orbBound}(L,N,m)\),
the uniform face-orbit bound of the integral Hermitian complex through
degree \(m\). Then \(T=H_m(G_N,\mathbb Z)_{\rm tors}\) is finite with
\[
                    |T|\le(m+2)^A s^{m A s^m}.
\]
\end{formalstatement}\nodestatus{P038}
\begin{outline}
Blueprint Z2.K7~\bploc{prop:P-r4-k7} constructs the face complex from normal cones of
vertices of the polyhedron of Hermitian forms with
\(\sum_{v\mid\infty} y_v^*h_v y_v\ge1\) for each nonzero integral
lattice vector \(y\). Their intersections in the positive dual
cone are convex and cover that cone locally finitely. The augmented
face chains \(C_i=\mathbb Z[X_i]\) are exact. The integral orbit
calculation bounds the number of orbits in degree \(i\le m\) by \(A\).
Each stabilizer fixes a positive definite form, hence is finite.
Reduction into the matrix ring modulo 3 embeds any such finite
subgroup, giving order at most \(3^{dN}=s\). The case \(N=0\) has
trivial group and already satisfies the bound.

A transitive face orbit with stabilizer \(J\) contributes \(H_j(J,\mathbb Z)\)
to \(H_j(G_N,C_i)\) by induction from \(J\). For \(j>0\) the group
is killed by \(|J|\), and its bar chains in degree \(j\) have rank
\(|J|^j\). Thus \(|H_j(G_N,C_i)|\le s^{As^j}\). Dimension-shift
along the exact augmented complex toward degree zero. In each step
the kernel is an image of the corresponding \(H_j(G_N,C_i)\).
The last cokernel is computed by the boundary map between face
coinvariants. Its target is free of rank at most \(A\), and each
column has \(\ell^1\)-norm at most \(m+2\) by the face formula.
Projecting independent columns to a nonsingular minor and applying
Hadamard bounds the saturation index by \((m+2)^A\). This also
works with infinitely many source columns. Multiplying the bounds
and using \(\sum_{j=1}^m s^j\le ms^m\) proves the inequality.
\end{outline}

Soulé's \emph{A bound for the torsion in the \(K\)-theory of algebraic
integers}, \S4.1, Theorem~1, assumes \(N\ge2\) and removes the prime
factors \(p\le1+\max(r,N)\) from the order of the torsion in \(H_k(\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathcal O_F),\mathbf Z)\), as indicated by its operator \(\mathrm{card}_{1+\sup(r,N)}\) (p.~778). Here finite stabilizer homology provides integral
control at those primes as well. \cite{r21-soule}
