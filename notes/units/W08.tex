and the rank-three automorphy input for unramified coefficient rings.

Type~I automorphy and Type~II modularity conclusions for the
specializations of each allowed deformation ring. For every $\chi$
there are $C\in\mathbb N$ and primes $r_1,r_2$ such that, for each
large host with $q_1,d>\max(r_1,r_2)$, $0<e<t$, every algebraic
closure, every choice of coefficient rings $O_\ell$ and every finite
set $T$ of odd primes with $\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\nmid2e$,

\[
 \sum_{\ell\in T}\log\ell\!
       \sum_{\psi\in X_h(O_\ell,e)} v_\ell(\psi,e)\le (Nt)^C .
\]
