## Which characters are being summed

Put $E_0=(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $s_0=((1,0),0)$,
$e_1(t)=s_0+(0,t)$ and
$\mathcal A=\{\xi\in\widehat{E_0\times H}:\xi(0,A_0)=1\}$.
The character sum at $y$ is denoted by $G_\xi(y)$.
Fix the character subset $K'$ and the real parameters $c,M,K_o,C_E$.
Define $v_\xi=\mathrm{wt}_c(\xi)\Re G_\xi(x)$ for $\xi\in K'$,
and $v_\xi=-|G_\xi(x)|$ for $\xi\in\mathcal A\setminus K'$.
Here $\mathrm{wt}_c$ is $1$ on the characters trivial on $H$, and $c$
on the other characters. Thus
$G:=\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal A}v_\xi=G_m^A(x)-\Sigma_A(x)$
by splitting the finite sum ([D565 in B09](../../units/B09.md)).

## Conversion to a character lower bound, D564–D565

Use $y_t=a_t/b$, so $|y_t-x|\le1$, and take $x\ge3$.
For nonzero $\xi$, $G_\xi(1)=\ell(\xi)$, whence the increment estimate gives
$|G_\xi(x)|\le x(\log x+C_d+|\ell(\xi)|)$.
For nonzero kept $\xi$, first replace $G_\xi(y_t)$ by $G_\xi(x)$ under
$\sum_t\xi(e_1(t))$. The cost per term is at most
$\log(x+1)+C_d+K_o$. Next replace the sum of its coefficients by
$n_T\mathrm{wt}_c(\xi)$: the values at $e_1(t)$ and $(0,t)$ agree,
and the total error is bounded by
$n_T M x^{-1+\varepsilon}x(\log x+C_d+K_o)$.
For $\xi$ trivial on $H$ this latter error is zero. For the principal
frequency the entire cost per term is $C_d$. For each discarded frequency
use $\Re(\xi(e_1(t))G_\xi(y_t))\ge-|G_\xi(y_t)|$ and the increment
bound to get a lower bound $v_\xi-(\log(x+1)+C_d+|C_E|\log x)$.
These are exactly the three cases of [D564](../../units/D564.md).

For a threshold independent of the host, let $s=x^{1/4}$,
$e=x^\varepsilon$ and
$$
 A_1=5+C_d+|K_o|+|M|(4+C_d+|K_o|)+4|C_E|.
$$
Since $\log x\le4s$, $\log(x+1)\le5s$ and $1\le s,e$, each of the
three per-character costs per $t$ is at most $A_1 s e$ (the coefficient
error uses $x^{-1+\varepsilon}x=e$). Increase the threshold until
$|M|A_1\le s$. Summing over the band costs at most $n_Ts^2e$,
so the factor four in the following bound is slack: the cost is at most $4n_Tx^{1/2+\varepsilon}$. Taking real parts gives
$$
 n_T(G-4x^{1/2+\varepsilon})
 \le \sum_{t\in TT}\Re\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal A}
                 \xi(e_1(t))G_\xi(y_t).
$$

## The four terms after division by the number of columns

Here is the numerical implication used in B09. Write $n=|H|$, $t=|TT|$,
$r=|T_2|$, $\delta=r/n$, $Q=tmb$, and $G=G_m^A-\Sigma_A$. Put
$X=x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$, $Y=x^{1/2+13\varepsilon/8}$ and $P=x^{C\varepsilon}$.
The bracket before normalization is
$$
 \mathrm{Br}=t a_E(1+m^2/k)
       +m\left(2nmbx^\varepsilon+
       \frac{r k b x^\varepsilon}{n^2 x^{-\varepsilon}}\right).
$$
Here $r=|T_2|$ is a cardinality local to this calculation.
The size record in [D566](../../units/D566.md) gives, with $x\ge3$, $\varepsilon>0$, $C\ge0$, $b>0$ and $n>0$,
$$
\begin{gathered}
0\le a_E\le2xb,\qquad n/t\le 2x^{\varepsilon/8},\qquad
x^{-\varepsilon}\le\delta\le1,\\
x^{1/2-\varepsilon}\le m\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon/2},\qquad
xm\le k\le n^2 x^{1/2-\varepsilon}.
\end{gathered}
$$
In particular $t,m,k,Q$ are positive. More precisely the original inequality
$t\ge n x^{-\varepsilon/8}/2$ gives positivity of $t$ before taking
reciprocals. Assume $26\le x^{3\varepsilon/8}$, as is possible for
every fixed $\varepsilon>0$.
See `SizeWindows`, `GrowthBoundGivesRiemannHypothesis.lean:109–123`,
and `size_windows_split`, `ChainSlopeWindows/PChain.lean:23–26`.

**Size bound, [D563](../../units/D563.md).** 
If $\mathrm{Err}\le P\,\mathrm{Br}$ for the source bracket and the
character average gives
$$
Q\,\frac{\delta}{2}(G-4X)\le\mathrm{Err}+2Q/x,
$$
then $G\le x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}$.

**Proof.** 
Expand $\mathrm{Br}$ and divide its four summands by $Q$.
For the first, the lower rank bound gives $x/m\le X$; for the
third the upper rank bound controls the extra factor of $m$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a_E}{mb}&\le2x/m\le2Y,\\
\frac{a_E m}{bk}&\le2xm/k\le2Y,\\
2(n/t)m x^\varepsilon
 &\le4x^{\varepsilon/8+1/2+\varepsilon/2+\varepsilon}=4Y,\\
\frac{r k}{n^2t}x^{2\varepsilon}
 &\le(n/t)x^{1/2+\varepsilon}\le2x^{1/2+9\varepsilon/8}\le2Y .
\end{aligned}
$$
These are precisely the terms of `errorBracketSizeWindows`
(`ErrorFormsWeakWindowsFalse.lean:133–136`);
the four estimates occur as `SZ.T1`–`SZ.T4`
in `LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:31–113`.
Consequently $\mathrm{Err}/Q\le 10PY$. Since $2/x\le1\le PY$, division by
$Q$ gives $\delta(G-4X)\le22PY$. Divide by $\delta>0$ and use
$\delta^{-1}\le x^\varepsilon$. Both $X$ and $PYx^\varepsilon$ are at
most $Z=x^{1/2+(C+21/8)\varepsilon}$ (the second equals $Z$), so
$G\le4X+22PYx^\varepsilon\le26Z$. Finally $26Z\le x^{3\varepsilon/8}Z$
is the asserted bound. See `proposition_size_bound`,
`LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:507–592`.
