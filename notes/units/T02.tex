\begin{formalstatement}[A common definite-side witness]\label{sub:T02}

Assume $P,F$, agreement of the theta integrals, local matching and T13's
covolume bound. For any reciprocity system $h$, suppose that for every real $c$
the closed family bound at two and three of T25 satisfies the linked norm
condition at $p(c)$. Then there exist a family class $\mathcal A$, a raw selection
of its bundles, a threshold function of two real variables, and all certificates
in \nolinkurl{HypsAlt} for the definite-side condition and $N(\mathcal C)
=\forall F_m\in\mathcal C,\ \mathrm{LinkN}(F_m)$.
The certificates include the existence and duty bounds, twists, row
primitivity and divisibility, spectral rows and counts, selected and twisted
periods, local kernels, masses and the remaining linkage. They belong to the
same selection. The proof takes the maximum of the evaluation-grid and
Fourier-fit thresholds before constructing it.
