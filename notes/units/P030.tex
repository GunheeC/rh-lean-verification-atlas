\begin{formalstatement}[A rational classifier of variable purity]\label{pn:P030}
Assume \(H'\), the level and Satake--Frobenius correspondence for
imaginary quadratic fields, and preservation of admissibility whenever
quadratic restriction is irreducible. For each \(n\ge2\) there exist
\(c,A\in\mathbb N\). For all \(m_1>\cdots>m_n\),
\(m_i+m_{n+1-i}=w_0\in\mathbb Z\), \(|m_i|\le H\), \(M\ge1\),
and \(p\nmid M\) prime, the rational place above \(p\) admits data
\(D\) of weight \(m\), purity \(w_0\), and \(|D.S|\le c[M(H+1)]^A\).
These data classify \(\mathrm{AdmRNAlt}(\iota,M,m,w_0)\) representations
of \(G_{\mathbb Q}\) at this place for every odd \(\ell\ne p\), closure
and \(\iota\).
\end{formalstatement}\nodestatus{P030}

Blueprint Z1.5.05~\bploc{r8:small:Z1.5.05} starts with \(n^2+2\) quadratic fields, of which
\(n^2+1\) remain after avoiding the auxiliary prime. Given the
quadratic classifiers and the aggregation lemma, the rational bound
has constant \(2^{n+1}(n^2+1)\sum c_j\) and exponent \(\sum A_j\).
Recovering compatible roots and controlling the restriction fibres by
quadratic self-twists are the unwritten parts. For a cuspidal invariant
lift over a cyclic extension of prime degree, Arthur and Clozel,
\emph{Simple Algebras, Base Change, and the Advanced Theory of the Trace
Formula}, Chapter~III, Theorem~4.2(d), identify the cuspidal descent
fibre up to twists by powers of the associated character. The rational
classifier needs the corresponding fibre test for the admitted Galois
representations together with compatible Frobenius root choices over
the selected fields. For that difference the proof is in Lean only.
