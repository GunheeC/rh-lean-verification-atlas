\begin{formalstatement}[Assembly of the five counting bounds]\label{sub:W13}
Assume Poitou--Tate duality, local finiteness and local Tate duality.
Fix a $C_K$ satisfying W05's cohomological bound and constants as
in W12 giving its simultaneous bound at $C_t=401$. Assume W14's
minus-part bound, the collective estimate of W08, both individual
estimates of W09, the restricted Case~V estimate of W10 and the
individual estimate of W09's shape for Case~Z (odd
$\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\mid2e$, $\psi=1$).
Then the Selmer window $V$ of \nodeid{P013} holds.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{W13}
The auxiliary joint-window theorem treats the remaining cases internally.
The last step uses the equivalence between the free minus-part bound and
its formulation for admissible cutoff functions. See \nodeid{P020}.
