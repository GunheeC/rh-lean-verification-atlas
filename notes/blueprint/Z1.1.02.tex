\paragraph{Z1.1.02. One determinant identity for the requested twist.}
\phantomsection\label{r8:small:Z1.1.02}
In \nolinkurl{UpperTheory.V.Z.Sym.W.seedInW_alt} put
\(\Theta=\chi'\eta\theta\). The chosen complex conjugation \(c\) has square
one and the character \(\theta\) has odd \(\ell\)-power condition
\(\theta^\ell=1\), so \(\theta(c)^2=\theta(c)^\ell=1\) forces
\(\theta(c)=1\). On \(G_L\) both \(\eta\) and \(\theta\) are trivial by
the two kernel assumptions. Thus \(\Theta|_{G_L}=\chi'|_{G_L}\).
The intersection of the three open kernels is contained in \(\ker\Theta\),
so the latter and \(\ker\Theta^{-1}\) are open. This verifies the parity
and finite-character conditions for the twist request with infinity
weight \(-(m+1)\). Conditional on the resulting \(\kappa\), take \(s'\)
conjugate to \(s\) by \(c\), let \(a=\chi_{\rm cyc}(s)\), and
\(b=\Theta(s)\), via restriction. The two supplied equalities give
\(\det\rho(s)=a^m b\) and \(\kappa(s)\kappa(s')=a^{-(m+1)}b^{-1}\).
Their product is \(a^{-1}\), the required polarization multiplier.
An alternative conjugation differs from \(c\) by an element of \(G_L\);
conjugating \(s'\) by that element leaves the one-dimensional \(\kappa(s')\)
unchanged. S19 controls the finite exceptional set at a fixed coefficient
prime. The existence of the twist with all prescribed local types, and the
window for that same twist, remain at \nolinkurl{Pl.lemTWM} and
\nolinkurl{seedInW_alt} (\path{LemTWM.lean:216--245};
\path{TSeedInW.lean:117--202,217--270}).
