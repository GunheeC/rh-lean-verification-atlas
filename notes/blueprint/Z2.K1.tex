\section*{Z2.K1. Cyclotomic tower and localization kernel}

% Parent source SHA-256: 1af64922b98ed2310744411b1f18b25cc426c97220646321471836e1a3a409d6
% blueprint/ch_P.tex lines 844 through 918; lem:P-r3-k1
\begin{lemma}[One tower module for all layers (K1)]\label{lem:P-r3-k1}
Under the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{prop:P-k-layer}, put
$L_\infty=L(\mu_{\ell^\infty})$. There is one twisted module $X$ with
automorphism $g$ such that the localization kernel at layer $m$ has
extended length $\operatorname{length} X/(g^{\ell^m}-1)X$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $c_0$ for the initial maximum root exponent. The cyclotomic image
is closed, lies in $1+\ell^{c_0}\mathbf Z_\ell$ and contains an element
$u$ with $v_\ell(u-1)=c_0$ by maximality. The binomial expansion gives
$v_\ell(u^{\ell^j}-1)=c_0+j$ since $c_0\ge2$ at $2$. Its powers
therefore generate each finite quotient of this principal unit group.
The image is the whole group, and $L_m=L(\mu_{\ell^{c_0+m}})$ has
degree $\ell^m$. The finite set of primes over $\ell$ admits choices of places in a
fixed algebraic closure, and the cyclotomic character factors through the
unramified-outside-$\ell$ quotient. These choices give the local data
used in \nolinkurl{step0} and \nolinkurl{ShaAsm_holds}
(\nolinkurl{ldata_nonempty}, \path{GaloisCohomologyProofs/LdataNonempty.lean:39--48}).
Choose $\gamma$ acting by $1+\ell^{c_0}$, so the
subgroup over $L_m$ has generator $\gamma^{\ell^m}$. See
\nolinkurl{cycRange}, \nolinkurl{layerUniq}
(\path{NumberFieldTowers/CycRange.lean:9}, \path{LayerUniq.lean:25--40}).

Let $S$ consist of the places over $\ell$ and the infinite places.
The cyclotomic tower is unramified outside $S$ since roots of
$\ell$-power order form an \'etale group there. Each $L_m$ has the same
maximal extension $L_S$ unramified outside $S$ inside a fixed closure:
the normal closure over $L$ of a finite extension of $L_m$ unramified
outside $S$ is still unramified there. Put $H=\operatorname{Gal}(L_S/L_\infty)$,
$A=A_E$, and let $B$ be the continuous homomorphisms $H\to A$
vanishing on all decomposition groups of $H$ above $\ell$.
The quotient $\Gamma_m=\operatorname{Gal}(L_\infty/L_m)$ acts on
$B$ through the action on $A$ and inverse conjugation on $H$.
Away from $\ell$ the inertia condition on these characters has already
been imposed by $L_S$. These are the local conditions in
\nolinkurl{XDual}, \nolinkurl{EqHom}
(\path{KummerTheory/Mk0IdMapCongr.lean:90--110}).

Restriction identifies the localization kernel with $B^{\Gamma_m}$.
Indeed $H$ acts trivially on $A$ and the inflation--restriction sequence
has outside terms $H^1(\Gamma_m,A)$ and $H^2(\Gamma_m,A)$.
For a topological generator $s$ its action on $A$ is a scalar
$\chi(s)^{-E}\ne1$. Multiplication by its difference from $1$ is
surjective on $\mathbf Q_\ell/\mathbf Z_\ell$. Subtracting a
coboundary therefore makes a cocycle zero on $s$, hence on its dense
powers. The degree-two term vanishes by the two-term free resolution
of $\mathbf Z_\ell$ over
$\mathbf Z_\ell[[\Gamma_m]]=\mathbf Z_\ell[[s-1]]$ with differential
multiplication by $s-1$. Thus every invariant character lifts uniquely
as a global cohomology class. At $w\mid\ell$ the image of the local
cyclotomic character is open: the cyclotomic extensions over
$\mathbf Q_\ell$ have unbounded degree, and passage to the finite
field extension $(L_m)_w$ changes the image by at most its degree.
It is an open subgroup of $\Gamma_m$, so the same degree-one argument
gives local injectivity. Vanishing on the infinite-level decomposition
group is therefore equivalent to local triviality at $w$.
The project records the restriction, lifting and field-identifications in
\nolinkurl{resInfInj}, \nolinkurl{resInfOnto}, \nolinkurl{desc}
(\path{KummerTheory/ResInfInj.lean:58}, \path{ResInfOnto.lean:102},
\path{NumberFieldTowers/Imports.lean:10--12}).

Take $X=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf Z_\ell}(B,\mathbf Q_\ell/\mathbf Z_\ell)$,
and take $g$ dual to the inverse generator action. Fixed elements under
a generator and its inverse coincide. Dualizing the kernel of
$\gamma^{-\ell^m}-1$ identifies its dual with the indicated quotient:
a functional on a submodule extends, since the target is divisible over
the discrete valuation ring. Finite-length modules and their duals have
the same length by a composition series. An infinite-length discrete
$\ell$-primary torsion module contains finite submodules of arbitrarily
large length, whose duals are quotients of its dual. This proves the
equality also at infinite length. See \nolinkurl{shaDGen},
\nolinkurl{cokerDual}, \nolinkurl{exists_descends}
(\path{NumberFieldTowers/ShaDGen.lean:9--11}, \path{CokerDual.lean:8--19},
\path{ExistsDescends.lean:26--57}). \emph{Status: complete.}
\end{proof}
