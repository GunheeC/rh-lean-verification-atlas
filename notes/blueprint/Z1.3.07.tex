\subsection{Z1.3.07. The inertia witness excludes the Greenberg condition}\label{r5:Z1.3.07}
Keep the hypotheses and the particular $(c,y,S')$ of Z1.3.06. The Greenberg condition $\mathrm{Gr}(W,A,y)$ would give a single $x\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ such that
\[
 y(s)_1=W(s)_{11}x-x\qquad(s\in D_A).
\]
The witness for $\mathrm{NzAt}$ lies in $I_A\subset D_A$ and has $W(s)_{11}=1$. The equation then forces its second coordinate to vanish. This contradicts the nonzero coordinate of the same cocycle. Cyclotomic triviality is part of the witness but the last subtraction uses only its matrix entry and membership in $D_A$. The five other pieces of the conclusion follow directly from $\mathrm{Sys}$: the matrices have entries in $c.O$, $y$ is a cocycle with all coordinates in $c.O$, it satisfies the unramified condition away from $\ell$, and it satisfies the tame condition outside $S'$ with the same character and level quantifiers. These are exactly $\mathrm{ES}(W,A)$. Quantifying over the original normal forms and sources gives $\mathrm{ZetaUr}$.
