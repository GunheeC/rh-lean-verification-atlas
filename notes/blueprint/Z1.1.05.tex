\paragraph{Z1.1.05. The inertia bound for the twist on a fixed frame.}
\phantomsection\label{r8:small:Z1.1.05}
Take the Hecke character \(Y\), exponent \(m\) and Artin realization
\(\kappa\) selected in \nolinkurl{UpperTheory.V.Sd.twist_of_printed_results}.
The given \(m\)-power bound on the local values of \(Y\) gives the
corresponding bound on the \emph{same} \(\kappa\): for an inertia element
at a place away from \(\ell\), S05 expresses its \(\kappa\)-value as
\(\iota^{-1}Y_w(u)\) for a unit \(u\). Raising to \(m\) transports the
identity \(Y_w(u)^m=1\) to the coefficient field. For the trivial valuation
the inertia element is already the identity. At an unramified finite place
S04 similarly determines \(\kappa(\mathrm{Frob})\) with the reciprocal
sign. S21 fixes the separate factor \(\chi_{\rm cyc}(c)=-1\) in the
conjugation identity. The local consequences do not construct \(Y\) or
the dense rank-three and rank-two marking on the linked frame. These
inputs remain in \nolinkurl{linked_endomorphism_dense}, called by
\nolinkurl{UpperTheory.V.Z.Sym.Top.linkedEnd_alt}
