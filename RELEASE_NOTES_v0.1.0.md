# RH Lean Verification Atlas · v0.1.0

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

This atlas groups statements and implications from the pinned Lean development into 217 reading units. It contains 55 Full arguments under stated inputs, 127 outlines and 35 statement-only entries. The 182 written units have 739 active steps. [Reading orders](docs/reading-orders.md) · [Progress](PROGRESS.md)

**Evidence.** A third-party audit reports kernel acceptance of the formal development. The atlas records edition-matched AI reviews of 20 written units and step-specific AI review of 6 active steps. Human-checked steps: 0. The conditional argument assumes the Selmer window V, a common definite-side witness F, Green’s input and 31 local implications. The hypothesis table in paper v6 lists the supplementary inputs used in the deduction. The Lean source supplies both V and F. No human-readable derivation of either is available in the atlas.
