\begin{formalstatement}[An integral ordinary zeta cocycle]\label{pn:P026}
Let \(\ell\) be odd, \(e\ge1\), \(A\) a place above \(\ell\), and \(W\)
a continuous integral two-dimensional representation of \(G_{\mathbb Q}\).
Assume the specified normal form: \(W\) is unramified outside \(S\)
containing the primes over \(\ell\), its determinant \(\delta\) has
square one and takes value \(-1\) on complex conjugations, and its
restriction to \(\ker\chi\cap\ker\delta\) is irreducible. At \(A\),
\(W\) is upper triangular,
a positive power of its first diagonal character on inertia equals the
same power of \(\chi^e\), and that character differs from \(\chi\)
somewhere on the decomposition group. Let \(g\) be a newform of weight
\(2e+1\), of level prime to \(\ell\), with unit \(a_\ell(g)\).
Suppose there is a character \(\eta\) trivial on the subgroup fixing
\(\mu_\ell\) pointwise such that \(g\) is attached (via \(\iota\))
to \(W\otimes\chi^{-e}\delta^{-1}\eta\), and
\(W(s)_{22}\chi(s)^e\delta(s)^{-1}\eta(s)=1\) on inertia at \(A\).
There exist a common coefficient ring \(O\) for \(W\), an \(O\)-integral
continuous cocycle \(y\), and a finite set \(S'\) with the following properties.
On inertia away from \(\ell\), \(y\) is locally a coboundary.
There is no scalar \(x\) with \(y(s)_2=(W(s)_{22}-1)x\) throughout
the decomposition group of \(A\).
For every \(n\ge1,\ w\notin S',\ \ell^n\mid Nw-1\), and every admitted
surjective character to \(\mathbb Z/\ell^n\) trivial on the subgroup
fixing \(\mu_{Nw}\) pointwise, the same \(y\) satisfies the tame norm relation.
\end{formalstatement}\nodestatus{P026}
