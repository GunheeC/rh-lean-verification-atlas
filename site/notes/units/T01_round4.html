Statement and hypotheses

Assume $P,F$, local matching, agreement of theta integrals, the closed covolume bound of T13, the reciprocity existence assertion $h$, and the primitive-character window with constant $401$. Then for every $N\ge1$, every complex Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$, and every $s$ with $\Re s>1/2$, one has $L(s,\chi)\ne0$ whenever $\chi\ne\chi_0$ or $s\ne1$. Here $h$ proves that a compatible reciprocity map exists for each place in the quantified setting.
