A single right-half-plane assertion is inserted directly into the first slot and used twice more to obtain the strip and zeta slots. The three components have different domains and exclusions. claimAll (MainTheorems.lean:15–16).

Fix the type of the shared input

The first proposition is nonvanishing for every character strictly to the right of one half, subject to the disjunction on character and argument. The second is an equality of real parts for any character with a zero in the open strip. The third concerns zeta, allows zeros anywhere and explicitly excludes $1$ and the negative even integers. The outer conjunction is right associated. In particular P004 can recover the strip statement without any projection on the individual complex arguments. Claim.HalfPlane (HalfPlane.lean:3–4). Claim.Strip (HalfPlane.lean:6–7). Claim.Zeta (HalfPlane.lean:9–10). claimAll (MainTheorems.lean:15–16).

Use the same half-plane witness in two branches

The first slot is literally P009. In the strip branch the case on the right contradicts that same input. On the left the proof passes to the conductor, removes the finite Euler factors, and reflects the resulting primitive zero to the inverse character. The modulus has changed along this branch. The original universal quantifier over the modulus is what makes a second application to the reflected character available. Claim.strip (ClaimStrip.lean:59–73). Claim.prim_zero (ClaimStrip.lean:23–33). Claim.prim_reflect (ClaimStrip.lean:42–57).

Close the zeta branch with the proper range

For a zero with positive real part below one the modulus-one character and the strip result give the desired equality. For real part at least one the known nonvanishing invoked in the source rules out the zero. In the nonpositive half-plane the argument first excludes zero itself; the gamma-factor zeros that remain would correspond to an excluded negative even integer. Reflect the resulting completed zero to $1-s$ and use nonvanishing for real part at least one. This branch uses its own completed-zeta argument. Identifying it with the separate modulus-one route in P008 would obscure which analytic reductions actually occur in the proof term. Claim.zeta (ClaimStrip.lean:75–100). claimAll (MainTheorems.lean:15–16).
