\begin{theorem}[Zero-freeness from host existence and connectedness]\label{pn:P016}
Let $\lambda>1$. Assume \code{TopG} and \code{HostExW}$(\lambda,G_\psi)$ for every $\psi\in\mathcal P$. Then $L(s,\psi)\ne0$ for every $\psi\in\mathcal P$ and $\Re s>1/2$.
\end{theorem}
\nodestatus{P016}
\begin{proof}
Fix $\psi$ and its cyclic level $\mathcal L$, which contains $\psi$. Every family in $\operatorname{cls}(G_\psi,\lambda)$ eventually satisfies both defining clauses of $G_\psi$ at every index. If these hold at $x>x_1$, take the new threshold $x_1+1$. The second clause gives \code{HypV}. The first gives the family-level explicit-formula hypothesis \code{EFamC} at $\mathcal L$ with $C=4$, since the member has scale $x$ and parameter $\varepsilon$. Now \code{TopG} gives connectedness. Its witnesses have $\lambda$-windows, a chain-tied tail and \code{EFamC}, in the order required by P019. P017 and P018 supply the other premises there. Apply P019 to the primitive characters. This is the reduction of blueprint Chapter~E, \emph{Families, the inputs, and the proof of zero-freeness}~\bploc{sec:E-top}.
\end{proof}
