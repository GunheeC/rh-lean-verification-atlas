How the lattice error fits the arithmetic bracket.

The lattice estimate uses the following bracket and fixed threshold. Write

$$ E=n_Ta(1+m^2/k)+m(B_0+N_c),\qquad K_* =200(1+K)^2(1+c_{HH})(1+e_K). $$

These are ErrText and KBig (ErrorFormsWeakWindowsFalse.lean:16–21).

A common threshold, D550

For every $\varepsilon>0$ and all fixed real $K,c_{HH},e_K$ there is $x_0$ such that $x\ge x_0$ implies $\log x\le x^\varepsilon$ and $K_*\le x^\varepsilon$.

Proof.

Take $x_0=\exp(\max\{0,2/\varepsilon^2,\log(\max\{1,K_*\})/\varepsilon\})$. For $u=\log x\ge2/\varepsilon^2$, positivity of the exponential series gives $e^{\varepsilon u}\ge (\varepsilon u)^2/2\ge u$. Also $e^{\varepsilon u}\ge\max\{1,K_*\}$. This proves both inequalities at the same scale (LErr.large_x_eventually, SubThreeMulLogTwoLe.lean:319–329).

Point for the reviewer

The same threshold must work when the fixed real K_* is smaller than one and when ε is very small.
