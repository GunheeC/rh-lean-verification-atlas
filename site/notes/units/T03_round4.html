T03 · Building the definite-side family from unramified row data

Outlined · pending_review. A definite-side witness can be assembled if rational rows, an eventually available retwisting grid and three linked bounds are supplied. The small-prime bound begins on the dependence parameters. The functional and covolume bounds already live on the stronger extension.

Statement and hypotheses

Assume $P$ and row rationality: every datum in the starting row test with the prescribed group, geometric, weight and residue-characteristic conditions passes the rational row test. Fix a proof $h$ of RecTExists, $c\in\mathbb R$, and an eventually large retwisting predicate $A$. Suppose the small-prime norm bound is linked at $p(c)$, and the functional inequality of T10 and covolume inequality of T13 are linked in their row versions at $p_A(c)$. Then the definite-side family, selection and certificates of T02 exist. The two types of parameter have distinct roles in this implication. hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt · HypothesesNonemptyOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:41-61

Keep the two parameter spaces separate

Fix the proof of reciprocity existence, dependence parameter and retwisting predicate. The last must be eventually available. The extension is constructed with this predicate, the indicated origin, no extra series extension and the closure of unrestricted geometry. The small-prime estimate starts at the original dependence point. The functional and third-place-series covolume conditions refer directly to the constructed extension. First use row rationality and the unitary hypothesis to obtain the mass ratio, membership and bundle existence. The geometric Galois inputs supply the third-layer records, while the unitary input supplies surjectivity and the lower bound. hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt · HypothesesNonemptyOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:41-48

Strengthen a linked norm estimate

Use local finiteness, local Tate duality, the Euler-characteristic input, depth-zero support and the small-prime estimate with the proof of RecTExists. This yields the required preliminary norm rows. The projection from data on the stronger extension to the earlier extension exhibits nonemptiness of the latter. Apply the two consequences of extension monotonicity separately: one transports the open conditions, and one transports the norm rows after specializing the family predicate. This is the delicate compatibility step. It is also the reason the proof cannot simply hand the original small-prime estimate to the last constructor. hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt · HypothesesNonemptyOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:44-53

Build the rows and assemble

Now all four row inputs refer to the strengthened extension: the transported open data, the supplied covolume and functional statements, and the new norm rows. Apply the all-row theorem using global Artin reciprocity, the convergent Siegel–Weil input, the listed discrete-series and harmonic-analysis assumptions, and both surjectivity/lower-bound outputs. What remains is a constructor for nonemptiness. Its independent inputs include thin-scale estimates, decay, finite dimensionality, the type and main-term/row-error assertions, and the volume and mass constants. Supply those with the earlier mass, membership and bundle data and the all-row record. This packages compatible data for one definite-side witness. hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt · HypothesesNonemptyOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:54-61
