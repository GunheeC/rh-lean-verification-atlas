Statement and hypotheses

For every slot system and bundle linkage, six conditions hold eventually on built and good members: evaluation on the reference chain, twisted points, periods at zero and after twisting, compatibility of the two evaluations, and the level-prime column valuations.

$$\bigwedge_{j=1}^{6}\mathrm{Lg1}(\mathrm{KPx},\,\mathrm{IsBuiltE},\,\mathrm{IsGoodE},\,P_j).$$

Understand the six large-slot assertions

Fix the slot system and bundle linkage. The first two predicates concern evaluation on the reference chain and on the twisted point. The next two concern the periods at zero and at the twisted point. The fifth is a tie of the real-side columns, and the sixth concerns level-prime valuations of the columns. In the statement each of these predicates has its own eventual large-slot quantifier. In the proof all six can use the same elementary auxiliary slot. The objects within each predicate still have to be transported to the correct linked bundle point. six_rows_at_member_alt · SixRowsAtMember.lean:11-17

Choose the common column view

Intersect the good slot with the predicate that is always true. The good-twist-core result gives the over-columns property there. The constantly true predicate is itself a large-base condition on built slots, furnishing a witness that can be reused. At this stage the argument has produced a way to read columns at good points. It has yet to deduce any one of the six period or valuation assertions, which are supplied by separate conversions. six_rows_at_member_alt · SixRowsAtMember.lean:18-24

Return each field to the row constructor

The other conversions use the same over-column witness for reference evaluation, twisted-point integrality, twisted-period integrality, real-side compatibility and the level-prime valuations. In the all-row constructor their projections have different positions: the sharp valuation field is selected before the point and period fields, and the tie follows these. A comparison of two periods on different linked points would not fill the projected row field.
