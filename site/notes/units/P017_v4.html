P017, family window estimate. Fix a level $\Lambda$, real $e_D,e_L$ and $\varepsilon>0$. Let $F$ be a family of type FamP at $\varepsilon$ with the uniform explicit-formula hypothesis $\mathrm{EFamC}(\Lambda,F)$. Suppose that there exist $K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x_0$ such that, for every $x\ge x_0$ in $\mathrm{Win}(F)$, $\mathrm{ChainTied}(F;e_D,e_L,K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x)$ holds. Then there exists $X$ such that on this same set for $x\ge X$,

$$ \mathcal G_\Lambda(x)\le x^{1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}.$$

Here $\mathcal G_\Lambda$ is the real weighted prime sum of v4 Definition 6.19 (p. 77). The estimate is one-sided.

Family quantifiers and proof of Proposition 3.1

Unpack $\mathrm{EFamC}$ to obtain $C_E,x_E$ such that every member at each $x\ge x_E$ satisfies $\mathrm{EAtH}$ with $C_E$. Fix the five eventual-tiedness witnesses of the proposition. Let $X$ be the maximum of $x_0,x_E$ and the B08 threshold for these constants. For $x\in \mathrm{Win}(F)$, $x\ge X$, unpack $\mathrm{ChainTied}$ at this $x$. Use its reference member $i_0$, take the translation map to be the identity on its frame group, and use the word fibre of the assigned member $\mathrm{pt}(u)$ at each $u$. Every frame element is its own preimage, so coverage holds on the whole frame group and in particular on $A_0$. The arithmetic tie's test-set membership equation and the family's common $TT$ image identify $TT$ with the reference host's $TT$ by injectivity of the frame coordinate. Its digit description at translate $0$ expresses each $s\in T_2$ as a digit in the cyclic subgroup $A_0$. The same tie supplies the compatible $\ell$. Restrict the weight bounds and cutoff from all translates to $A_0$, and retain the two bijections, identities, lattice size and error bounds, thin scales, costs and remaining-size bounds. These are exactly the existential witnesses for $\mathrm{CoreAtH}$ at $i_0$. Evaluate the universally quantified explicit-formula bound at this very $i_0$. B08 now gives the claimed estimate. Since $X$ was fixed before $x$ and $i_0$, it works on the whole tail of the window. Scope for review

The family may select a different reference member at every $x$. The same threshold works because the explicit-formula constant and all five tiedness witnesses were chosen first. This implication assumes tiedness and the family explicit-formula property.
