Outlined · pending_review. At any chosen dependence parameter, the third-place unitary hypotheses yield twelve open conditions on linked rows. Their shared parameter lets the all-row construction use geometric compatibility, type labels, multiplicities and the other row fields together.

Statement and hypotheses

Given $P$, every proof $h$ of RecTExists and every real $c$, the following twelve conditions are linked at $p(c)$: primitivity, lambda-prime bounds, divisors, decomposition points, selection jumps, ties, labels, goodness, weak geometric compatibility, multiplicities, kappa and defect. Set up the common extension

Fix the proof of reciprocity existence and a real dependence parameter. Every element of the extension with list projects to an inhabitant of the extension with law. At each of its points there is also a recorded bookkeeping law. Obtain the primitive and decomposition-point conditions by their two direct constructions. The real tie uses the extension-with-law witness together with discrete-series, Harish–Chandra, Weil and globalization inputs. Combine the primitive and decomposition data with the tie to obtain the jumps. The presence of the common extension in these calls matters more than the final ordering of the twelve fields. open_orbit_of_unramified_exponents_alt · OpenOrbitOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:39-49

Multiplicity, divisor and prime data

The third-place multiplicity calculation applies the in-repo constant GlobalTheory.Hk3.howeUnram_holds and fields of $P$ for the Weil and Siegel–Weil formulas, doubling, classification and strong approximation. Its two components have distinct uses. Combine its first component with the bookkeeping law to obtain the divisor entry. The real-group calculation gives the lambda-prime entry, using both local representation theory and the finite-group input. Feed that entry into the Harish–Chandra calculation giving kappa. The second component from the multiplicity input remains available for the final tuple. The two uses need not be deduced from one another. open_orbit_of_unramified_exponents_alt · OpenOrbitOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:50-63
