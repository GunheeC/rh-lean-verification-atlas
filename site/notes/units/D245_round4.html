D245 · Where the extra power of the coefficient prime goes

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

pending_review · Unit card · Track guide

If every Frobenius iterate of a positive crystalline period has zero theta value after comparison, its Frobenius image is divisible by the chosen period in the positive crystalline ring. The proof clears a denominator, proves integral divisibility with an extra factor of $p$, and then removes that factor in the localized ring. GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.phiPlus_mem_span_periodT (FontaineLemma.lean:42–72).

Pass to an integral numerator

Let $K$ be the characteristic-zero perfectoid field of the source at the specified prime $p$. The tilt element has untilt one and its inverse-Frobenius untilt differs from one; both conditions are part of the choice of period. For $y$ in the positive crystalline ring write $yp^k=a$ under the coefficient map from the integral crystalline ring. Compatibility of Frobenius on that map and of theta with the comparison map transfers each zero theta value for the iterate of $y$ into a zero theta value for the corresponding iterate of $a$. This step does not assert divisibility by the crystalline period yet. GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.phiPlus_mem_span_periodT (FontaineLemma.lean:42–72). GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.theta_phi_iterate_eq_zero_of_mul_eq (FontaineLemma.lean:31–38).

Use the intermediate ideal

The integral lemma applied to the family of theta equalities yields a coefficient $w$ with $p\varphi(a)$ a multiple of the auxiliary cyclotomic element $\mu$. A second divisibility statement writes $\mu$ as a multiple of the period $t$. Combining the two witnesses gives $p\varphi(a)=t(cw)$ in the integral ring. Keeping the extra $p$ here is essential. These two ideal inclusions are the substantive suppliers; multiplying their witnesses takes place before returning to the positive crystalline ring. GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.p_mul_phi_mem_span_mu (FontaineLemmaPhi.lean:130–183). GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.mu_mem_span_periodT (FontaineLemmaPeriod.lean:273–278).

Undo the denominator in the proper ring

Apply positive Frobenius to $yp^k=a$. It fixes the image of the integer $p$, so multiplying once more by $p$ gives $\varphi(y)p^{k+1}=t(cw)$ in the positive crystalline ring, with coefficients transported by its algebra map. The localization inverts powers of $p$, hence $p^{k+1}$ is a unit. The explicit coefficient $(cw)p^{-(k+1)}$ witnesses membership in the principal ideal generated by the image of $t$. This witness lives in the positive crystalline ring and pertains to $\varphi(y)$; deleting Frobenius or moving the witness back to the integral ring would change the statement. D230 uses comparisons involving these two crystalline rings. GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.phiPlus_mem_span_periodT (FontaineLemma.lean:42–72).

Locate the use of all iterates inside the integral lemma

The integral proof splits $p\varphi(a)$ into an image from the Witt coefficient ring and a term already carrying $\mu$. At the $n$th iterate the second term has zero theta value. For the first term the test reduces to $p$ times the theta value of the coefficient at that iterate. The left side vanishes by the original hypothesis at $n+1$, and cancellation of the nonzero integer $p$ gives zero theta value for the coefficient. The coefficient-ring criterion for membership in the principal ideal of $\mu$ then applies to the full sequence of equalities. This identifies the sensitive quantifier: checking only the initial theta value of $a$ would not supply the $n+1$ input required at each $n$. GroundTheory.PAdicHodge.ACris.p_mul_phi_mem_span_mu (FontaineLemmaPhi.lean:130–183).

First step to challenge. Check that the hypothesis is imposed on every iterate. The integral divisibility lemma requires the entire family of vanishing theta values for the numerator.
