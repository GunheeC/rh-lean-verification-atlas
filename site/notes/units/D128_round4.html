Statement and hypotheses

At the eligible valued points of the specified integral model, a generating section gives a unit representing its theta key. If its $N$-fold box is $a$ times a generator and $8\mid N$, then $a\kappa^{N/8}$ is represented by a unit.

What a unit means at a valued point

Fix a CM host, positive signature sum, a base ring as specified, prime level, and a model with the level, N3, N4 and split-fibre conditions. Fix a compatible point and its lift, a Lie basis, a section and its generic fibre. A generating section gives a valuation-ring unit representing the key and generating box sections in degrees divisible by eight. More generally, if the box section is $a$ times another generator in degree $N$, the required unit represents $a\kappa^{N/8}$. Extend the unit on a chart

Begin on the open generic chart where the key has an invertible representative. The argument uses density of the generic part, normality, countability and the extension criterion at discrete valuations to move from that open to the chosen chart on the model. Apply the criterion twice to obtain extensions of the representative and of its inverse. Their product restricts to one. Injectivity of restriction makes the product one on the model chart. Normalize the section at the lifted point

Choose the chart at the closed point, so the map from the local base factors through it. The chart unit gives the desired value for a generating section. For a general section, choose a generator nonzero on the closed fibre and express the section as its scalar multiple $b$. On the box power the multiple becomes $b^N$. Compare the chosen generator on that product with the box of the original generator; their comparison coefficient is a unit. Compatibility of the key rule with rescaling makes the combination in the conclusion a product of units.
