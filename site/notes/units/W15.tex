\begin{formalstatement}[Vanishing of the exceptional trivial-character length]\label{sub:W15}
For every algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, each prime $\ell\ge5$,
each allowed coefficient ring $O$, and every even $e\ge2$ such that
$\ell-1\mid2e$, the $O$-length of the localization kernel of the
trivial-character coefficient module twisted by $\kappa^{-e}$, at
modulus $\ell$, is zero. The proof term separates
$\ell-1\mid e$ from $\ell-1\nmid e$.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{W15}
