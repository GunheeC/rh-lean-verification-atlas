\begin{formalstatement}[Character hosts on power windows]\label{sub:B07}
For each primitive character $\psi$, assume the bound of
Section~\ref{sec:mechanism} at $t=\lfloor x^{401}\rfloor$ with the
quantifiers of $V$ for this $\psi$. For every $0<\varepsilon<1/52$
there are $C_{T2},C_8,C_5,c_6,C_b,x_0$ with $C_8\ge1$ and
$C_5,c_6,C_b>0$, and a system of scales with exponent-$3/2$ windows.
At each eligible scale $s$ with $s.\varepsilon=\varepsilon$ and
$s.x\ge x_0$ there exist the specified ranks, a host with invariant
$C_{T2}$, and an auxiliary prime parameter satisfying the rank and
host-size conditions. The character request at these data enforces the
kept-character and share conditions for $\operatorname{cyc}(\psi)$,
the pointwise bounds on the other characters (logarithmic-derivative
constant $4$), and the finite-length Selmer bound with budget $\sqrt b$.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{B07}
