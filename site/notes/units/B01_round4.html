Statement and hypotheses

Assume the following stable-range lift. Fix good CM base data, $n\ge1$ and the Hermitian space with matrix $1$ and signature $(n,0)$. There is $d_0$ such that for every $d\ge d_0$, skew space with matrix $\delta I_d$ and signature as in the lift, and splitting Hecke characters $\chi_X,\chi_W$ with the prescribed quadratic restrictions and infinity exponents $m_X,m_W$, each automorphic $U$ with weights $\lambda_v$ satisfying $d+m_W(v)\le2\lambda_v(k)$ for every $v,k$ has a target $U'$ satisfying the specified theta-lift relation. Under this assumption every regular algebraic polarised cuspidal pair as above admits $r$, $w_\pi\in\mathbb Z$ and Hodge--Tate multisets $H_\tau$ satisfying the whole row below.

The representation $r$ is continuous and semisimple. The multiset $H_\tau$ is $(a-1)/2$ minus the infinitesimal character at $\iota\tau$, and $H_{\tau c}=w_\pi-H_\tau$. At each $w\nmid\ell$ and for every parameter map satisfying the local laws in B04, compare $\mathrm{WD}(r|_{E_w})$ with the parameter of the algebraically normalised local component. They have equal dimension. A toral target with zero monodromy gives equality; under the source's IsQpBig, tame and squarefree conditions, a target with zero monodromy gives zero monodromy and equality of characteristic polynomials on degree-zero Weil elements. For every tied local comparison datum and inducing bridge above $\ell$, a spherical component gives crystallinity, the de Rham property and jumps $H_\tau$ at every induced embedding. A zero-monodromy toral target, or a zero-monodromy target satisfying the three tame-range conditions, gives the de Rham and potentially semistable properties, the same jumps and the preceding Weil--Deligne comparison. galoisRepresentations_of_thetaLift · GaloisRepresentationsOfThetaLift.lean:6-6
