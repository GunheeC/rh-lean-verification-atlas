\begin{formalstatement}[Connected families from Green and the closed link]\label{sub:B05}
Assume the finite-field Green character statement. For any family class
$\mathcal A$, take the data and hypotheses of the alternative shape:
existence from the Minkowski division input, the family duties, and a
twist selection with spectral, level, height, column and bounded-row
realisations. Impose its twist, primitivity, division, point, size,
literature, volume, decay and count conditions; its definite rows and
indefinite selections; its point, period and complement conditions for
the served twists; its closed count, projection and real-place ties;
and its volume-ratio and genus-mass hypotheses. The two scalar predicates
$V,NR$ in the shape are true. Its extra family premise is the following
closed link on $D_3(3,\mathcal A)$: eventually there are common constants
$C,x_0$ for a family so that, for each member at $x\ge x_0$ with finite
prescribed localisation lengths and each lift $j$, summability of their
weighted prime terms $v_\ell$ gives
\[
  \eta_j\le dZ_{0,j}\left[C\{fmbL^2+(\log x)^2N_c\}
                         +\sum_\ell v_\ell\right].
\]
Here $\eta_j$ sums weighted local errors at the varying places. The shape
concludes that for every $\lambda>1$ and every request $G$, if hosts exist
on $\lambda$-windows and every family in the request class eventually
satisfies its Selmer bound, then there are $e_D,e_L$ and $\varepsilon_1>0$.
For every $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_1$ that class contains a family
with constants $K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x_0$ and tied data on every window scale
$x\ge x_0$.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{B05}
