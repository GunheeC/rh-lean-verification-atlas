The finite character average and its four size terms

Which characters are being summed

Put $E_0=(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $s_0=((1,0),0)$, $e_1(t)=s_0+(0,t)$ and $\mathcal A=\{\xi\in\widehat{E_0\times H}:\xi(0,A_0)=1\}$. The character sum at $y$ is denoted by $G_\xi(y)$. Fix the character subset $K'$ and the real parameters $c,M,K_o,C_E$. Define $v_\xi=\mathrm{wt}_c(\xi)\Re G_\xi(x)$ for $\xi\in K'$, and $v_\xi=-|G_\xi(x)|$ for $\xi\in\mathcal A\setminus K'$. Here $\mathrm{wt}_c$ is $1$ on the characters trivial on $H$, and $c$ on the other characters. Thus $G:=\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal A}v_\xi=G_m^A(x)-\Sigma_A(x)$ by splitting the finite sum (D565 in B09). Quantified estimate. For arbitrary real $C,\varepsilon,C_E,K_o,c,M$ with $C\ge0$, $\varepsilon>0$ and $|c|\le1$, there exists $x_1$ so that for every $x\ge x_1$ and every eligible finite abelian group, arithmetic datum, column family, subgroup and character subset, the following implication holds. Assume HostFactsS, $b>0$, $a_E\ge0$, $k>0$, $m\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon/2}$, $xm\le k$, SizesA, ThinScales, CostWindows, RestSize, $T_2\subseteq A_0$, CharsA and the gap bound on every element of $A_0$. Then $G_m^A(x)\le x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}+\Sigma_A(x)$. CharsA supplies the kept subset, the counts, the shares and the character bounds. The increment estimate for $G_\xi$ and the estimate for changing a positive width are supplied by D562, respectively A4 and A9. B08 specializes these abstract constants to a level and host. Conversion to a character lower bound, D564–D565

Use $y_t=a_t/b$, so $|y_t-x|\le1$, and take $x\ge3$. For nonzero $\xi$, $G_\xi(1)=\ell(\xi)$, whence the increment estimate gives $|G_\xi(x)|\le x(\log x+C_d+|\ell(\xi)|)$. For nonzero kept $\xi$, first replace $G_\xi(y_t)$ by $G_\xi(x)$ under $\sum_t\xi(e_1(t))$. The cost per term is at most $\log(x+1)+C_d+K_o$. Next replace the sum of its coefficients by $n_T\mathrm{wt}_c(\xi)$: the values at $e_1(t)$ and $(0,t)$ agree, and the total error is bounded by $n_T M x^{-1+\varepsilon}x(\log x+C_d+K_o)$. For $\xi$ trivial on $H$ this latter error is zero. For the principal frequency the entire cost per term is $C_d$. For each discarded frequency use $\Re(\xi(e_1(t))G_\xi(y_t))\ge-|G_\xi(y_t)|$ and the increment bound to get a lower bound $v_\xi-(\log(x+1)+C_d+|C_E|\log x)$. These are exactly the three cases of D564.

For a threshold independent of the host, let $s=x^{1/4}$, $e=x^\varepsilon$ and

$$ A_1=5+C_d+|K_o|+|M|(4+C_d+|K_o|)+4|C_E|.$$

Since $\log x\le4s$, $\log(x+1)\le5s$ and $1\le s,e$, each of the three per-character costs per $t$ is at most $A_1 s e$ (the coefficient error uses $x^{-1+\varepsilon}x=e$). Increase the threshold until $|M|A_1\le s$. Summing over the band costs at most $n_Ts^2e$, so the factor four in the following bound is slack: the cost is at most $4n_Tx^{1/2+\varepsilon}$. Taking real parts gives

$$ n_T(G-4x^{1/2+\varepsilon}) \le \sum_{t\in TT}\Re\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal A} \xi(e_1(t))G_\xi(y_t).$$

Finite-group average and B09

Put $f=|\Phi|=2n$. For each $u\in A_0$ compare the frozen sum $T(u,t)$ of Lemma 3.9 with one column of $\mathrm{De}$. At a thick place $b\le w\le b+\eta+1$ and $a_t\le(x+1)b$, so replacing its weighted value by the frozen one costs at most $3(x+1)(\eta+1)$. Indeed A9 gives $|wG_\xi(A/w)-bG_\xi(A/b)|\le 3A|w-b|/\min(w,b)$, so take $A=a_t$ and use $w\ge b>0$. See lipschitz_weight_all at LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:957–964 and th_le at ChCore.lean:117–123,307–309. At a thin place $0<w\le2b'$. Since $a_t\ge(x-1)b$, $b'X'\le xb$ and $x\ge3$, one has $a_t/w\ge X'/3$. Thinness therefore bounds $w|G_\xi(a_t/w)|$ by $a_t\theta\le (x+1)b\theta$. There are at most $f$ places and $2f$ characters, each with unit-modulus Fourier coefficient and normalization $1/f$. The sum of absolute errors is at most

$$ 2f\big[3(x+1)(\eta+1)+(x+1)b\theta\big]\le 2b/x$$

by the two cost inequalities in B09. Since $|a_t/b-x|\le1$, $x\ge3$ and $b>0$, we have $a_t>0$. The cutoff $a_t/w\le N$ on $\Phi$ comes from SizesA. These are the inputs to ch_balance used at ChCore.lean:292–295,309–310. The balance identity now gives $\Re T(u,t)\le\mathrm{ASK}_{t,j}-\Re\mathrm{MAIN}_{t,j}+2b/x$ for every column $j$. Thus if $\mathrm{gap}(u)\le E$ on $A_0$, then $\sum_{t,j}\Re T(u,t)\le E+2n_Tmb/x$ on $A_0$. CharacterBalanceMoves.Th, th_le, LandauTheorems/ChCore.lean:16–20,75–152; CharacterBalanceMoves.core, LandauTheorems/ChCore.lean:283–320.

For $s\in T_2\subset A_0$, sum $\xi(-e_1(s+u-t))$ over $u\in A_0$. Its value is $|A_0|\xi(e_1(t))$ if $\xi\in\mathcal A$, since $-e_1(s+u-t)=e_1(t)+(0,-s)+(0,-u)$. Otherwise choose $u'\in A_0$ with $\xi(0,u')\ne1$; translating the sum by $u'$ multiplies it by $\xi(0,-u')$, hence the sum vanishes. Consequently the average of $T(u,t)$ over $A_0$ equals $b(\delta_2/2)\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal A}\xi(e_1(t))G_\xi(y_t)$: the factor is $bn_2/f$. Sum over all $t$ and the $m$ columns and apply the preceding character lower bound. Since $|A_0|>0$, the result is

$$ n_T m b(\delta_2/2)(G-4x^{1/2+\varepsilon})\le E+2n_Tmb/x .$$

With $E=x^{C\varepsilon}B$, $C\ge0$, the size-window calculation in Lemma 3.10 now gives $G\le x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}$. Its hypotheses are precisely RestSize, the upper rank and lower $k$ inequalities, $x\ge3$, $26\le x^{3\varepsilon/8}$, and the sign and cardinality conditions (D563, D566). Adding $\Sigma_A$ proves B09. The threshold precedes the choice of $H,TT,T_2$, the weights, and $m$. The four terms after division by the number of columns

Here is the numerical implication used in B09. Write $n=|H|$, $t=|TT|$, $r=|T_2|$, $\delta=r/n$, $Q=tmb$, and $G=G_m^A-\Sigma_A$. Put $X=x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$, $Y=x^{1/2+13\varepsilon/8}$ and $P=x^{C\varepsilon}$. The bracket before normalization is

$$ \mathrm{Br}=t a_E(1+m^2/k) +m\left(2nmbx^\varepsilon+ \frac{r k b x^\varepsilon}{n^2 x^{-\varepsilon}}\right).$$

Here $r=|T_2|$ is a cardinality local to this calculation. The size record in D566 gives, with $x\ge3$, $\varepsilon>0$, $C\ge0$, $b>0$ and $n>0$,

$$\begin{gathered} 0\le a_E\le2xb,\qquad n/t\le 2x^{\varepsilon/8},\qquad x^{-\varepsilon}\le\delta\le1,\\ x^{1/2-\varepsilon}\le m\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon/2},\qquad xm\le k\le n^2 x^{1/2-\varepsilon}. \end{gathered}$$

In particular $t,m,k,Q$ are positive. More precisely the original inequality $t\ge n x^{-\varepsilon/8}/2$ gives positivity of $t$ before taking reciprocals. Assume $26\le x^{3\varepsilon/8}$, as is possible for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$. See SizeWindows, GrowthBoundGivesRiemannHypothesis.lean:109–123, and size_windows_split, ChainSlopeWindows/PChain.lean:23–26.

Size bound, D563. If $\mathrm{Err}\le P\,\mathrm{Br}$ for the source bracket and the character average gives

$$Q\,\frac{\delta}{2}(G-4X)\le\mathrm{Err}+2Q/x,$$

then $G\le x^{1/2+(C+3)\varepsilon}$.

Proof. Expand $\mathrm{Br}$ and divide its four summands by $Q$. For the first, the lower rank bound gives $x/m\le X$; for the third the upper rank bound controls the extra factor of $m$:

$$\begin{aligned} \frac{a_E}{mb}&\le2x/m\le2Y,\\ \frac{a_E m}{bk}&\le2xm/k\le2Y,\\ 2(n/t)m x^\varepsilon &\le4x^{\varepsilon/8+1/2+\varepsilon/2+\varepsilon}=4Y,\\ \frac{r k}{n^2t}x^{2\varepsilon} &\le(n/t)x^{1/2+\varepsilon}\le2x^{1/2+9\varepsilon/8}\le2Y . \end{aligned}$$

These are precisely the terms of errorBracketSizeWindows (ErrorFormsWeakWindowsFalse.lean:133–136); the four estimates occur as SZ.T1–SZ.T4 in LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:31–113. Consequently $\mathrm{Err}/Q\le 10PY$. Since $2/x\le1\le PY$, division by $Q$ gives $\delta(G-4X)\le22PY$. Divide by $\delta>0$ and use $\delta^{-1}\le x^\varepsilon$. Both $X$ and $PYx^\varepsilon$ are at most $Z=x^{1/2+(C+21/8)\varepsilon}$ (the second equals $Z$), so $G\le4X+22PYx^\varepsilon\le26Z$. Finally $26Z\le x^{3\varepsilon/8}Z$ is the asserted bound. See proposition_size_bound, LipschitzWeightAndMinimum.lean:507–592.

Scope for review

The factor $b\delta_2/2$ comes from $bn_2/(2n)$ after averaging a normalized sum, before the column count. Both the cancellation of nontrivial frequencies and the common, host-independent threshold are needed.
