\begin{formalstatement}[A uniform second-cohomology bound]\label{sub:W05}
Assume the rank-$n$ literature record. There is a real $C_K$ such that,
for every prime $\ell$, totally complex field $L$ containing a primitive
$\ell$th root (a fourth root for $\ell=2$), algebraic closure and
local data, and $e\ge1$, the group $H^2(G_{L,\ell},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1))$
is finite. With $r=[L:\mathbb Q]$ and $D=|\operatorname{disc}(L)|$,
\[
 \log\# H^2(G_{L,\ell},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1))
 \le \exp\big((2er)^{C_K}\log(2D)\big).
\]
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{W05}
Soulé's Theorem~3 bounds the product for $p\ge4(e+1)-1$ and $p\ge r+2$.
For odd $\ell$, $\mathbb Q(\mu_\ell)\subseteq L$ gives $\ell\le r+1$.
Thus the needed prime falls outside that product, as does $2$.
Proposition~4(i) also has different discriminant dependence.
