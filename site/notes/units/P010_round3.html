P010 · Supplying the polarized row to the every-character theorem

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

pending_review · Reading outline against Lean commit 40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005. Unit card

Read the quantifiers before supplying the row

The conclusion is the universal half-plane assertion $H$ used by P009. P010 obtains it by applying the row-to-$H$ implication P011 to an inhabitant of its row hypothesis. This is the exact application in UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean:10–11). Paper v3, §6.10, p. 56 identifies that supplier. Its type and especially its compatibility across places explain why this specialization has mathematical content upstream. Manuscript excerpt

Fix a CM number field $E$, an algebraic closure, a coefficient prime $\ell$ and an identification of its algebraic coefficient field with $\mathbb C$. Fix a positive rank $a$, a cuspidal datum $\pi$ and a character over the maximal real subfield. Assume the regular algebraic and polarized-pair conditions. The required row provides a Galois representation of rank $a$, an integer weight and a family of multisets of Hodge–Tate weights indexed by embeddings of $E$. The output includes one proof of the compatibility record for these choices. The order of the universal inputs and the joint existence clause are in UpperTheory.GalStar.Row0b (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean:69–73).

That record requires continuity and semisimplicity. Away from $\ell$, for every parameter assignment satisfying IsRecT, the two parameters $x,y$ have equal dimensions. They agree when $y$ is toral with zero monodromy. If the completion is continuously isomorphic to $\mathbf Q_g$ with $n=1$ or $16n^2<g$, and $y$ is tame, squarefree and has zero monodromy, then $x$ has zero monodromy and the characteristic polynomials agree on degree-zero Weil elements. Outside good classes the parameter assignment in B04 has extra freedom. It specifies the weights and their transformation under complex conjugation. Its last field gives the indicated conditions above $\ell$ when the requisite local bridge and tied data are present. Quantifying independently over places after choosing a single representation is essential. Independent choices of representations at the different places would not fill the record. The six fields are in UpperTheory.GalStar.PropsB (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean:54–67). The particular parameter relation is the one named in UpperTheory.Gal.Int.recT (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/Development.lean:18–18).

Follow the geometric supplier

The term passed to P011 is UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/RowSrc.lean:6–6). It calls the theorem turning a theta-lift input into the row and supplies the named theta-lift result UpperTheory.TP.K.tLiftK (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TLiftK.lean:4–5). The conversion UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisRepresentations_of_thetaLift (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GaloisRepresentationsOfThetaLift.lean:6–6) also calls the geometric existence result for the prescribed geometric input. Thus a referee checking existence at this point needs to inspect both sources. An asserted local compatibility on its own cannot create the global representation.

Follow the conversion one level further. Given the geometric and theta-lift inputs, UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobal_of_geometry_lift (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/Development.lean:27–28) builds a seed for the odd-level argument from the geometry and obtains a congruence input from the weight result. It passes these with the lift to UpperTheory.Gal.Int.coreAll_of (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/Development.lean:20–22). The latter brings together the descent, closed-trace and limiting steps to build a common core record. The next map UpperTheory.Gal.Int.rest (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/Development.lean:24–25) uses this core for the underlying row and for the spherical and Iwahori compatibility statements. The code explicitly derives the Iwahori input from the spherical compatibility on the same core. This is where an attempted derivation from independently chosen local data would have to supply an identification.

Weaken the row and discharge the implication

What these steps initially produce is the stronger row Row0. The weaker version needed at P011 is obtained by UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_of_geometry_lift (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/Development.lean:30–31). Unfold UpperTheory.GalStar.Row0.row0b (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean:84–86). For each choice of $E,\ell,a,\pi$ and polarization, extract $r$, its weight and its embedding-indexed multisets from the strong row. Return the same three witnesses. All six fields persist. In the tame away test and in the nice test above the coefficient prime, the premise IsQp is restricted to IsQpBig. No fresh representation is chosen. The result therefore has exactly the parameter dependence required above.

Finally specialize UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter_of_row (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean:7–8) to this supplied inhabitant. Its output has the same predicate $H$ as P010, so the last step is function application. This outline follows the construction down to its named geometric, theta and local-global inputs. It provides no independent proof of those existence and comparison results, or of the Selmer-window construction used inside P011.

First point for review. The source obtains one representation with the entire local compatibility record. Independently justifying this simultaneous existence from the geometric and theta-lift inputs is the first substantive gap in this outline.

Round 4 source comparison

The Lean head has no premises. The development supplies the row using the geometric realization geoOne_holds and theta lift tLift_holds_k. The proof calls wtLacEx_holds for the weight input and uses congPhi_holds, descOffT and limFinOdd_of to construct the common core. These are in-repository declarations. The restricted local comparison above is the project's variant; equivalence with a published local–global compatibility theorem has not been established here.
