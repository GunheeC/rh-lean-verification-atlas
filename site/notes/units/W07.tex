\begin{formalstatement}[Vanishing for the two rank-two twists]\label{sub:W07}
Assume $H'$. Fix an odd prime $\ell$, $e\ge1$ with $\ell-1\nmid2e$,
an imaginary quadratic field $K$, an even positive level $N$ coprime to
$D_K=|\operatorname{disc}K|$, with $\ell\nmid D_K h_K$, and an
algebraic closure $\Omega/\mathbb Q$ containing $K$. Write
$\kappa$ for the cyclotomic character and $\theta$ for the quadratic
character cutting out $K$. Take $\psi$ and $W:G_{\mathbb Q}\to
\mathrm{GL}_2(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ satisfying the source
conditions \code{Used}. Explicitly, $\psi$ factors through level $N$,
has prime-to-$\ell$ order, has square one on inertia at $\ell$, and
$\kappa^e\psi$ is even. The representation $W$ has level datum
$(ND_K;e,-e)$, the ordinary polynomial condition for $(e,-e)$, and
determinant $\theta$. On inertia at $\ell$ its trace is
$a+a^{-1}$ for $a=\kappa^e\psi$. Globally the residual trace is
$\theta a+a^{-1}$. At a chosen place above $\ell$, inertia lies
in $\ker\theta$ and the prescribed flag characters restrict to
$\kappa^{\pm e}\chi$ for a single character through level $\ell$.
The restriction to $G_{K(\mu_{\ell^\infty})}$ is irreducible.
Suppose a weight-$2e+1$ newform realizes $W\otimes\kappa^{-e}$
via $\iota$, and on every quadratic CM restriction on which $W$ is
irreducible it has the witness \code{WitA}: local unlinked flags
above $\ell$ become power-unlinked, and each attached regular
algebraic cusp form has a common real parameter for its local window
away from $\ell$ and at the power-unlinked places. For both $W$ and $W\otimes\theta$, every cocycle satisfying the
Bloch--Kato local conditions is a coboundary.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{W07}
Kato's Theorem~14.2(1) gives finiteness for lattices attached to a cusp
form at noncentral critical twists over finite abelian extensions.
The comparison here identifies the two normalized representations and
their local finite conditions in the specified cocycle model.
The unramified zeta input is \nodeid{P026}.
