\begin{formalstatement}[Bounded class-group ranks in cyclotomic layers]\label{sub:W11}
Let $\ell$ be any prime and $K/\mathbb Q$ a finite abelian extension
containing $\mu_\ell$ for odd $\ell$, or $\mu_4$ for $\ell=2$. There
is an integer $r\ge0$ such that for every $n\ge0$ and every model
$K'$ of $K(\mu_{\ell^n})$ over $K$,
\[
    \big|\operatorname{Cl}(K')/\operatorname{Cl}(K')^\ell\big|\le\ell^r .
\]
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{W11}
Ferrero and Washington give $\mu=0$ for the cyclotomic
$\mathbb Z_\ell$-extension of an abelian field. Washington's
Theorem~7.15 states this for every prime and Proposition~13.23 identifies
$\mu=0$ with bounded $\ell$-ranks of the layer class groups. The
roots-of-unity hypothesis places each $K'$ in this tower, and the
dimension of the displayed quotient over $\mathbb F_\ell$ is exactly
that rank. The closed term \nolinkurl{UpperTheory.V.FW.ferreroWashington}
constructs a single bound from its embedding, cyclotomic-layer and descent
estimates, then applies it to each $n$ and $K'$.
