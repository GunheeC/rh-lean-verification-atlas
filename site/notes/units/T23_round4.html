Outlined · pending_review. For a nef multidegree, the ranks of the normed chart lattices have a leading term determined by the generic degree. The argument sandwiches those ranks between ordinary section ranks with two fixed twists, preserving the particular chart lattice needed for the volume comparison.

Claim and hypotheses

Let $y$ be a birational multihomogeneous presentation in a characteristic-zero field with arithmetic variety $P$ of dimension $D+1$, and let the integral multidegree $a$ be nef on the generic fibre. Put $V_n=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}(\mathrm{chartLatNm}(y,a,n))$. Each $V_n$ is finite-dimensional and

$$ \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{D!\,\dim_{\mathbb Q}V_n}{n^D} =\mathrm{genDeg}(P,D,a).$$

Identify the normalized sequence

Fix a finite multihomogeneous coordinate presentation $y$ over a characteristic-zero field, a birationality witness and the nef multidegree $a$. Write $V_\tau$ for the rational span of its normed chart lattice. The claim gives finite dimension for every $\tau$ and the indicated real limit after multiplying by $D!$ and dividing by $\tau^D$. This is a rank asymptotic for fixed data. The degree-zero-dimensional case is dispatched by a separate model lemma before any positive-dimensional estimates are invoked. Inject the normed chart into an ordinary chart with fixed twist

When $D>0$ the twist-shift theorem returns a multidegree $e$ and a nonzero scalar $c$. Multiplication by $c$ is an injective rational linear map on the field. Its image on $V_\tau$ lies in the ordinary chart space in multidegree $\tau a+e$. This comparison is simultaneous over powers, so the same twist is available for the asymptotic theorem. The ordinary chart space is finite-dimensional. Its subspace containing the injected image therefore proves that $V_\tau$ is finite-dimensional, via its isomorphism to that image. Bound the rank in both directions

For the upper bound use invariance of rank under this injection onto its image, followed by monotonicity of finite-dimensional rank under inclusion. For the lower bound no multiplication is needed. The ordinary chart at the unshifted multidegree $\tau a$ is a subspace of the rational span of the normed chart. Rank monotonicity in this other inclusion gives the opposite inequality. The lower and upper spaces thus use the twists zero and $e$, respectively. Their comparison does not identify them with one another at individual degrees. Squeeze only after normalization

For a prescribed positive tolerance use the nef sections lower estimate with twist zero and the chart dimension upper estimate with twist $e$. Each eventually bounds the appropriate endpoint in the rank sandwich. Transitivity gives both eventual bounds for the real rank of $V_\tau$ itself, and the asymptotic-bounds lemma yields the limit with the required normalization. The rank comparisons and the squeeze are elementary parts of the deduction. The source of the nef lower estimate, the fixed-twist upper estimate and the dimension-zero model remain separate inputs to this outline. PositivityTheory.Tls2.Nef.nefRR · NefRR.lean:45–52
