The receiving theorem P012 expects a three-field record and a Selmer window. Fill the middle field

The middle field is supplied by B04. Start with the local data, its place and coefficient conventions. The construction chooses the local class-field input and a cuspidal parameter. A local extension theorem then produces an assignment on all irreducible representations, together with its restriction on the cuspidal ones. Dimension and twist identities are obtained for that same extension. The remaining fields assert the required compatibilities, including the central character, duals, injectivity, depth and the specified type condition. The type condition itself is discharged after restricting to the indicated cuspidal case. The choices and the final record, including its side conditions, are in UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.recTExists (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/RecTExists.lean:20–66).

Thus $\langle h,\mathrm{recTExists},h\rangle$ provides precisely the argument required by the first binder of P012. Local existence is a substantial input to this step. The mere repeated occurrence of $h$ supplies neither the extension of local parameters nor its compatibility fields.

Produce the right kind of window

The second argument is P013, used as UpperTheory.One.vwin_of_row (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/VwinOfRow.lean:4–5). It constructs the open-image-condition record from $h$ and supplies the named base-change result together with $h$ to UpperTheory.One.Join.automorphic_window_of_inputs (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/TopMainZj.lean:71–74). That theorem supplies the CM abelian inputs to the window calculation. The output is a statement quantified over every primitive character $\psi$, with exponent $401$, as fixed by UpperTheory.Top.VWin (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:49–49).

To see why a single bound at one field or layer would not fill this argument, first fix the admissible $\varepsilon$ and a layer selector satisfying the page inequalities. The assertion chooses $J\ge1$ and a threshold. For every sufficiently large $X$ and every admissible pair at scale $X^{1/J}$ it must then return a lower endpoint $Y\ge X^{1/J}$ with $Y^2\le X$. On the entire interval $[Y,Y^2]$ the bound must hold at the preselected layers. This order of choices is UpperTheory.Top.VWinAt (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:44–47). At a point $x$ it applies to each field realizing the specified character and layer, by UpperTheory.Top.VAt (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:41–42). The bound itself includes finiteness of the localization-kernel lengths at every prime and an inequality for the weighted sum over every finite set of primes and all indices from one up to the cutoff. Both clauses appear in UpperTheory.Top.VVle (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean:12–17). Obtaining them uniformly is the large mathematical obligation inside P013.

Apply the two-input theorem

Feed the completed record and this single window proof to UpperTheory.LiteratureTheorems.every_character_of_literature_of_vWin (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/EveryCharacterOfLiteratureOfVWin.lean:810–813). There the record supplies eigenvalue and theta data, compatibility of the integral and its local evaluations, the covolume input, and the local parameter. The Selmer window is passed on explicitly in the final line. The result is the common half-plane statement $H$ of UpperTheory.EveryCharacter (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/EveryCharacter.lean:13–14).

This establishes the order of application and identifies the arithmetic and geometric suppliers a referee must open next. In particular the row assumption on P011 should not be strengthened silently by adding a separate uniform-window hypothesis to its public statement. Rather, the source claims to derive that full window from the row through P013. An evaluation of that claim needs the underlying counting estimates. P012 · P013

First point for review. Check that the row simultaneously supports both fields in the literature record and the uniform field-and-layer quantifiers of VWin. The code gives the window supplier, while the stronger arithmetic estimates remain separate reading obligations.
