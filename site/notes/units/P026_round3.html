P026 · One integral Euler-system cocycle with a local obstruction

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Outlined · pending_review. The conclusion selects one coefficient ring $O$, one integral two-coordinate cocycle $y$ and one exceptional prime set $S'$. Its global requirements concern integrality, inertia away from $\ell$ and tame norm relations at every allowed level. Its local requirement says that there is no scalar $a$ with $y_2(s)=(W_{22}(s)-1)a$ on the whole decomposition group at the chosen place $A$. The same $y$ occurs in all these assertions. Fix the modular source and the ordinary parameters

Let $\ell$ be odd, $e\ge1$, $W$ the admitted integral rank-two representation of the absolute Galois group of $\mathbb Q$, and $A$ the prescribed place over $\ell$. The ordinary normal form includes the ramification conditions away from $S$, the quadratic odd determinant, irreducibility on the intersection of the character kernels, and an upper-triangular restriction. For some integer $m\ge1$, $W_{11}(s)^m=\varepsilon(s)^{em}$ for every inertia element; for some decomposition-group element $s$, $W_{11}(s)\ne\varepsilon(s)$. The source is a weight-$2e+1$ newform of level prime to $\ell$ with unit $a_\ell$. After the indicated cyclotomic, determinant and character twists (the auxiliary character satisfies KillsMu ℓ χ) it realizes $W$. The second diagonal entry satisfies the stated identity on inertia. These conditions are used by both producers at the same newform, place and embedding. Test that very tuple locally

The local producer is universal in the tuple. It assumes Sys, IwSys2, SpLink and Pin7 and finds an inertia element $s$ with trivial cyclotomic character, $W_{22}(s)=1$ and $y_2(s)\ne0$. To see where the nonvanishing enters, suppose no such element exists. On cyclotomically trivial inertia the ordinary-character input gives $W_{22}(s)=1$, hence the failure of the proposed witness forces $y_2$ to vanish there. Specialization then implies the eventual zero congruences at every required precision for the sums of $Z_r$.
