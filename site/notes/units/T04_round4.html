Statement and hypotheses

Assume global Artin reciprocity, the convergent unitary Siegel--Weil formula, the discrete-series cover, Harish--Chandra modules, rapid decay, the Casselman--Wallach statement, discrete-series classification, and both estimates of T05. For any proof of RecTExists, norm-choice extension, series extension, geometry and family predicate $N$, let $p$ be their extension parameters. Suppose T08's open conditions and the linked covolume, functional and $N$-norm conditions hold at $p$. Then all 26 large-slot row conditions hold for its built and good slots and its bundle linkage.
