T16 · The Chow height of an image model

Outlined · pending_review. For an integral image model with its specified sections and conjugation-compatible metrics, one controls a shifted self-intersection by the image degree and image height. This turns arithmetic information on the model into a bound expressed in the coordinates of the image.

Claim and hypotheses

Assume the Hodge-bundle record. Let $K$ be a number field with no real embeddings, $V$ a $K$-module compatibly acted on by $\mathcal O_K$, and $\Omega/K$ a field. Let $N_\sigma$ on $V$ have Gram data and be invariant under conjugating $\sigma$. Let $W\subset V$ be a finitely generated $\mathcal O_K$-module spanning $V$ over $K$, and $\mathrm{ev}:V\to\Omega$ linear with generating ratios. For degree index $D\ge0$ suppose $d=\mathrm{imageDeg}(\mathrm{ev},D)>0$. Let $(X,L)$ be its integral image model over $\mathcal O_K$ with the specified embedding $\Omega\to K(X)$ and sections indexed by $W$ for $N_\sigma,W,\mathrm{ev},D$. If the constant metric shift $L(t)$ has self-intersection $a$, then

$$ a\le [K:\mathbb Q]\,(D+1)d\, \bigl(\mathrm{imageHeight}(N,W,\mathrm{ev},D)+t\bigr).$$

What the bound measures

Fix the shift $t$ before making comparisons. The target keeps the same evaluation map, positive image degree and finitely generated spanning lattice all the way to the image height. In particular the multiplier is the absolute degree times $D+1$ times the image degree. The witness $a$ is a self-intersection for this shift on the supplied model.
