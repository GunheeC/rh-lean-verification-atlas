\begin{formalstatement}[Arithmetic inputs for the covolume bound]\label{sub:T13}

Assume resolution, principalisation, integrated Poincaré--Lelong, the three
Hilbert--Samuel statements, the arithmetic bigness bound, Hodge--Tate
orthogonality, the Zarhin splitting and nef Riemann--Roch (T17--T24).
Assume also integral Siegel toroidal models, the toroidal-fan input for KS,
seminegativity of the semiabelian Lie bundle, separation of pairs of
points, arithmetic degree, the 28 Hodge-bundle inputs, and Grothendieck
deformation. There exists $\varepsilon_1>0$ such that for each
$0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_1$ there is a largeness parameter $K$.
For every family $F$ at $\varepsilon$ and real $H$ with
$\mathrm{FamHyp}(F)$ and $F.\mathrm{Large}(K,H)$, there are
$C_0\ge0$ and $x_0$ such that, for $x>x_1$ with $x\ge x_0$, every family
index and point $p$ with the point conditions and reciprocity tie (\nolinkurl{RowsTAlt}), every
$C\ge C_0$ and every $j$, the false-side row satisfies
\[
   \operatorname{lcv}(XW_j,M_j)/D\ \le\ N(C)_{L3,j}.
\]
The normalization $D$, row space and lattice in this expression belong to $p$.
