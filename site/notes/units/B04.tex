\begin{formalstatement}[A local parameter with the tame type law]\label{sub:B04}
For any number field $E$, algebraic closure $\Omega$, valuation subring
of $\Omega$ over a finite place $w$, and resulting local field $E_w$,
there exist an Artin map and a parameter map from irreducible smooth
$\mathrm{GL}_n(E_w)$-classes of all ranks to Weil--Deligne parameters.
The map preserves dimension, agrees with reciprocity in rank one,
takes Langlands quotients of multisegments to the corresponding segment
sums, takes cuspidals to irreducible parameters and commutes with real
norm twists. The absolute value of its determinant recovers that of the
central character. For classes with good cuspidal support it respects
character twists, the full central character and duality, is injective
against arbitrary classes, and yields a tame parameter in the presence
of principal-congruence fixed vectors. Here each good cuspidal constituent
has rank one or, over a field isomorphic to $\mathbb Q_p$, is a twist of
a representation with such fixed vectors.

The last law prescribes a tame type. Over the indicated unramified residue
extension of degree $n$, let $\vartheta$ be a regular residue-field character
and let the inflated cuspidal type have the prescribed elliptic character
values. The parameter has zero monodromy. At an inertia element whose Artin
image reduces to $z$, its inertial characteristic polynomial is
$\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(X-\vartheta(z^{p^j}))$, with restriction to the given
base as in the local law.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{B04}
