\begin{formalstatement}[Weight-one geometric realisation in odd rank]\label{sub:B02}
Fix a CM field and coefficient data as above, odd rank $a$, admissible field
datum $d$ and $x\in E$ with $x^2=-d$. Fix the nonsplit exceptional set $T$,
separated places $w_0,v'\mid\ell$ agreeing on integral elements of
$\mathbb Q(x)$, and a finite set $S$. Let $U$ satisfy the automorphic
condition off $T$, the specified $\ell$-type at $w_0$, the spherical condition
off $w_0,S$, and the Satake-existence and lacunary-weight conditions at $v'$.
Then there exists a continuous semisimple $\rho$ of some dimension with
the source's Satake polynomial condition off a finite set of admissible
places away from $\ell$, and a Hodge-jump witness containing $a$ lacunary
integers. Call these common properties $V(\rho)$.
At each such admissible $w\nmid\ell$ with Satake data one can insist on
$V(\rho)$ and the polynomial condition at $w$ for every Satake multiset
there. At each $w\mid\ell$ agreeing with $v'$ on $\mathbb Q(x)$, separated
from $w_0,v'$ and having Satake data, the following two witnesses exist:
one has $V(\rho)$, crystalline comparison and the prescribed weight jumps
among its jumps at $w$; another has $V(\rho)$ and, for each Satake datum
and inducing bridge, crystalline comparison and a degree $-1$ Weil element
whose characteristic polynomial divides a power of the Satake polynomial.
The dimension and representation may vary with the demanded witness.
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{B02}
