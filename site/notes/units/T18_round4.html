T18 · Three arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel consequences

Outlined · pending_review. Three consequences of arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel theory enter the deep estimate. The first gives the sign of a top intersection. The other two bound small-section growth from above on two specified chart lattices. Their hypotheses and their lattices must be kept separate in applications.

Claim and hypotheses

For each intersection system and finite multihomogeneous presentation as above the following three statements hold. (i) On an arithmetic variety of dimension $D+1$, relative semipositivity, fibre nefness, conjugation invariance and nonnegative degree on every arithmetic curve imply $I_P(A^{D+1})\ge0$. (ii) For a birational characteristic-zero presentation with $D\ge1$, small-generation data and conjugation invariance, the limsup of the small-section count for chartLat, divided in degree $n$ by $n^{D+1}/(D+1)!$, is at most $I_P(A^{D+1})$. (iii) For a birational presentation over a field with $D\ge1$, nonnegative multidegree, relative semipositivity at $P_0\subseteq P$, bounded monomials and conjugation invariance, the same limsup bound holds for chartLatNm.
