\begin{formalstatement}[The individual bounds in Cases II and III]\label{sub:W09}
Assume $H'$, W05 and the rank-four conclusions of W06. Each of the
following two cases has its own $C\in\mathbb N$ depending only on
$\chi$. For every large host, $0<e<t$, odd $\ell$, closure, coefficient
ring and $\psi\in X_h(O,e)$ in the case, the length is finite and
$v_\ell(\psi,e)\log\ell\le(\ell Ne)^C$:
\begin{itemize}
\item Case II requires $e\ge2$, even order of $\chi_P$, and either
$\ell\in B_\chi$ or $\ell-1\mid2e$.
\item Case III requires $\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\mid2e$ and
$\psi$ nontrivial of odd order.
\end{itemize}
\end{formalstatement}\subnode{W09}
