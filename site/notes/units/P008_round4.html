Specializing the half-plane input to the character of modulus one gives right-half-plane nonvanishing for zeta away from one. A separate reflection argument turns that into the zeta predicate consumed by the public exports. rhOfAll (MainTheorems.lean:9–9).

Specialize the disjunction correctly

Write $h$ for P009. With modulus one and character one the disjunction in $h$ must use the argument different from one. Thus for every $t$ with $\Re t>1/2$ and $t\ne1$ the ordinary zeta value at $t$ is nonzero. The rewrite uses the modulus-one identity for the L-function. This single specialization supplies the function needed in the reflection lemma. The potentially much larger obligation of supplying $h$ remains at its own unit. rhOfAll (MainTheorems.lean:9–9). Formalism.VW.rh_of_every (CritLine.lean:29–32).

Locate the forbidden factors

Given a nontrivial zero with $\Re s<1/2$, write $t=1-s$. Positivity of $\Re t$ rules out all the nonpositive integers at $t$ and gives a nonzero gamma factor. Also $\zeta(0)$ rules out $s=0$, so $t\ne1$. The functional equation now writes the alleged zero as a product involving $\zeta(t)$, the gamma factor, a complex power with nonzero base, and $\cos(\pi t/2)$. Each factor needs a reason for nonvanishing. In particular, a cosine zero would make $t$ an odd integer, so $s$ would be an even integer. In the present range a negative even value is explicitly excluded and zero has already been treated. Formalism.VW.rh_of_right (RhOfRight.lean:5–62).

Return the predicate expected at the public interface

On the right of one half the specialized nonvanishing contradicts the proposed zero immediately; equality is the conclusion in the middle case. The resulting term has the Mathlib predicate as its type. Both P001 and P002 name it as their supplier and the latter spells the excluded values out explicitly. These source uses explain why a check of this theorem should combine a check of the analytic side conditions with an identification of the public predicate. Formalism.VW.rh_of_right (RhOfRight.lean:5–62). riemannHypothesis (Main.lean:4–4). riemannZeta_eq_zero_re (Main.lean:6–6).
