\begin{formalstatement}[Two uniform bounds on indefinite rows]\label{sub:T05}

For every $P$, both of the following assertions hold on the admissible
families. First, for a family with \nolinkurl{DIVinN}, some $C_0\ge0$ works
eventually at points $p$ satisfying \nolinkurl{More}: for $C\ge C_0$ the
false-side row satisfies \nolinkurl{ON}. This gives the upper bound on the
sum of the two conjugate-place indices for every twisted place, nonnegative
row errors there, and the summed weighted-error bound $E_{1,j}$ for each $j$.
Second, eventually at points and for all $C$, the true-side row satisfies
$N_{L6,j}\le\log\lambda_j(\mu_0,p,v)$ for every $j,p,v$ in the row datum.

\end{formalstatement}\subnode{T05}
