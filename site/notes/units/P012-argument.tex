\subsection{P012: the covolume bound and a common definite-side witness}
\label{sec:r23-p012}

The Selmer window and the definite-side witness are separate inputs to
\nolinkurl{every_character_of_theta_integrals_local_matching}
(\path{SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean:69--73}).
We trace the latter input backwards through two implications. The statements
refer to the individual field specifications in \hyperref[sub:T13]{T13}
and \hyperref[sub:T02]{T02}.

\begin{formalstatement}[The closed covolume bound, \decid{T13}]
Assume the following fifteen inputs in their source formulations:
resolution and principalisation in characteristic zero, invariant
Poincaré--Lelong, Hilbert--Samuel (\nolinkurl{HSUse}), Moriwaki bigness,
Fargues exactness, Zarhin's statement, nef Riemann--Roch, integral
Siegel toroidal models, toroidal models for the fan and KS,
seminegativity of the semiabelian Lie bundle, point separation for a
pair, arithmetic degree, the published Hodge-bundle record, and
Grothendieck deformation. There is $\varepsilon_1>0$ such that for
each $0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_1$ there is a largeness parameter $K$
with the following property. For every $\varepsilon$-family $F$ and
$H\in\mathbb R$ satisfying $\mathrm{FamHyp}(F)$ and
$F.\mathrm{Large}(K,H)$ there exist $C_0\ge0$ and $x_0\in\mathbb R$.
For every $x>x_1(F)$ with $x\ge x_0$, family index $\iota$, and point
$p$ over $(x,\iota)$ satisfying \nolinkurl{RowsTAlt}, set $S=p.S(\mathrm{false})$.
For all $C\ge C_0$ and all column indices $j$,
\[
   \frac{\operatorname{lcv}(S.XW_j,S.M_j)}{S.D}
      \le (p.N(C,H)).\mathrm{l3}_j .
\]
\end{formalstatement}
\noindent\nolinkurl{SelfIntersectionVolumeEstimates.topCovolSlim2_use_alt_alt},
\path{ThirdPlaceSeries/TopCovolSlim2Use.lean:16--22};
\nolinkurl{CovolumeBoundOfX} and \nolinkurl{EventuallyAtAllPoints},
\path{LiftLambdaBound.lean:60--61} and \path{LiftLambdaBound.lean:17--19};
\nolinkurl{Formalism.MainNotions.Closed}, \path{ParamsHeightRankConstant.lean:159--161}; \nolinkurl{L3},
\path{IndefSide.lean:315--316}.

\begin{outline}
The first eight inputs give the lower bound on the average of the
component slopes at a good deep datum (\decid{T14}). To pass from
components to rows one needs two further geometric records.
For the first, combine KS, seminegativity and separation with
the Green assertion from regular characters (\decid{D575}) and the
Fubini--Study Chow height formula from the Hodge record (\decid{D155}).
For the second, obtain fan charts from KS (\decid{D157}), pass to
toroidal charts using the integral Siegel model, and adjoin arithmetic
degree. Toroidal nonvanishing (\decid{D153}) gives injectivity on
small sections (\decid{D127}); pair this with the Chow-form height
comparison (\decid{T16}). The remaining intersection inputs are
fields $q_6,q_8,q_{13},q_7,q_{10},q_{12}$ of the Hodge record,
integrality of the Chow form (\decid{D156}) and its hyperplane
compatibility (\decid{D154}).
Thus all seven premises of \decid{T15} are available.
There the degree and share estimates on a cover, together with the
height lower bound and the cover height sum, give a uniform lower
bound on its components. Lifting this bound and applying the
truncation, bad-set and lambda estimates yields the displayed
inequality. These estimates are lower inputs to the outline.
See \nolinkurl{topCovolSlim2_use_alt_alt},
\path{ThirdPlaceSeries/TopCovolSlim2Use.lean:17--22}, and
\nolinkurl{topCovolG_alt_alt}, \path{ThirdPlaceSeries/TopCovolG.lean:39--58}.
\end{outline}

\begin{formalstatement}[The definite-side record, \decid{T02}]
Assume the third-place unitary-group record $P$, the local theta
record $F$, agreement of theta integrals, local matching, and the
closed covolume bound above. Fix any $h:\mathrm{RecTExists}$.
For $c\in\mathbb R$ let $p(c)=\mathrm{parametersOfDependence}(h,c)$.
Write $B$ for \nolinkurl{BoundAtTwoThreeFamilyFonAlt}. Suppose that
for every real $c$ the following proposition holds:
\[
 \operatorname{LinkedAt}\bigl(p(c),\,
 (Q,L)\mapsto\operatorname{SlotNormCondition}
 (Q,L,\mathcal A\mapsto\operatorname{Closed}(\mathcal A,B))\bigr).
\]
Then $\mathrm{HypsAlt}(\mathrm{S0.NL},\mathrm{DefiniteSideCondition})$
has an inhabitant. Its family class, bundle selection and threshold
function are shared by its certificates.
\end{formalstatement}
\noindent\nolinkurl{UpperTheory.Lit.nonempty_hyps_definite_side_of_closed_bound},
\path{SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean:44--68};
\nolinkurl{GlobalTheory.Fc.HypsAlt}, \path{ThirdPlaceSeries/Hyps.lean:22--39}.

\begin{outline}
Separation of functionals follows from the unramified Howe input and
convergent unitary Siegel--Weil (\decid{T09}). With matching and
agreement, the test-vector result gives row rationality (\decid{D035}).
There are two thresholds: evaluation on a grid gives $C_1$
(\decid{D034}), and constant-function Fourier fit (\decid{T10})
gives the threshold $C_0$ for the linked functional condition
(\decid{D036}). Put $c=\max(C_0,C_1)$ and
$A=\mathrm{GridA}(c)$. Since $c\ge C_1$, $A$ holds eventually.
Take the open parameter statement at $p(c)$ (\decid{T08}) and
transport it to the extension with origin and property $A$, no
additional series and the closure of all geometries (\decid{D039}).
On this extension $c\ge C_0$ now supplies the functional condition;
the closed covolume estimate supplies the linked third-place row bound
(\decid{D041}). Apply \decid{T03} with these two linked conditions
on the extension and the assumed small-prime bound at the original
$p(c)$. Its member-stage construction transfers the needed norm
condition, assembles the row certificates and selects one definite-side
record. This is where choosing a single $c$ for all three conditions
matters. See \nolinkurl{nonempty_hyps_definite_side_of_closed_bound},
\path{SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean:45--68}, and
\nolinkurl{hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt},
\path{HypothesesNonemptyOfUnramifiedExponents.lean:41--61}.
\end{outline}
