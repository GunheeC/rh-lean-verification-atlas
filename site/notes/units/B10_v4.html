The bound before taking characters

At one scale let $H$ be the finite additive group of the reference frame, $A_0\le H$, $TT\subset H$, $T_2\subset A_0$ and $n=|H|$, $n_T=|TT|$, $n_2=|T_2|$, $\delta_2=n_2/n$. Write $\mathrm{gap}(u)$ for the sum of $\mathrm{ASK}-\Re\mathrm{MAIN}$ over $t\in TT$ and $1\le j\le m$ at translate $u$. Let $B$ be the four-term bracket in B09, with $a_E$ the lattice parameter $a$. Lattice inputs for the gap. Under the eleven hypotheses in P021, at each lattice unit $v$, $\mathrm{ASK}(v)-\mathrm{Main}(v)\le\mathrm{ErrX5at}(v)$. Under the error windows and $\mathrm{LargeX}$, $\mathrm{ErrX5at}(v)\le x^{(e_{\rm Div}+e_{\rm Loss}+3)\varepsilon} \mathrm{ErrText}$ (D567). The actual bounds for $B_0,N_c$ and the required size and identification hypotheses are specified in B10. The weighted mean and slope inputs to P021 are recorded at D568 and D570.

Derivation of the gap bound, B10

For $v$ mapping to $u$, replace the top and place indices by the given bijections with $TT$ and $\Phi$. The summands of the lattice main term then have exactly the recorded $a_t$, $w_{u,j}$, Euler constant and $\Re\ell(\chi_C)$, so the sum is $\sum_{t,j}\Re\mathrm{MAIN}_{t,j}$. The identification of the ask term gives $\sum_{t,j}\mathrm{ASK}_{t,j}$. Their difference is $\mathrm{gap}(u)$ (D569). Now $\mathrm{ErrText}=n_Ta_E(1+m^2/k)+m(B_0+N_c)$. Insert $B_0\le2nmbx^\varepsilon$ and $N_c\le n_2 k b x^\varepsilon/(n^2x^{-\varepsilon})$. All multipliers are nonnegative, so $\mathrm{ErrText}\le B$. Combining the two inequalities in 3.3 gives

$$ \mathrm{gap}(u)\le x^{(e_{\rm Div}+e_{\rm Loss}+3)\varepsilon}B.$$

Every $u\in A_0$ has a preimage by the coverage clause in CoreAtH; this transfers the bound to the entire subgroup. Deduction.ch_top_main_eq, LandauTheorems/ChTop.lean:11–35; Deduction.ch_top, LandauTheorems/ChTop.lean:37–72; CoreAtH, PrimitiveCosets/CoreAtH.lean:29–41.
