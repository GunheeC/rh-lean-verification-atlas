Statement and hypotheses

Assume admission for the specified tied entries when the curve, series, character, local-law and comparison data at each stage are supplied, and existence of the indicated Lubin--Tate curve realization for each eligible inert prime. Fix a tied host, lattice system and kernel, comparison input $I$, comparison hypotheses $H$, labels and joint datum. At a place $w$ of inert residue characteristic $p\ge5$ outside the level primes, assume the host nonvanishing condition and $p\nmid T_u$. Suppose $\mu$ is used, the level-zero stabilizer condition holds, and $2r_G\le k$. On the chain $\operatorname{chainAt}(H,h_T)$ the semilinear comparisons satisfy the level-zero identification, kernel, rationality and terminal valuation conditions. The same chain admits a system at the specified place and levels, with its standard coordinates, uniformizer and ramification normalization, and $d_G(0)=0,\ d_G(j)\le v_p(D_{\Gamma,u})$. site_cmpLite_siteAdm · JointSiteChainComparedCarriesAdmittedSystem.lean:77-89
