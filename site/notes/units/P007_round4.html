For a primitive character, the functional equation turns a zero left of the middle of the strip into a zero of the inverse character to the right. The all-character input rules out that reflected zero. critAll (MainTheorems.lean:11–13).

Separate primitive from arbitrary characters

Choose the nonzero modulus, then a primitive character, then the point in the open strip. The target is an equality of its real part with one half. Both end inequalities are strict. The reduction fixes the original modulus throughout. In the unrestricted all-character export P004 one must first pass to the conductor. Here primitivity is already supplied at the point where the functional equation is invoked. critAll (MainTheorems.lean:11–13). PrimitiveDirichletLZerosOnLine (Statements.lean:14–16).

Remove the gamma factor at the zero

Positive real part gives a nonzero real gamma factor in the even case. In the odd case its argument has been shifted by one and still has positive real part. The equality between ordinary L and completed L divided by this factor transfers the zero to the numerator. The point $s=0$ is excluded by the strict lower bound on its real part. This explicit denominator check matters because the division operation in Lean is total. Formalism.VW.gammaFactor_ne_zero (CritLine.lean:6–9). Formalism.VW.critLine (CritLine.lean:11–27).

Apply the all-character input on the proper side

For $\Re s<1/2$, invariance of the conductor under inversion supplies primitivity of $\chi^{-1}$. Apply its functional equation at $s$, substitute the completed zero, and rewrite its double inverse as $\chi$. The ordinary L-function for $\chi^{-1}$ then vanishes at $1-s$. Both exclusions used here have witnesses: $1-s\ne0$ follows from $s\ne1$, and $1-s\ne1$ follows from $s\ne0$. Since $\Re(1-s)>1/2$, P009 contradicts the reflected zero. A point on the right gives a contradiction directly with $s\ne1$. The middle equality remains. Formalism.VW.critLine (CritLine.lean:11–27). critAll (MainTheorems.lean:11–13).
