Outlined · pending_review. For the specified twist selection and enriched family, sufficiently large points have the required level-prime valuation bound on period columns. The point, period and size estimates used to obtain it must apply to the same family at the same scale.

Statement and hypotheses

Fix $\mathcal A$ with a twist selection. On $D_3(3,\mathcal A)$ suppose the following are closed: untwisted point and twisted-point integrality; the untwisted period-class Weyl sum and twisted-period admissibility; the sharp untwisted and twisted column bounds at the selected level; and the existence of a common constant for the used tuples and the family size at the type prime. Suppose the selection is served on this class. Then the following too is closed: there is $x_0$ such that for $x\ge x_0$, $x>x_1(F)$ and each index at $x$, for every unit $u$, place $w$, lattice vector $\phi\in L.\mathrm{NuLat}(u)$ and form $F'$ in the prescribed level module and the subspace $V_0$, there is a coefficient-field element $y$ whose fixed embedding equals the period column at $(u,w,\phi,F')$. It is zero or satisfies $v_w(y)\ge\lfloor\operatorname{ur}(D,w,L.\mathrm{sg}(u,w))\rfloor$. closed_family_admissible_twist_link_alt · ClosedFamilyAdmissibleTwistLink.lean:12-31

Get one large scale for all the local tests

For a fixed family take a maximum of the finitely many point, period, service, stabilizer and size thresholds and the auxiliary threshold $3^{5/e_z}$. Positivity of $e_z$ translates the auxiliary threshold into $3\le x^{e_z/5}$. Thus all quantities needed by the local calculation are present at once.
