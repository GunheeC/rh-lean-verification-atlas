Statement and hypotheses

Assume $F$, the automorphic join, the Hecke--Tate functional equation, both theta comparisons of T11, separation as in T09, and the lower-weight assertion of T05. Then some $C_{F,0}$ works for every $C_F\ge C_{F,0}$. Tie local and global tests

The local theta input alone gives the finite-place, away and infinity estimates. Use the local theta input together with both theta comparisons for the joined local-sum relations. Fit the specified functional

The closure step collects a bundle built from rows and a scaling estimate. The embedded-field result and denominator data provide the function that is to be fitted. Use the resulting threshold

The next argument obtains a grid threshold for the functional condition and another for the existence of evaluations. Choosing their maximum makes the same grid admissible for both. This illustrates why T10 chooses a threshold before all larger grid sizes and before specializing the family.
