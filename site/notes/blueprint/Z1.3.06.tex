\subsection{Z1.3.06. Specialize the local test to the global cocycle}\label{r5:Z1.3.06}
Fix a prime $\ell$, an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $\mathbb Q$, a coefficient-field identification $\iota$, an integer $e\ge1$ as required by the ordinary normal form, the finite set $S$, valuation ring $A$, representation $W$ and form $g$ of weight $2e+1$. Retain both the ordinary normal form and the unramified-source hypotheses. The claim holds for any common predicate $\mathrm{Pin}$. Its global premise produces one ordered tuple
\[
 (c,y,S',\nu,\chi,Z)
 \quad\text{with}\quad
 \mathrm{Sys}(W,c,y,S'),\quad\mathrm{IwSys2}(c,A,\nu,Z),\quad
 \mathrm{SpLink}(A,e,\chi,Z,y),\quad\mathrm{Pin}(e,A,W,c,\nu,\chi,Z).
\]
Its local premise quantifies universally over all six entries of precisely this tuple. Apply that premise at these witnesses with the same four proofs. It supplies an $s$ in inertia with cyclotomic character $1$, $W(s)_{11}=1$ and $y(s)_1\ne0$ (indices $0,1$). Keep $c,y,S'$ and the first proof. This proves the existential assertion $\mathrm{ZetaSysUr2}$, including the tame condition for every eligible $w\notin S'$ and every admissible surjective character. The dependence of the local test on $y$ comes through the specialization link, even though $y$ is absent from the arguments of $\mathrm{Pin}$.
