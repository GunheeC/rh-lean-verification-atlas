\subsection{Z1.3.08. Assembling the unconditional unramified-source statement}\label{r6:Z1.3.08}
Fix the closure, embeddings, coefficient prime, ordinary normal form and unramified
modular source in \nolinkurl{ZetaUr}. The pin used at this call is
\nolinkurl{Pin7}. Z1.3.04 takes three inputs: the integral Euler-system
statement (.01), the smoothed Hodge--Tate law (.02), and existence of the
admissible nonvanishing projection (.03). Specializing the third to the
\emph{same} closure gives a single tuple $(c,y,S',\nu,\chi,Z)$ satisfying the
global system, inertia, specialization-link and pin assertions. Apply the
universal local test (.05) to these witnesses. Its inertia element has a
nonzero quotient coordinate of $y$, while the corresponding diagonal matrix
entry is $1$. Every Greenberg coboundary has quotient coordinate $(1-1)x=0$
at that element. Retaining $c,y,S'$ and the original exceptional and tame
conditions gives the asserted system. This is exactly the order of the two
implications .06 and .07. Choosing a second projection or a new inertia
system after obtaining $y$ would lose the link to this cocycle.

\emph{Partial for the unconditional declaration.} The assembly just given
uses the two already reconstructed universal implications. To remove its
antecedents one must produce the global pinned tuple and pass the local
specialization test under the public normal form and modular-source hypotheses.
The remaining assertions for each supplier are in Sections~\ref{r6:Z1.3.01}--\ref{r6:Z1.3.05}.
See also the external coefficient and local-condition tests on
page~\pageref{r6:kato-lit}.
