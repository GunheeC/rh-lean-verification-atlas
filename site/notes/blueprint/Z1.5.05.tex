\paragraph{Z1.5.05. A uniform numerical bound after restriction.}
\phantomsection\label{r8:small:Z1.5.05}
The lower quadratic classifier uses S20 twice, for \(M\) and \(\ell\):
intersecting the two cofinite sets still leaves a cofinite set on which
both representations to be compared have the same candidate Frobenius
multiset. Assume the lower classifiers and the root-multiset aggregation
bound. The rational construction starts from \(n^2+2\) fixed quadratic
fields with constants \(c_j,A_j\) chosen before the auxiliary prime.
It retains \(n^2+1\) distinct indices \(u(i)\). Set \(X=M(H+1)\ge1\).
Each lower size is at most \(c_{u(i)}X^{A_{u(i)}}\), hence at most
\((\sum_j c_j)X^{\sum_j A_j}\). The aggregation estimate multiplies the
sum of these \(n^2+1\) sizes by at most \(2^{n+1}\).
The resulting constant and exponent are therefore independent of the
prime and the retained indices. To obtain the rational classifier it
remains to construct the compatible root choices and bound the rational
representation fibres by the restriction/self-twist argument
