Reading notes

Blueprint v13 and papers v3, v4 and v5 are this project’s AI-written, unpublished drafts. None has had human review. Their author is not the author of the Lean development. Blueprint v13, its separate cover, and paper v5 are distributed as assets of release v0.1.0. For v3 and v4 the atlas supplies selected excerpts and notes. Edition register

The atlas indexes 217 units, with source excerpts, round-3 mathematical reading notes and seven graduate reading packets. Each packet includes its TeX source and full typeset pages from blueprint v13. Read the target and its premises before assigning the rest of a companion unit.
