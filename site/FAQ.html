What do the line counts measure?

A written step cites a range of lines in a particular Lean file. The counts take the union of those ranges within each category. A citation identifies the source for an inference. It says nothing about how many lines a person has checked or how much proof lies behind the cited result. The status page gives the two denominators and links to the separate table locating each source line in the index. Definitions and counts · History

What does an AI review cover?

An AI system wrote the exposition. A separate AI review can compare a statement, inspect a particular step or assess an entire specified edition. A review of one passage applies only within its recorded scope. Review archive · Authorship

How can I check a single assertion?

Choose a unit and compare its quantifiers, hypotheses and exceptional cases with the linked Lean statement. Read an individual numbered step and follow each input it uses. Record the edition identifier, the step number and your verdict. You can record a gap or a step you could not follow. Starter packets · How to submit

What do the grades mean?

L0 identifies the formal source. L1 records a comparison of the statement and its assumptions. L2 records an AI review of the written argument under the stated inputs. L3 requires matching evidence from at least two independent sources. L4 applies only to a Full unit and requires a derivation beyond an appeal to an input. Every current step and a separate statement comparison must have a qualifying personal check. An unresolved objection prevents that target from qualifying. A second reader other than the maintainer can resolve a dispute, or a new edition can supersede its target. Names are self-declared. Partial and negative assessments stay visible.
