Glossary

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Terms such as “host”, “polarized row”, “page function”, “closed link”, “tied family” and “definite-side witness” name Lean identifiers or constructions in this project’s drafts. They are not standard names for these particular definitions in the literature. Each source link opens the exact Lean definition or theorem. The unit column identifies the statement owner when one exists and otherwise gives the place to read its construction or use. A shared short name may have other meanings elsewhere in the development.
