P011 · Nonvanishing conditional on the polarised row

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Assuming the polarised Galois row, the conclusion is the required half-plane nonvanishing for all Dirichlet characters, subject to the principal-pole exception. This conditional statement isolates what the arithmetic comparison is being asked to supply.

Statement

Assume $\mathcal R$. Then $H$ holds for every character of every positive modulus, with its exception at the principal pole.

Assumptions

  • Assume the full polarized Galois row $\mathcal R$.
  • For the half-plane assertion use positive modulus, $\Re s>1/2$, and exclude the principal-character pole.
