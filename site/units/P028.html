P028 · The lower bound from integral derivations

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Injectively represented integral point derivations that vanish on the chosen equations bound the length of a module by that of a cotangent-lattice quotient. This gives the lower comparison needed when an independent geometric or orbit count bounds the quotient from above.

Statement

Let $O$ be a discrete valuation ring with finite residue field, $A$ an algebra graded by an abelian group, $x:A\to O$ a point vanishing on $A_j$, and $F_i\in A_j$. Fix $0\ne c\in O$. Suppose an additive injection $D:M\to\operatorname{Hom}_O(A,O/(c))$ kills all $F_i$ at every value. Suppose each $D(\mu)$ is the reduction of an $O$-valued point derivation of weight $j$ at $x$. With the saturated lattice as above,

$$\operatorname{length}_O M \le \operatorname{length}_O(L_j/\langle\overline F_i\rangle).$$

Assumptions

  • Fix a DVR with finite residue field, a graded algebra, a marked point vanishing on the selected degree, elements $F_i$ in that degree and $c\ne0$.
  • The additive injection into the dual modulo $c$ kills all $F_i$, and every value lifts to a point derivation of that weight.
