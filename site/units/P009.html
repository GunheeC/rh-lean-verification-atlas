P009 · Half-plane nonvanishing for all Dirichlet characters

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The half-plane assertion here ranges over all characters, including imprimitive ones. For real part greater than one half it requires nonvanishing except at one for the principal character. This is the statement passed to the public export.

Statement

For every $N\ge1$, Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$ and $s\in\mathbb C$, $\Re s>1/2$ and $(\chi\ne\chi_0\ \text{or}\ s\ne1)$ imply $L(s,\chi)\ne0$.

Assumptions

  • Fix a Dirichlet character of positive modulus and $s$ with $\Re s>1/2$.
  • Exclude the pole by requiring a nonprincipal character or $s\ne1$.
