W13 · Assembling the Selmer window from five counting cases

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

With the uniform second-cohomology estimate, common exceptional-layer window and odd-minus bound, the five stated counting cases give the Selmer window at cutoff exponent 401. Checking the assembly requires knowing which collective or individual estimate covers each character and prime.

Statement

Assume Poitou–Tate duality, local finiteness and local Tate duality. Fix a $C_K$ satisfying W05's cohomological bound and constants as in W12 giving its simultaneous bound at $C_t=401$. Assume W14's minus-part bound, the collective estimate of W08, both individual estimates of W09, the restricted Case V estimate of W10 and the individual estimate of W09's shape for Case Z (odd $\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\mid2e$, $\psi=1$). Then the Selmer window $V$ of P013 holds.

Assumptions

  • Assume Poitou–Tate, local finiteness and local Tate duality. Take a uniform W05 constant and W12 constants at the same cutoff 401.
  • Use the minus-part estimate, the collective Case I, individual Cases II and III, restricted Case V, and the indicated exceptional trivial-character Case Z bound.
