Statement

For every chain and adelic datum $A$, determinant map $d$ and exponent $a$ satisfying the given adelic pin and tie conditions, the functions $\Xi=\Xi_{\rm ad}(A,a)$ and $\mu_T=\mu_{\rm of}(t,u,\mu,d)$ satisfy the entire $\mathrm{AdLawAt}$. The first function reads the prescribed adjacent kernels after change of frame, transforms under rational and level actions, depends only on the specified bad-place components, satisfies the local congruence condition, is supported on the prescribed denominator lattice and is integral off the designated primes. The second reads both rank-one determinants and class representatives, is algebraic and multiplicative on norm-one ideles, is trivial on the prescribed integral ideles, and on norm-one rational points is the product of the prescribed integral embedding powers.

Assumptions

  • Take a chain, its adelic data, determinant map and exponent.
  • Require the specified adelic pin and tie conditions for those same data.
