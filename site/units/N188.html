N188 · Nonsingular Hermitian Gram coefficient

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

In the indicated stable dimension range, inverse Fourier integration of the Gram pushforward at a nonsingular Hermitian matrix equals the normalized orbit average over its rational Gram fibre. This identifies a Fourier coefficient with an orbital integral for the given test function.

Statement

For a CM Hermitian space of dimension $M>2n$, every adelic Schwartz–Bruhat test and nonsingular Hermitian $T$, inverse Fourier integration of the Gram pushforward at $T$ equals the orbit average of the rational Gram fibre with the specified probability measure. Weil's Theorem 5 in type $\mathrm{I}_2$ gives the normalized comparison in Chapter T.

Assumptions

  • Work over the given CM Hermitian space with dimension greater than twice the target rank.
  • Use an adelic Schwartz–Bruhat test and a nonsingular Hermitian Gram index.
