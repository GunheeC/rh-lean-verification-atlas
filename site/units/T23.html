T23 · Nef growth of the chart lattice

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a nef multidegree, the ranks of the normed chart lattices have a leading term determined by the generic degree. The argument sandwiches those ranks between ordinary section ranks with two fixed twists, preserving the particular chart lattice needed for the volume comparison.

Statement

Let $y$ be a birational multihomogeneous presentation in a characteristic-zero field with arithmetic variety $P$ of dimension $D+1$, and let the integral multidegree $a$ be nef on the generic fibre. Put $V_n=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}(\mathrm{chartLatNm}(y,a,n))$. Each $V_n$ is finite-dimensional and

$$\lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{D!\,\dim_{\mathbb Q}V_n}{n^D} =\mathrm{genDeg}(P,D,a).$$
