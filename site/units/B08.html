B08 · A one-scale bound at a host

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At one sufficiently large scale, a host meeting the character-share and discarded-character estimates can be paired with compatible lattice and size data to bound the level prime sum. The exponent records the division and loss costs. The threshold is chosen before the individual frame, host and test set.

Statement

For a level $\mathcal L$, fixed real $e_D,e_L,K,c_{HH},e_K,C$ and $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $x_1$ such that, for every $x\ge x_1$, frame, host on that frame and finite test set $TT$, the following two conditions imply

$$\mathcal G_{\mathcal L}(x)\le x^{1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}.$$

First, the host and level have the same order $o$, represent every kept character in the positive family, and agree on the distinguished quadratic character's exceptional status. Each translate of the subgroup $A_0$ has a share of $TT$ within $x^{-1+\varepsilon}$ of its target ($4/(3o+1)$ for $A_0$ and $3/(3o+1)$ otherwise, in the equivalent unnormalised counting inequality). For the positive-family characters outside the kept set and all negative-family characters the bounds are $|G_\chi(x)|\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ and $|(L'/L)(1,\chi)|\le C\log x$.

Second, there are finite unit and word data, with nonempty word fibres, a map onto every element of $A_0$, and arithmetic coefficients whose nontrivial character logarithmic derivatives agree with the host's. They identify the lattice top and place indices with $TT$ and the index-two subgroup, and satisfy the lattice hypotheses, error-window and $B_0,N_c$ bounds in B10 and the size, thin, cost and remaining-size conditions in B09, with $T_2\subset A_0$. The scale and exponent of the lattice data are $x,\varepsilon$; their division, loss and height exponents are bounded above by $e_D,e_L,c_{HH}$ respectively.

Assumptions

  • Fix the level and real constants $e_D,e_L,K,c_{HH},e_K,C$ and $\varepsilon>0$ before choosing the frame, host and test set.
  • At the chosen scale the host satisfies the explicit-formula, character compatibility, share and derivative clauses for the level.
  • Require a core lattice realization on the same host, with B10's error and exponent controls, B09's sizes, thinness and costs, and the stated subgroup inclusion.
