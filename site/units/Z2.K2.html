Z2.K2 · Cycles on integral matrices and uniform residue evaluation

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A divisor condition yields an integral matrix-group cycle and one exceptional denominator. At every allowed reduction its evaluation recovers the character of a twisted norm times an explicit product. The same cycle can therefore test many reductions.

Statement

In a cyclic layer with $2e+3<\ell^c$, any $\alpha$ satisfying the divisor condition gives a degree-$(2e+3)$ cycle over $\mathcal O_L$ with coefficients modulo $\ell^\nu$ and a single nonzero denominator. At every allowed residue map, every additive evaluator with the prescribed scalar value returns $P_{e+1}v(u)$ for a residue $u$ of the twisted norm, with $P_{e+1}=\prod_{0\le i<2e+3,\ i\ne e+1}(\kappa^{e+1}-\kappa^i)$ and $\kappa=1+\ell^c$. A primitive reduced root supplies such an evaluator.

Assumptions

  • Use a cyclic layer with $2e+3<\ell^c$ and an element satisfying the divisor condition.
  • The residual maps and evaluators satisfy the stated scalar conditions.
