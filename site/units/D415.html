D415 · Injectivity of the adjoint theta scalar formula

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a specified real datum and admissible system of scalar forms, the theta map and its adjoint satisfy a scalar formula on the parts of the row. Positivity on the nonzero parts makes the adjoint injective. This supplies the injectivity input for the construction of automorphic rows.

Statement

For the given real datum and face, assume the comparison hypotheses, unit conductor ideal, agreement of the indicated place sets and the fixed-point condition at conjugation-invariant good places. Every admitted scalar form then satisfies the bilinear formula, and each theta operator has injective adjoint.

$$\forall S_F,\ \mathrm{IsSF}(R,S_F)\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{BForm}(R,S_F)\wedge\mathrm{UpInj}(R).$$

Assumptions

  • Use the harmonic-analysis, Siegel–Weil, Hecke-form and doubling input records specified at the pin.
  • For the given real datum and face assume the comparison hypotheses, unit conductor ideal, agreement of place sets and the fixed-point condition at conjugation-invariant good places.
  • Take a scalar form satisfying the stated admissibility condition.
