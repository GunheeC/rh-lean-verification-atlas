Z5.2 · Height of the evaluation dual

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

In positive full rank the evaluation dual has reciprocal height and size to the primal lattice. Its ordinary slope therefore changes sign. This lets the same height calculation supply the slope of the dual under the stipulated frame normalization.

Statement

For positive full rank $D$ and invertible archimedean frames over the same field, the evaluation dual has reciprocal height and size. Thus its ordinary slope has the opposite sign to the primal slope $-2\log\mathrm{ht}/(nD)$.

Assumptions

  • Use a lattice of positive full rank in the invertible archimedean frames.
  • Use the specified evaluation pairing.
