D570 · Upper bound for the weighted mean of lower slopes

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The lower-slope correction varies over a finite set of lattice indices. A common count bound at a single unit gives an upper bound for each correction, so the same bound holds after taking its weighted mean.

Statement

Under the Hida size setting and Count5, the d(u)-weighted mean of the lower quantities Lo5 at any unit u is at most cSl1·IO(u)+K7v5.

$$\mathrm{wmean}\bigl(d(u),\mathrm{Lo5}(u)\bigr)\le c_{\rm Sl1}\mathrm{IO}(u)+K_{7,v5}.$$

Assumptions

  • Use the Hida size setting, the counting hypothesis and one unit with its prescribed weights.
