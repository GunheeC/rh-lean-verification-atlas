W08 · The collective bound in Case I

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

In counting Case I, the hypotheses give two auxiliary primes and a single exponent for each initial character. The resulting bound is collective: it controls a sum of lengths over all relevant characters and every finite set of eligible odd primes.

Statement

Assume $H'$, the uniform bound W05, the two-twist vanishing of W07, and the rank-three automorphy input for unramified coefficient rings. In the latter, a common marked element gives finiteness and the Type I automorphy and Type II modularity conclusions for the specializations of each allowed deformation ring. For every $\chi$ there are $C\in\mathbb N$ and primes $r_1,r_2$ such that, for each large host with $q_1,d>\max(r_1,r_2)$, $0<e<t$, every algebraic closure, every choice of coefficient rings $O_\ell$ and every finite set $T$ of odd primes with $\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\nmid2e$, all the lengths below are finite and

$$\sum_{\ell\in T}\log\ell\! \sum_{\psi\in X_h(O_\ell,e)} v_\ell(\psi,e)\le (Nt)^C .$$

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$, the uniform cohomological bound of W05, two-twist vanishing and rank-three automorphy for unramified coefficients.
  • In the last input the two kinds of specialization use the prescribed common mark.
