P003 · Nonvanishing of Dirichlet L-functions in the right half-plane

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The public half-plane target concerns every Dirichlet character of every positive modulus. Its L-function is required to be nonzero to the right of one half, with the principal character at one excepted. This is the nonvanishing formulation used to connect the other public goals.

Statement

For every $N\ge1$, Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$ and $s\in\mathbb C$, $\Re s>1/2$ and $(\chi\ne\chi_0\ \text{or}\ s\ne1)$ imply $L(s,\chi)\ne0$.

Assumptions

  • Take a character of positive modulus and $s$ with $\Re s>1/2$.
  • If the character is principal, require $s\ne1$.
