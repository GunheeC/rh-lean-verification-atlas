Z1.3.07 · Failure of the local Greenberg condition

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The matching local and global assertions give an integral system violating the Greenberg condition at the designated place for each allowed modular ordinary normal form. The violation is the local feature needed of that global system.

Statement

Given the matching global and local assertions, every allowed modular ordinary normal form has a system violating the Greenberg condition at its specified place.

Assumptions

  • Fix an ordinary normal form.
  • Assume its global and local existence assertions with a common pin.
