P004 · Real parts of Dirichlet zeros in the open strip

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The public assertion applies to every character of a positive modulus. Its proof selects the open-strip component of a conjunction. The analytic reduction behind that component must handle characters induced from smaller conductors.

Statement

For every $N\ge1$ and every Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$, if $L(s,\chi)=0$ with $0<\Re s<1$, then $\Re s=1/2$.

Assumptions

  • Take a Dirichlet character of positive modulus.
  • The point is a zero in the open strip $0<\Re s<1$.
