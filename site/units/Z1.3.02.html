Z1.3.02 · Hodge–Tate law for smoothed classes

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For the stated integral class and smoothing data, the second coordinate obeys an explicit Hodge–Tate law with one nonzero scalar and compatible units at every precision. This keeps comparisons at different levels tied to the same scalar.

Statement

For admissible Hodge–Tate parameters and every compatible embedding, integral class family, eigendistribution and $d_0\ge2$ prime to $\ell$, the smoothed second coordinate satisfies the explicit HT law with one nonzero scalar and precision-compatible units at every level.

Assumptions

  • Fix compatible Hodge–Tate data, embedding, integral class family and eigendistribution.
  • The smoothing integer is at least two and prime to the coefficient prime.
