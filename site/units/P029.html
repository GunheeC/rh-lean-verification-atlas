Statement

Assume the automorphic hypotheses $H'$ and the CM level and Satake–Frobenius correspondence $\mathrm{LevelKNAlt}$. For each CM field $K$ and $n\ge2$ there exist $c,A\in\mathbb N$. Let $m_1>\cdots>m_n$ satisfy $m_i+m_{n+1-i}=1-n$ and $|m_i|\le H$. Given $M\ge1$ and $w\mid p$ with $p$ prime, $p\nmid M\operatorname{disc}(K)$, there are eigenvalue data $D$ at $Nw$ of weight $m$ and purity $1-n$, with

$$|D.S|\le c[M(H+1)]^A .$$

For every odd prime $\ell\ne p$, closure and coefficient comparison $\iota$, these same data classify the admitted $\mathrm{AdmKNAlt}(\iota,M,m)$ representations of $G_K$ at $w$.

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$ and LevelKNAlt. Fix a CM field and rank $n\ge2$.
  • The strictly decreasing weights satisfy $m_i+m_{n+1-i}=1-n$ and the given height bound. Use the stipulated admissibility, level and good-prime conditions.
