P019 · A zero-free half-plane for a selected class of primitive characters

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Uniform choices of the division and loss exponents let the family prime-sum bound approach the half power as epsilon shrinks. If suitable tied families and the level window criterion are available for each selected primitive character, its L-function is nonzero to the right of one half.

Statement

Fix a predicate $Q$ on $\mathcal P$, $\lambda>1$, and a system of families with core and tied predicates as in TopWin.lean:19–33. For each $\psi$ satisfying $Q$, write $\mathcal L=\operatorname{cyc}(\psi)$ and suppose the following.

  • For every $e_D,e_L\in\mathbb R$, every $\varepsilon>0$ and every family $F$ at $\varepsilon$, the conjunction of EFamC$(\mathcal L,F)$ and eventual tiedness on its window set with $e_D,e_L$ implies $\mathcal G_{\mathcal L}(x)\le x^{1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}$ at every sufficiently large point of that set.
  • The level-window implication of P018 holds for $\mathcal L$.
  • There exist $e_D,e_L\in\mathbb R$ and $\varepsilon_1>0$, common to all subsequent $\varepsilon$. For every $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_1$ there is a family $F$ at $\varepsilon$ satisfying EFamC$(\mathcal L,F)$ with exponent-$\lambda$ windows and eventual tiedness on that set with these same $e_D,e_L$. In both clauses eventual tiedness uses fixed $K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x_0$ for the selected family, as in P017.

Then $L(s,\psi)\ne0$ for every $\psi$ satisfying $Q$ and every $s$ with $\Re s>1/2$.

Assumptions

  • Fix a character predicate $Q$, $\lambda>1$ and the stipulated family system. For each selected character use its cyclic level.
  • The P017-type bound and the P018 window criterion hold on that level.
  • There are fixed real $e_D,e_L$ and $\varepsilon_1>0$. For each $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_1$, a family satisfies EFamC, has exponent-$\lambda$ windows and is eventually tied with those $e_D,e_L$.
