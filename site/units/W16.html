W16 · Finiteness of the candidates in a base-change frame

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A comparison frame admits finitely many relevant eigenvalue pairs and local product models, together with one invariant-dimension bound for its auxiliary places. This is the candidate finiteness needed by the definite isobaric base-change reduction.

Statement

Fix a local parameter reading rule, an everywhere finite-unramified CM extension $E/E^+$, $n\ge1$, an automorphic $\pi$ on the unitary group, a finite set $S$ and a comparison frame for $(\pi,S)$. Choose local test data and an auxiliary finite set $T'$ of nonsplit places disjoint from the frame's initial set $S_0$ and its bad set. For each partition index $i$ and each $w\in T'$, the ordered pairs of joint Hecke eigenvalue tuples of all candidate pairs lie in a finite set. For each $w\in S_0$ a finite list of model products suffices. Every candidate product has the same irreducible subquotients and the same dimension of $K'_w$-invariants as one on the list, whose invariant space is finite-dimensional. A single integer bounds, over all candidates and all $w\in T'$, the dimension of the candidate pair's invariants under the prescribed two-block subgroup $I_2$.

Assumptions

  • Fix a local parameter rule, finite-unramified CM extension, positive rank, a unitary automorphic representation and its comparison frame.
  • Use the local tests and auxiliary nonsplit places disjoint from the initial and bad sets.
