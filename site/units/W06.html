Statement

Assume $H'$. For every algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, large host $(h,t)$, odd prime $\ell$, coefficient ring $O$, $0<e<t$, $\psi\in X_h(O,e)$ and good rank-four symplectic datum $D$ in Case II or III of W09, the following hold. Here the datum has character $\psi$, ramification set the primes dividing $2\ell Np_1$, and an odd auxiliary character built from a primitive character at $2^{n'}$ and one of order $m_0$ at $p_1$. The prescribed $(n',p_1,m_0)$ is $(n_{\rm II},1,1)$ in Case II and $(n_{\rm III},p_1(\chi),\operatorname{ord}\psi)$ in Case III. The goodness conditions fix the places, inertia and parity of the characters, a lift of the mod-$\ell$ cyclotomic character of exponent $\ell-1$, distinguished Frobenius elements and their residual separation, and vanishing of the locally trivial residual cocycles for $\alpha,\beta,\theta$ and their inverses. They include the distinguished limit, the nontriviality of $\bar\alpha$ and $\bar\Psi$ on $g_0$, and an inertial $s_0\in\ker\psi\cap\ker\omega$ of infinite cyclotomic order. For every lift satisfying the imposed component conditions, its weight-zero monomials generate a module-finite $O$-algebra. At any geometric point with block image the blocks indexed by $\{0,2\}$ span a proper subspace of $M_2$. There is one $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ for all irreducible geometric points. Whenever such a point is continuous and stays irreducible on restriction to a quadratic CM field, that restriction is attached via $\iota$ to a regular algebraic cusp form and has the local witness of W07.

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$ and use an eligible large host, odd coefficient prime, $0<e<t$ and the prescribed character in $X_h(O,e)$.
  • Take a good rank-four symplectic datum satisfying the Case II or III and character restrictions in the statement.
