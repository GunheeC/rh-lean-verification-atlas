Z1.5.07 · CM Frobenius trace bound

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At a prime away from level and discriminant, the CM counting input bounds the list of possible inverse Frobenius traces for the designated rank-three representations. For a fixed CM field, one polynomial in level and twist works across the allowed coefficient primes.

Statement

From CountKAlt obtain, over each fixed CM field, a trace classifier for Adm3NAlt at inverse Frobenius of norm $\mathrm Nw$. Its size is at most $c[N(e+1)]^A$, when $p$ is prime to $N$ and the field discriminant, for each odd coefficient prime distinct from $p$.

Assumptions

  • Assume CountKAlt for the fixed CM field.
  • Use a place prime to the level and discriminant of the field.
