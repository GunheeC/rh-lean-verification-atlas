Statement

In ranks 2–4 the local theorem record and dense ordinary vanishing give the adjoint lifting assertion at the prescribed generic points. Under the parity condition a finite polarized adjoint cocycle is a coboundary. Construction of the vanishing input and the rank-three even-level flag are additional assignments within this review.

Assumptions

  • Use ranks two through four and the local theorem record.
  • Assume dense ordinary vanishing at the prescribed generic points. For the coboundary clause impose the stated parity.
