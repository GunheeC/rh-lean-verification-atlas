P005 · Real parts of primitive-character zeros in the open strip

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The primitive-character target restricts the open-strip zero statement to primitive Dirichlet characters. It specifies the public conclusion that can be read before considering how changing conductor affects zeros.

Statement

For every $N\ge1$ and every primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $N$, if $L(s,\chi)=0$ with $0<\Re s<1$, then $\Re s=1/2$.

Assumptions

  • Take a primitive Dirichlet character of positive modulus.
  • The point is a zero in the open strip $0<\Re s<1$.
