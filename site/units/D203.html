D203 · Fixed vectors over the de Rham period ring

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A fixed element of the de Rham period field is the image of an element of the chosen finite local field. Its filtration level and leading term determine the coefficient, and invariance forces the higher-filtration error to vanish.

Statement

For the specified p-adic field K, every element of the de Rham period ring fixed by all automorphisms of the p-adic algebraic closure over K is the image of an element of K.

$$\forall x\in B_{\rm dR},\quad (\forall\sigma\in G_K,\ \sigma x=x)\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists k\in K,\ \iota_K(k)=x.$$

Assumptions

  • Fix the prescribed $p$-adic field $K$ and $x\in B_{\rm dR}$.
  • Every element of the absolute Galois group of $K$ fixes $x$.
