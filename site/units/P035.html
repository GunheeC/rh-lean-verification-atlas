P035 · Stability of integral homological torsion orders

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Beyond the stated rank threshold, the torsion in integral homology is finite and its order stays fixed as the matrix rank increases. This permits the counting argument to use a stable-rank torsion order.

Statement

For a totally complex field $L$ and $n'\ge n\ge 2m+4$, the torsion in $H_m(G_{n'},\mathbb Z)$ is finite, of order $\lvert H_m(G_n,\mathbb Z)_{\rm tors}\rvert$.

Assumptions

  • Use a totally complex number field $L$ and nonnegative homological degree $m$.
  • The matrix ranks satisfy $n'\ge n\ge 2m+4$.
