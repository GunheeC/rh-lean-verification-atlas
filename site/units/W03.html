Statement

Given the rank-$n$ literature record and W04, there is a common record $H'$ with the following fifteen fields. It retains the rank-$n$ input, supplies the cohomological, conjugation and Borel-injectivity assertions, and attaches Galois representations in rank two over totally real fields. It supplies strong multiplicity one, the Gelfand–Kazhdan duality, admissibility, quadratic base change and the newform realization on the real side. Its last five fields give weak unitary descent, the cyclic base-change and rank-two descent conditions, definite quaternionic existence and semisimplicity of the real cuspidal representation.

Assumptions

  • Use the rank-$n$ literature hypothesis record and weak unitary descent in ranks two through four.
