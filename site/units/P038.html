P038 · All-prime torsion from faces and stabilizers

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a totally complex field, an explicit face-orbit bound and a stabilizer-size parameter bound the whole torsion subgroup of integral homology. Because the conclusion gives its full order, subsequent estimates can apply at every prime.

Statement

For every totally complex number field $L$ and $N,m\ge0$, let $d=[L:\mathbb Q]N$, $s=3^{dN}$, and $A=\mathrm{orbBound}(L,N,m)$, the uniform face-orbit bound of the integral Hermitian complex through degree $m$. Then $T=H_m(G_N,\mathbb Z)_{\rm tors}$ is finite with

$$|T|\le(m+2)^A s^{m A s^m}.$$

Assumptions

  • Fix totally complex $L$ and nonnegative $N,m$.
  • Use the uniform face-orbit count through degree $m$ for the integral Hermitian complex at this rank.
