D550 · A common scale for logarithms and fixed constants

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a fixed positive epsilon and fixed family constants, one scale threshold makes both the logarithm and the specified large constant smaller than the epsilon power of the scale. This allows both losses to be absorbed in the same eventual estimate.

Statement

For positive epsilon and fixed K,cHH,eK there exists x0 such that for every x at least x0 both log x and the constant KBig(K,cHH,eK) are at most x^epsilon.

$$\forall\varepsilon>0,\ \forall K,c_{HH},e_K,\ \exists x_0,\ \forall x\ge x_0,\quad\max\{\log x,K_{\rm big}(K,c_{HH},e_K)\}\le x^\varepsilon.$$

Assumptions

  • Fix a positive exponent $\varepsilon$ and real constants $K,c_{HH},e_K$ before choosing the scale threshold.
