Statement

Assume $H'$, W05 and the reduced unitary rank-three lifting input. Over a model of $\mathbb Q(i)$ this input selects, for each host frame, a marked element at which the weight-zero piece of every universal lift is module-finite and each Type I specialization satisfies the prescribed automorphy clause of weight $-1$. For every $\chi$ there is $C\in\mathbb N$ such that the individual finite-length bound of W09 holds for each large host, closure, coefficient ring, $0<e<t$ and $\psi\in X_h(O,e)$ satisfying $\ell$ odd, $\ell\in B_\chi$, $e=1$ and

$$R_1=16\,\operatorname{ord}(\chi)(\ell\operatorname{cond}(\chi))^2 4^{n_0}\le 2^{c_2(\psi)}.$$

Here $c_2$ is the exponent of $2$ in the conductor of the local $2$-component of $\psi$, and $n_0=v_2(\ell^{f_0}-1)$, with $f_0$ the order of $\ell$ modulo the least common multiple of $4$ and the order of the prime-to-$\ell$ part of $\psi$ stripped of its $2$-component.

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$, the W05 uniform cohomology bound and reduced unitary rank-three lifting.
  • Use the marked host frame and eligible Case V parameters.
