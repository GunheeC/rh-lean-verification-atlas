D193 · The normalized flat model and universal isogeny

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The generic Hecke correspondence supplies two maps and a universal isogeny. Its normalization has to carry extensions of both maps and of that same isogeny, retain the required type, and preserve the universal property on the given open.

Statement

The normalization furnishes the flat model and universal isogeny of type Tj with the specified local rings and identifications.

$$\mathrm{EsNorm}.$$

Assumptions

  • Fix the datum at a prime $p$ over a characteristic-zero discrete valuation ring with $p$ in its maximal ideal. Use a model of level $N\ge3$ coprime to $p$.
  • Take a maximal ideal containing the ideal at $r$, $j\le n$, and $c=p^e$ with $e>0$ if $j>0$. The multiplicity parameter is one.
