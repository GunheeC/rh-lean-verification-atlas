Statement

Assume the seven repository propositions GlobalArtinReciprocity, SiegelWeilUnitaryConvergent in the HeckeCharacterZeroLocus.SiegelWeilU namespace, DiscreteSeriesCover, HarishChandraModules, CuspRapidDecay, CasselmanWallach and DiscreteSeriesClassification, together with both estimates of T05. These propositions have not been compared here with a published formulation. For any proof $h_L$ of RecTExists, norm-choice extension over $h_L$, series extension, geometry and family predicate $N$, let $p$ be their extension parameters. Suppose T08's open conditions and the linked covolume, functional and $N$-norm conditions hold at $p$. Then all 26 large-slot row conditions hold for its built and good slots and its bundle linkage. They give class counts, the $j$-invariant, rank, the second complement, prime column valuations, arithmetic at bundle points, reference and twisted points and periods, the row tie, row bounds, twists, primitivity, lambda primes, divisors, decomposition points, selection jumps, the $L^p$ and order conditions, service, zero locus, the frame condition, irreducibility counts, definite rows and the $N$-norm condition. Each row condition has its own existential large-slot quantifier.

Assumptions

  • Use the seven repository propositions named in the statement and both bounds in T05.
  • Fix a proof of RecTExists and a norm-choice extension over that proof, series extension, geometry and family predicate. Require T08’s open conditions and the three linked bounds at their extension parameters.
