Statement

At compact open level over a totally complex field, the cardinality of any finite family of pairwise inequivalent cohomological cuspidal representations with a fixed vector is at most the dimension of the specified coefficient-system cohomology.

$$|S|\le \mathrm{cohDim}(\top,U,\operatorname{rat}_{\mathbb C}\Xi).$$

Assumptions

  • Use a totally complex field, positive rank, compact open level and the designated algebraic coefficient representation.
  • Take a finite family of pairwise inequivalent cuspidal representations that contribute to the specified cohomology and have a level-fixed vector.
