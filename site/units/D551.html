Statement

For each eligible CM subfield, procyclic local data and positive twist e, a power of the prime kills the minus kernel. Its exponent times log of the prime is bounded by C times the field degree, (e+1) and log(e times the prime times the conductor parameter).

$$\ell^r\operatorname{Sha}^{-}(D,e)=0,\qquad r\log\ell\le C[E:\mathbb Q]\,(e+1)\log(e\ell N).$$

Assumptions

  • The CM field embeds into the indicated cyclotomic field of positive conductor $N$.
  • Use procyclic local data at the chosen prime and an integral twist $e\ge1$.
