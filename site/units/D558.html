Statement

For an open normal subgroup in the character kernel with the stated two-primary restriction and a nonzero twist, cyclotomic restriction on the indicated locally trivial Selmer group is injective, with its image equal to the prescribed character eigenspace.

$$\ker(\mathrm{selRes})=0,\qquad \operatorname{im}(\mathrm{selRes})=\mathrm{shaEig}\bigl(\mathrm{twist}_2(\ell,\psi\chi_O^n),\ell,\mathrm{cyc}_U,U,1\bigr).$$

Assumptions

  • Take an open normal subgroup in the kernel of the given character and a nonzero integral twist.
  • At the prime two the subgroup satisfies the stated cyclotomic containment condition.
