P021 · A pointwise upper bound from weighted lattice slopes

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Eleven lattice hypotheses give an upper bound, at each unit, by a main term and an explicit error involving counting, division and slopes. Keeping those terms visible lets the arithmetic comparison determine whether their combined size fits the desired prime-sum bound.

Statement

For any data, constants and real $X_{pp}$ as above, assume the eleven hypotheses of Definition 6.38 (manuscript p. 69). Then for every $u_0\in U_0$

$$ASK(u_0)\le\mathrm{Main}(u_0)+\mathrm{Err}(u_0),$$

where, with $M_{Lo}=c_{Sl1}Lo_P+c_{Sl1}n_Tm(a/k+b')\,c_{Count}mL+c_{Sl1}X_{pp}+1$, $V=2mc_EB_0+c_H(n_TaLa+mB_0)+A\,c_{Count}\log k$ and $\bar U=c_{Slope}n_TaLa(2+m/k)+V+c_{Sl1}mB_0+M_{Lo}$,

$$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{Err}(u_0)={}&A\,c_{Count}\Bigl(La+\frac{m^2La}{k}\Bigr)+E_{Div}+m\,c_EB_0\\ &+c_{Slope}n_TaLa\Bigl(1+\frac mk\Bigr)+\log|W(u_0)|+Lo_P+M_{Lo}\\ &+\frac1{1-\bar\delta}\Bigl(4\log(1+x^{c_{HH}})\,(\bar U+b)+(\bar\eta+4\sqrt{\bar e})\,x^{c_{HH}}b\Bigr). \end{aligned}$$

Assumptions

  • Fix the finite unit and word-fibre data, constants and $X_{pp}$.
  • Assume the Hida setting, division bound, $E$ and $H$ comparisons, counting, slopes and quotient-slope bounds of the cited lattice definition.
  • Use LossBounds, SlopeIneq, HHBound and FParams on the same data (Definition 6.38, p. 69).
