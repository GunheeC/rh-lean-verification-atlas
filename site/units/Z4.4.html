Z4.4 · Twisted periods on the selected chain

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

With the stated pins, integral rows and tied character, one field element represents a chosen twisted period and satisfies the lower valuation bound at every allowed place. This makes the denominator estimate simultaneous for that period.

Statement

Assume Ok, Pins, the fixed laws and later data, integral rows, and a tuple tied to the prescribed $\xi$. For every divisor datum, used nonreference tuple, integral point and permitted form there is one field element realizing the twisted period. At each allowed $w$ it is zero or has valuation at least $-e_w v_p(D_{\Gamma,u})$.

Assumptions

  • Use the Ok and Pins records, fixed laws, later data and integral rows.
  • The tuple is tied to the prescribed character.
