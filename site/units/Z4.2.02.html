Z4.2.02 · Local realization of the tagged kernel

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At a standard served site, curve existence and a joint datum give one local realization of the tagged kernel. It includes a compatible embedding, a Lubin–Tate curve, stabilizers and adelic data obeying their laws, so those ingredients refer to the same site.

Statement

Given CurveEx, a standard served site and a joint $J$ for the fixed tagged kernel datum, construct an embedding compatible with the global field, a Lubin–Tate curve datum, finite classes and stabilizers, and adelic data $\Xi$ and $\mu_T$ satisfying the law, together in $J$.At.

Assumptions

  • Assume CurveEx.
  • Use a standard served site and a joint for the fixed tagged kernel datum.
