Statement

With the designated subgroup, kept characters and TT amplitudes, assume the derivative bound, normalization and three explicit error bounds r1–r3 by R. For every character psi in the subgroup band, $|TT|(\operatorname{vv}(\psi)-R)$ is at most the sum over t in TT of the real part of $\psi(e_1t)$ times the smoothed sum at $y_t$.

$$|TT|\,(\operatorname{vv}(\psi)-R)\le\sum_{t\in TT}\operatorname{Re}\bigl(\psi(e_1t)G_\psi(y_t)\bigr)\qquad(\psi\in\Psi_{A_0}).$$

Assumptions

  • Take $x\ge3$, a character in the subgroup band and sample points at distance at most one from $x$.
  • Assume the derivative estimate, nonnegative constants, kept-character normalization, share and functional bounds, and the three explicit errors bounded by the same $R$.
