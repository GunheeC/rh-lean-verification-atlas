Z1.1.05 · Automorphy of linked endpoints

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Given the automorphic hypotheses and the requisite dense seeds, sufficiently large linked frames support both rank-three and rank-two endpoint conclusions. The rank-three endpoint includes finiteness and automorphy of the inverse twist; one algebraic character realizes the rank-two comparison.

Statement

From $\mathrm{HypothesesH}'$ and the rank-two and dense rank-three seeds, for every sufficiently large admissible linked frame with a place above $\ell$, obtain rank-three finite points and cyclotomic-inverse automorphy, and one algebraic character giving rank-two automorphy.

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$ and the rank-two and dense rank-three seed assertions.
  • The linked frame is admissible and has the indicated place above $\ell$.
