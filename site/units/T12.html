T12 · The value of the doubled Eisenstein series

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For the specified definite signature, doubling model, measure and test vector, the Eisenstein sum continues to the required point and equals a normalized theta average there. The factor is one when the second rank is smaller and two otherwise; this normalization enters the theta comparison.

Statement

For every good number-field base $(F,E,\sigma,B)$ and spaces $A,Y$ of ranks $m,n\ge1$, take opposite Hermitian and skew-Hermitian signs, with the signature $(r,s)$ of $A$ satisfying $\min(r,s)=0$. Fix a Hecke character restricting as $\eta^m$, any finite-index Darboux doubling model satisfying its splitting, rationality, invariance and theta-summation rules, a frame, standard compact $K$ and height $a$. For every Haar measure with a fundamental domain $D$, every Schwartz vector $\Phi$ with finite-dimensional $K$-span, and every $x$, the flat-section Eisenstein sum for $g\mapsto l(\omega(g)\Phi)$ has a meromorphic continuation from a right half-plane, holomorphic at $s_0=(m-n)/2$, with value

$$E(x,s_0)= \begin{cases}1,&n<m,\\2,&m\le n\end{cases} \ \mathop{\mathrm{avg}}_{g\in D}\, \theta_F\bigl(\omega(x)S(g)\Phi\bigr).$$

The average is taken with respect to the specified Haar measure.

Assumptions

  • Use a good number-field base and positive-rank spaces of opposite signs; the first has definite signature.
  • Fix the specified Hecke character, Darboux doubling model, frame, compact subgroup, height, Haar measure with its fundamental domain and a Schwartz vector of finite compact span.
