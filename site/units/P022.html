P022 · Landau’s criterion on logarithmic windows

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A one-sided window bound for a function with the stipulated pole data forces every listed pole to lie on or to the left of the specified vertical line. This analytic implication explains why bounds on distant intervals can restrict the zeros represented by those poles.

Statement

Let $(g,\Phi,Z)$ be pole data, $0<\theta<1$ and $\delta>0$, and assume $\mathrm{W}(g,\theta,\delta)$. Then $\operatorname{Re}\rho\le\theta$ for every $\rho\in Z$.

Assumptions

  • The triple $(g,\Phi,Z)$ satisfies the pole-data record, including its growth, transform, isolated-singularity and residue conditions.
  • Take $0<\theta<1$ and $\delta>0$. The logarithmic window condition $W(g,\theta,\delta)$ holds.
