P047 · Four cases for the first column bound

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Four coefficient estimates, covering different prime and orbit cases, yield the required lower column bound for a used tuple at each place outside the specified exceptions. The stabilizer-denominator identity is needed in the remaining case, so the local bounds retain their dependence on that identity.

Statement

Assume the coefficient inequalities for orbits without the operator, medium inert primes, discriminant primes, and the remaining primes under the stabilizer-denominator identity. Assume the size and error definitions. Fix a word $u$, a used tuple $\mu$, a place $w$ of residue characteristic $p\ne T_u$ outside $\{g_0,g_1\}\cup Q(\mu)$, and $x\in S_{\rm ram}(u,w)$. The column of $T$ at $u,\mu,w,x$ has its prescribed lower bound $\operatorname{lowS}(u,w)$.

Assumptions

  • Assume the four coefficient cases, the size and error definitions and the stated stabilizer-denominator identity.
  • The tuple is used and the prime is off level, distinct from $T_u$ and outside the excluded set. The point lies in the indicated ramified set.
