D230 · Crystalline invariants over the maximal unramified extension

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A fixed crystalline period maps into the de Rham field as a scalar in the maximal unramified part of the chosen local field. The reduction first replaces the element by a fixed representative in the positive crystalline ring.

Statement

For an element of the chosen crystalline period ring fixed by $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K)$ (with $\ell$ the local prime), its image in the de Rham period ring is the image of an element of the maximal unramified subfield of K.

$$x\in B_{\rm cris}^{G_K}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists k\in K_{\rm ur},\ \iota_{\rm cris,dR}(x)=\iota_K(k).$$

Assumptions

  • Use the chosen crystalline period ring and its comparison map into the de Rham ring.
  • The given element is fixed by every automorphism of the algebraic closure over $K$.
