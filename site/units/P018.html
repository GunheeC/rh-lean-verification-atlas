P018 · The level window criterion

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A one-sided bound on the level prime sum over arbitrarily distant power windows excludes level zeros whose real part exceeds the chosen exponent. An entire interval at a time must satisfy the bound; this is the analytic criterion that the family estimate must feed.

Statement

Let $\mathcal L$ be a level, $\lambda>1$ and $0<\theta<1$. If the set $\{x:\mathcal G_{\mathcal L}(x)\le x^{\theta}\}$ has windows of exponent $\lambda$, then $\mathcal L$ is zero-free above $\theta$.

Assumptions

  • Fix a level, $\lambda>1$ and $0<\theta<1$.
  • The set of $x$ on which the level prime sum is at most $x^\theta$ contains power windows of exponent $\lambda$.
