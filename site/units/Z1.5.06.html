Z1.5.06 · Rational Frobenius trace bound

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The two counting and admissibility inputs give a polynomial-size list of possible traces of the specified inverse Frobenius power for admissible rank-two data. Absolute constants and independence from the odd coefficient prime make the list suitable for uniform counting.

Statement

CountQAlt and NewAdmAlt give absolute constants $c,A$. In particular, over every number field, for $e,M$ positive and $p$ away from $M$, the inverse Frobenius at $p$ raised to the given power has a trace classifier for Adm2B of size at most $c[M(e+1)]^A$. It works for every odd coefficient prime unequal to $p$.

Assumptions

  • Assume CountQAlt and NewAdmAlt.
  • The level and exponent are positive and the prime avoids the level.
