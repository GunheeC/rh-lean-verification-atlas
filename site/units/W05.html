Statement

Assume the rank-$n$ literature record. There is a real $C_K$ such that, for every prime $\ell$, totally complex field $L$ containing a primitive $\ell$-th root (a fourth root for $\ell=2$), algebraic closure and local data, and $e\ge1$, the group $H^2(G_{L,\ell},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1))$ is finite. With $r=[L:\mathbb Q]$ and $D=|\operatorname{disc}(L)|$,

$$\log\# H^2(G_{L,\ell},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1)) \le \exp\big((2er)^{C_K}\log(2D)\big).$$

Assumptions

  • Assume the rank-$n$ literature record.
  • In the bound take totally complex $L$, a primitive $\ell$-th root in $L$ for odd $\ell$ (fourth root for $\ell=2$), admissible local data and $e\ge1$.
