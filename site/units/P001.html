P001 · The Riemann hypothesis in Mathlib's formulation

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

This public goal uses Mathlib's formulation of the Riemann hypothesis. It asks that every zeta zero other than the excluded negative even integers, with the pole at one excluded too, have real part one half. The card pins the precise statement targeted by the conditional deduction.

Statement

For every $s\in\mathbb C$, if $\zeta(s)=0$, $s\ne1$, and $s\ne-2(n+1)$ for every $n\in\{0,1,\ldots\}$, then $\Re s=1/2$.

Assumptions

  • Take a zero $s$ of the Riemann zeta function with $s\ne1$.
  • Exclude all negative even integers $s=-2(n+1)$ for $n\ge0$.
