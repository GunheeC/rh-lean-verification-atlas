Z1.3.08 · Unramified classes in the ordinary normal form

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Statement

Combining the integral, Hodge–Tate and Hecke projection results with the local power-series test supplies ZetaUr for every ordinary normal form with its unramified weight-$2e+1$ modular source.

Assumptions

  • Fix an ordinary normal form.
  • Its modular source is unramified as specified and has weight $2e+1$.
