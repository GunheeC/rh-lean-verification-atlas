B04 · A local parameter with the tame type law

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The local parameter map attaches Weil–Deligne data to smooth representations of general linear groups. Its laws identify the dimensions, twists and particular tame inertia types needed when comparing local components with Galois representations. Several of the stronger laws require good cuspidal support.

Statement

For any number field $E$, algebraic closure $\Omega$, valuation subring of $\Omega$ over a finite place $w$, and resulting local field $E_w$, there exist an Artin map and a parameter map from irreducible smooth $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_w)$-classes of all ranks to Weil–Deligne parameters. The map preserves dimension, agrees with reciprocity in rank one, takes Langlands quotients of multisegments to the corresponding segment sums, takes cuspidals to irreducible parameters and commutes with real norm twists. The absolute value of its determinant recovers that of the central character. For classes with good cuspidal support it respects character twists, the full central character and duality, is injective against arbitrary classes, and yields a tame parameter in the presence of principal-congruence fixed vectors. Here each good cuspidal constituent has rank one or, over a field isomorphic to $\mathbb Q_p$, is a twist of a representation with such fixed vectors.

The last law prescribes a tame type. Over the indicated unramified residue extension of degree $n$, let $\vartheta$ be a regular residue-field character and let the inflated cuspidal type have the prescribed elliptic character values. The parameter has zero monodromy. At an inertia element whose Artin image reduces to $z$, its inertial characteristic polynomial is $\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(X-\vartheta(z^{p^j}))$, with restriction to the given base as in the local law.

Assumptions

  • Fix a number field, an algebraic closure, a valuation ring above a finite place and the corresponding local field.
  • For the tame-type clause use the residue-characteristic, level and character data in that clause.
