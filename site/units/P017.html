P017 · Chebyshev control on a chain-tied family

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

If one family satisfies the character conditions and its inequalities hold together with fixed constants on its window tail, its level prime sum obeys a half-power bound there. The explicit epsilon loss records the division and loss exponents. A separate argument must supply the family and its windows.

Statement

Let $\mathcal L$ be a level. For all $e_D,e_L\in\mathbb R$, $\varepsilon>0$, and every family $F$ at $\varepsilon$ satisfying EFamC$(\mathcal L,F)$, suppose there exist $K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x_0\in\mathbb R$ such that at every $x\ge x_0$ in $\mathrm{Win}(F)$ the condition ChainTied$(F;e_D,e_L,K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L;x)$ holds. Then some $x_1'$ satisfies

$$\mathcal G_{\mathcal L}(x)\le x^{1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon} \qquad(x\in\mathrm{Win}(F),\ x\ge x_1').$$

Assumptions

  • Fix a level, real $e_D,e_L$, $\varepsilon>0$ and a family $F$ at $\varepsilon$ satisfying the level explicit-formula condition EFamC.
  • There are fixed $K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x_0$ such that ChainTied holds on every window point $x\ge x_0$ for this family with those same constants.
