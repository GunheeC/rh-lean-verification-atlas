Statement

Fix a CM field and coefficient data as above, odd rank $a$, admissible field datum $d$ and $x\in E$ with $x^2=-d$. Fix the nonsplit exceptional set $T$, separated places $w_0,v'\mid\ell$ agreeing on integral elements of $\mathbb Q(x)$, and a finite set $S$. Let $U$ satisfy the automorphic condition off $T$, the specified $\ell$-type at $w_0$, the spherical condition off $w_0,S$, and the Satake-existence and lacunary-weight conditions at $v'$. Then there exists a continuous semisimple $\rho$ of some dimension with the source's Satake polynomial condition off a finite set of admissible places away from $\ell$, and a Hodge-jump witness containing $a$ lacunary integers. Call these common properties $V(\rho)$. At each such admissible $w\nmid\ell$ with Satake data one can insist on $V(\rho)$ and the polynomial condition at $w$ for every Satake multiset there. At each $w\mid\ell$ agreeing with $v'$ on $\mathbb Q(x)$, separated from $w_0,v'$ and having Satake data, the following two witnesses exist: one has $V(\rho)$, crystalline comparison and the prescribed weight jumps among its jumps at $w$; another has $V(\rho)$ and, for each Satake datum and inducing bridge, crystalline comparison and a degree $-1$ Weil element whose characteristic polynomial divides a power of the Satake polynomial. The dimension and representation may vary with the demanded witness.

Assumptions

  • Fix the CM and coefficient data, odd rank, admissible field parameter and a square root of its negative.
  • Use the prescribed nonsplit exceptional set, separated places above $\ell$ agreeing on the quadratic subfield, and the automorphic, local type, spherical, Satake and lacunary-weight conditions.
