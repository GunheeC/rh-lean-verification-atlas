Z2.K1 · Cyclotomic tower and localization kernel

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

One cyclotomic generator of exact level describes the localization-kernel invariant as a cokernel length in every allowed layer and positive twist. The formula permits studying the varying invariants through powers of a single action.

Statement

For a number field with the required roots of unity and $\ell$ prime, a single cyclotomic generator $\gamma$ has exact level $c$ and the base contains a primitive $\ell^c$-th root. For every $E\ge1$ and every layer $L_m$, its localization-kernel invariant is the cokernel length of $x_{\rm Tw}(E,\gamma)^{\ell^m}-1$.

Assumptions

  • Fix a coefficient prime and a base number field containing the required roots of unity.
