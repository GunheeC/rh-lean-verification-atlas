D097 · The crystalline locus in the ordinary local deformation space

The rank-three ordinary deformation datum has a specific saturated ideal in its universal coefficient ring. One assertion identifies a crystalline consequence of killing the ideal. A second says that a prescribed class of crystalline infinitesimal tests kills it.

Statement

For the universal ordinary rank-three local datum the saturated ideal has both crystalline-locus properties: its characteristic-zero points satisfy the specified crystalline condition and the admissible crystalline test lifts kill the ideal.

$$\mathrm{PtsCr}(D,U,I_Y(D,U))\ \wedge\ \mathrm{CrPts}(D,U,I_Y(D,U)).$$
