W09 · The individual bounds in Cases II and III

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

In each of Cases II and III, one exponent for the initial character gives a finite length bound for every qualifying character individually. The cases have different parity and divisibility requirements, so the estimates can be applied only after identifying the correct case.

Statement

Assume $H'$, W05 and the rank-four conclusions of W06. Each of the following two cases has its own $C\in\mathbb N$ depending only on $\chi$. For every large host, $0<e<t$, odd $\ell$, closure, coefficient ring and $\psi\in X_h(O,e)$ in the case, the length is finite and $v_\ell(\psi,e)\log\ell\le(\ell Ne)^C$:

  • Case II requires $e\ge2$, even order of $\chi_P$, and either $\ell\in B_\chi$ or $\ell-1\mid2e$.
  • Case III requires $\ell\notin B_\chi$, $\ell-1\mid2e$ and $\psi$ nontrivial of odd order.

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$, W05 and the rank-four input of W06.
  • Use the indicated large host, odd prime, coefficient ring and Case II or Case III character with $0<e<t$.
