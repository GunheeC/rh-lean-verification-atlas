Z2.K6 · Uniform layer count and the global kernel

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

If the equivariant character groups in all allowed layers have order at most one common bound, the base localization-kernel invariant is finite and the corresponding prime power is at most that bound. This passes from counts in the tower to a length at its base.

Statement

Let $L$ be totally complex with maximal $\ell$-power root exponent $c$ satisfying TopRoot, and $E\ge1$. If the specified equivariant character groups are finite of order at most $B$ for every allowed layer, there is a finite integer $s=\mathrm{shaInv}(L,\ell,E)$ with $\ell^s\le B$.

Assumptions

  • Use totally complex $L$, $E\ge1$ and TopRoot at the maximal root exponent.
  • On every allowed layer the designated equivariant character group is finite with the same order bound $B$.
