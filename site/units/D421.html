Statement

For each twist-good family satisfying the common normalization facts with nonnegative bound and the special bounds at two and three, there are uniform constants such that every eligible member with finite odd Selmer data admits a hub satisfying the required estimates.

$$C\ge0\ \wedge\ \mathrm{TwGood}(F)\ \wedge\ \mathrm{FactsFAlt}(F,C,C_T)\ \wedge\ \mathrm{BoundAtTwoThreeFamilyFonAlt}(F)\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{ConclF}(F)\quad(\mathrm{Hypotheses}_2).$$

Assumptions

  • Assume the second-layer hypothesis record, a twist-good family and its normalization facts for $C\ge0$ and the specified $C_T$.
  • The family has the bounds at two and three; the memberwise clause uses its specified odd Selmer finiteness condition.
