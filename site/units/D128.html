D128 · Integrality of the theta scalar on the integral models

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At a valued point of an admissible integral model, a theta key computed from a generating section comes from a unit of the valuation ring. The formula also tracks how a change of section affects tensor powers. This supplies the unit information needed when comparing integral frames.

Statement

At a valued integral-model point with the stated level, splitting and regularity data, a generating section gives a unit representing its theta scalar. For a general section with the required generator comparison and $8\mid N$, the normalized value $a\kappa^{N/8}$ is a unit. $\mathrm{KeyUnitOnModels}$.

Assumptions

  • Take a key rule satisfying its defining condition and a CM host with signature ranks of positive sum.
  • Use the stipulated base ring and prime-level structure on the model, including conditions N3, N4 and split fibres.
