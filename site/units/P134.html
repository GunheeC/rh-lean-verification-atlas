P134 · Depth-zero congruence in a represented base frame

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a represented point in a tied chart, conjugating a level-group element into each prescribed base frame gives the required depth-zero congruence for both the element and its inverse. This checks that the allowed coordinate change works in either direction.

Statement

For tied data, fix a chart $\beta$ at $i\le m$, a represented point $p\sim_{i,\beta}x$ and an element $\kappa$ of its level group. For every $P\in B$ write $k=\mathrm{rat}(b_P)^{-1}\kappa\,\mathrm{rat}(b_P)$. Then $\mathrm{Cong}_{s(P),0}(k)$ and $\mathrm{Cong}_{s(P),0}(k^{-1})$ both hold.

Assumptions

  • Use tied data and a chart at stage $i\le m$.
  • The point is represented in that chart, the element belongs to its level group, and the conjugating frame is the stated one at a place of $B$.
