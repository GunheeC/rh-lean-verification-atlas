Z1.3.05 · Nonvanishing at cyclotomic inertia

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a tuple satisfying the four specified conditions, some inertia element with trivial cyclotomic action and lower diagonal entry one gives a nonzero second cocycle coordinate. This detects the failure of a local coboundary description for that tuple.

Statement

Fix the coefficient ring, cocycle $y$, exceptional primes, twist, test characters and cyclotomic layers of an ordinary normal form. If the same tuple satisfies $\mathrm{Sys}$, $\mathrm{IwSys2}$, $\mathrm{SpLink}$ and $\mathrm{Pin7}$, some inertia element with trivial cyclotomic action and lower diagonal entry $1$ has nonzero second coordinate under $y$.

Assumptions

  • Fix the normal form and modular source with their local and cyclotomic parameters.
  • A single tuple satisfies Sys, IwSys2, SpLink and Pin7 for those data.
