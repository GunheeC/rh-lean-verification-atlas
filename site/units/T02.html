Statement

Assume the third-place and theta-local records $P,Q$, agreement of the theta integrals, local matching and the covolume bound. Fix any proof $h_L$ of the proposition UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists. Suppose that for every real $c$ the closed family bound at two and three gives the linked slot-norm assertion at parametersOfDependence$(h_L,c)$. Then there exists one record HypsAlt$(N,D_*)$, where $D_*$ is DefiniteSideCondition and $N(\mathcal C)=\mathrm{Closed}(\mathcal C,(F_m,H)\mapsto\mathrm{LinkN}(F_m))$. Its family class, raw selection and threshold function support the existence, row, period, spectral, mass and norm certificates together. The proof uses the same maximum of the grid and Fourier-fit thresholds for the linked rows. The predicate RecTExists asserts the existence, for each number field with algebraic closure and each place with an extension over it, of a map satisfying IsRecT. The quantified argument is evidence for this existence proposition. See nonempty_hyps_definite_side_of_closed_bound (SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean:44–68) and RecTExists (IsRecTOfIsRec.lean:78–80).

Assumptions

  • P is UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses and Q is ThetaLocalInputs. The three further inputs are ThetaIntegralsAgree, LocalMatching and CovolumeBoundOfX (SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean:44–45).
  • For each proof hL of RecTExists, the last premise supplies the linked closed small-prime bound at parametersOfDependence hL c for every real c. The local map is existential inside RecTExists, separately for each field, closure and place over a place (IsRecTOfIsRec.lean:78–80).
