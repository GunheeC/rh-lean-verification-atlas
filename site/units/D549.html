D549 · The omitted-frequency correction

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Only a controlled set of frequencies is omitted by the host character sum. Under the explicit-formula and compatibility assumptions, their total correction is at most four times the host order parameter times the scale to the half-plus-epsilon power.

Statement

With x>0, explicit-formula data and compatible character functional, the omitted-character correction SigA on the host subgroup and kept set is at most 4o x^(1/2+epsilon).

$$\Sigma_A(D,\ell,A_0,K_{\rm kept},x)\le4o\,x^{1/2+\varepsilon}.$$

Assumptions

  • Take $x>0$ and an axial host at the stated level and test set.
  • Assume its explicit-formula record and compatibility of the character functional.
