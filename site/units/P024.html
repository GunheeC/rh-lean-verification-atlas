Statement

Assume the automorphic hypotheses $H'$ together with weak descent, cyclic base change and quadratic descent, the specified quaternion existence theorem, and real semisimplicity of the cusp data.

  • For every admitted CM field $K$, coefficient ring, nonzero level, weight parameter, character and marked datum $D$, every $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$, and every valuation above $\ell$, there is a single $g_1$ satisfying the marking equations for $D$, the finiteness condition $\mathrm{FinEnd}$, and both automorphic end conditions, of ranks three and two respectively.
  • Suppose also that $[K:\mathbb Q]=2$ and $K$ contains a root of $X^2+1$. For every admitted host datum and host frame $F$ there is a marked $g_1$ such that for each universal representation $u$ at that mark, its weight-zero part is finite as a module over the coefficient ring. At every Type I specialization $r$ of $u$, the indicated matrix representation satisfies the prescribed automorphy condition over $K$ with twist $-1$.

Assumptions

  • Assume $H'$, weak descent, cyclic base change, quadratic descent, the quaternion existence input and real semisimplicity.
  • Use the prescribed admissible marked CM datum; the reduced host conclusion additionally assumes a quadratic $K$ containing a root of $X^2+1$.
