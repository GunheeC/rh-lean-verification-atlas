P015 · Primitive-character nonvanishing from Green, the Selmer window and the family witness

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Statement

Assume $\mathcal B,V,\mathcal F$. For every $\psi\in\mathcal P$ and every $s$ with $\Re s>1/2$, one has $L(s,\psi)\ne0$.

Assumptions

  • Assume Green's character statement, the Selmer window and a common definite-side family witness.
  • The character is in the prescribed primitive character class and $\Re s>1/2$.
