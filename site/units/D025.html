D025 · A nonzero Bessel period

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For a fixed discrete-series representation, a finite-dimensional comparison representation and a lowest-weight unit vector, two translates make the specified Bessel integral nonzero. The result supplies a detecting test even though a particular choice of translates could give zero.

Statement

For admissible discrete-series parameters with even $k\ge2$, interlacing and half-integral difference in the two-unitary-group setup, for every admissible discrete-series representation, comparison representation, lowest-weight unit vector and pair of Haar measures, two group translates give a nonzero matrix-coefficient Bessel integral.

$$\exists x,x_0\in U(H_{p_k}),\quad \int \langle\sigma(x_0)w,\sigma(uv)\sigma(x)w\rangle\,\overline{\psi_{\rm gen}(v)}\,\pi(u^{-1})\,d(\mu_W\otimes\mu_N)(u,v)\ne0.$$

Assumptions

  • Take the prescribed even rank, interlacing weights and half-integral discrete-series parameters.
  • Use the admissible representations, lowest-weight unit vector and Haar measures on the two integration factors.
