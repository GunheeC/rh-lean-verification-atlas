T08 · The twelve linked conditions at dependence parameters

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At any chosen dependence parameter, the third-place unitary hypotheses yield twelve open conditions on linked rows. Their shared parameter lets the all-row construction use geometric compatibility, type labels, multiplicities and the other row fields together.

Statement

Given $P$, every proof $h$ of RecTExists and every real $c$, the following twelve conditions are linked at $p(c)$: primitivity, lambda-prime bounds, divisors, decomposition points, selection jumps, ties, labels, goodness, weak geometric compatibility, multiplicities, kappa and defect. In particular the geometric field supplies a comparison with the prescribed point summands, class-sum normalization, point compatibility and the additional point condition More.

Assumptions

  • Assume the third-place unitary hypothesis $P$.
  • Fix a proof of RecTExists and a real dependence parameter and use the specified built, good and linked row data.
