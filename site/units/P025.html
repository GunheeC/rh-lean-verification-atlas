Statement

Assume local cohomological finiteness, Tate duality, the Euler characteristic formulas and the local Bloch–Kato theorem in the specified coefficient models. Assume the dense ordinary vanishing hypothesis: at every locally generic automorphic point below each allowed ordinary adjoint cocycle is a coboundary. Let $L$ be CM with $\mu_\ell\not\subset L$, take a Hida setting and level $V$ with $V.S'=S$, large coefficients, $2\le n\le4$, an admitted residual representation, and a locally generic automorphic point $x,\rho$ of its universal ordinary determinant ring. For each $v\mid\ell$ suppose there are strictly decreasing integers $m_i$ and $k>0$ such that on inertia

$$\bigl(\psi_{v,i}\psi_{v,j}^{-1}\chi^{m_j-m_i}\bigr)^k=1 \quad(i,j=1,\ldots,n).$$

For every allowed polarizing form and adjoint extension $R$, each continuous cocycle $z$ whose pullback satisfies the Bloch–Kato finite condition has the form $z(g)=R(g)X-X$ for one matrix $X$ and all $g$.

Assumptions

  • Assume local finiteness, Tate duality, Euler characteristics, Bloch–Kato local comparison and dense ordinary adjoint vanishing.
  • Use the admitted CM Hida setting with $\mu_\ell\not\subset L$, ranks two through four, a locally generic automorphic point and the stated inertia ratios for strictly decreasing weights.
  • For the chosen polarizing form and extension the pulled-back continuous cocycle satisfies the finite local condition.
