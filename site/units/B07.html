B07 · Character hosts on power windows

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

A Selmer bound uniform on the requested windows supplies hosts for a primitive character on windows of exponent three halves. At each eligible scale the host carries the required ranks, kept characters, estimates for the other characters and a finite localization budget together. Later estimates can therefore use one choice of host.

Statement

For each primitive character $\psi$, assume the bound of Section 2 (manuscript p. 7) at $t=\lfloor x^{401}\rfloor$ with the quantifiers of $V$ for this $\psi$. For every $0<\varepsilon<1/52$ there are $C_{T2},C_8,C_5,c_6,C_b,x_0$ with $C_8\ge1$ and $C_5,c_6,C_b>0$, and a system of scales with exponent-$3/2$ windows. At each eligible scale $s$ with $s.\varepsilon=\varepsilon$ and $s.x\ge x_0$ there exist the specified ranks, a host with invariant $C_{T2}$, and an auxiliary prime parameter satisfying the rank and host-size conditions. The character request at these data enforces the kept-character and share conditions for $\operatorname{cyc}(\psi)$, the pointwise bounds on the other characters (logarithmic-derivative constant $4$), and the finite-length Selmer bound with budget $\sqrt b$.

Assumptions

  • Fix a primitive character.
  • Assume its pointwise Selmer-window assertion with cutoff exponent 401, including the stated window and page-function quantifiers.
