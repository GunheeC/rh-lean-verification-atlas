P033 · The determinant evaluation on scalar prisms

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For the specified finite-field character and root of unity, an additive evaluator sends each scalar conjugation prism to the character of the determinant times the stated power. This provides an explicit way to distinguish homological cycles through their reductions.

Statement

Assume the chain, prism, prism composition and basic-cycle identities, the transfer of scalar functionals and the scalar cocycle formula. For any finite field $k$, positive integer $q$, $e\ge0$, primitive $q$-th root $\zeta\in k^\times$, and character $v:k^\times\to\mathbb Z/q$, there is an additive evaluating family $\theta$ of degree $2e+3$. For every finite $k$-vector space $V$, every $x\in\mathbb N$, and $g\in\mathrm{GL}(V)$,

$$\theta_V\!\left(P_g((j\mapsto\zeta^{xj}I_V)_*z)\right) = x^{e+1}v(\det g).$$

Here $P_g$ is the conjugation prism, and $z$ is the specified degree-$2e+2$ cyclic bar cycle.

Assumptions

  • Assume the chain, prism, composition and basic-cycle identities, the scalar-functional transfer and the scalar cocycle formula.
  • Fix a finite field, a primitive $q$-th root with $q>0$, the stipulated character and the specified evaluation data.
