Z1.5.04 · Classifiers over an imaginary quadratic field

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Over a fixed imaginary quadratic field, the classifier allows a variable symmetric purity weight. The same polynomially bounded list at the selected good place serves the allowed systems for every odd coefficient prime different from the residue characteristic.

Statement

Assume the automorphic hypotheses and $\mathrm{LevelPNAlt}$. For each imaginary quadratic field $K$ and $n\ge2$ there are $c,A$ such that for all strictly decreasing weights $m$ with $|m_i|\le H$ and $m_i+m_{\mathrm{rev}(i)}=\mathrm{wt}$, levels $M\ge1$ and places $w\mid p$ with $p\nmid M\,\mathrm{disc}(K)$, there is a single Frobenius classifier $D$ over $w$ of size at most $c[M(H+1)]^A$ for every allowed $\mathrm{AdmPNAlt}$ system at every odd coefficient prime $\ell\ne p$.

Assumptions

  • Use the automorphic hypotheses and LevelPNAlt over the imaginary quadratic field.
  • The weight is symmetric and the prime avoids the level and field discriminant.
