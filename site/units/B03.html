Statement

Suppose the geometric existence assertion in B02 and the stable-range theta lift specified in B01 both hold. For every CM field, closure, prime $\ell$, coefficient isomorphism, rank $a\ge1$ and regular algebraic polarised cuspidal pair, there is a single triple $(r,w_\pi,(H_\tau)_\tau)$ satisfying all the global, away-from-$\ell$ and above-$\ell$ clauses of B01.

Assumptions

  • Assume odd-rank geometric existence as in B02 and the stable-range theta-lift hypothesis used in B01.
  • Fix an algebraic closure of the CM field, $\ell$, the coefficient isomorphism and a regular algebraic polarized cuspidal pair.
