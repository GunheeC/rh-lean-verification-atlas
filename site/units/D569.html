D569 · Transport of the main term across the finite bijections

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Two finite bijections identify the lattice indices with the arithmetic indices. They transport the main term exactly, including its averaging factor. This lets the gap computation subtract the same main term on both sides.

Statement

Assume the per-unit arithmetic/lattice identifications, including the chosen bijections onto TT and the character set. Then for every u, Main(u) equals the sum over t in TT and j<m of the real part of MAIN(ell,a(t),w(tr(u),j)).

$$\mathrm{Main}(u)=\sum_{t\in TT}\sum_{j<m}\operatorname{Re}\mathrm{MAIN}(\ell,a(t),w(\mathrm{tr}(u),j)).$$

Assumptions

  • Fix the unit, translation map, top and place bijections, and compatible arithmetic coefficients.
  • The combined identification record holds for these very bijections.
