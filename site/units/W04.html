W04 · Weak unitary descent in ranks two through four

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

An eligible conjugate-self-dual cuspidal representation in ranks two through four has a weak descent to the specified unitary group. The descent is unramified at nonsplit places and matches the given Satake parameters at all but finitely many split places.

Statement

Let $E/E^+$ be CM and unramified at finite places. Fix a prime $p$ and a finite set $S$ of finite places of $E^+$ containing those above $p$, all split in $E$. Let $2\le n\le4$ and let $\pi$ be a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb A_E)$ with $\pi^\vee\simeq\pi^c$ and Satake parameters at all places above the complement of $S$. There exists an automorphic representation $\sigma$ of the specified unitary group, unramified at every nonsplit finite place. At all but finitely many split $w$ the component of $\sigma$, read on $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_w)$, has the same Satake multiset as $\pi_w$.

Assumptions

  • Take a CM extension unramified at finite places, a prime and a finite exceptional set of split places containing the places above it.
  • Use rank two through four and a regular algebraic cuspidal representation, conjugate self-dual with Satake parameters off the exceptional set.
