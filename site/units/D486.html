Statement

Assume Green’s finite-group character input and the closed existence of injectively labelled, joined and counted rows with cIc positive. Eventually at every index the row deviations are nonnegative and their delta statistic is at most $\exp(-x^{e_z/7})$.

$$\delta\le\exp(-x^{e_z/7}).$$

Assumptions

  • Assume Green's character input and $c_{\rm Ic}>0$.
  • Assume closed eventual existence of rows satisfying injective labeling, joining and counting on the same family.
