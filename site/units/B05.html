B05 · Connected families from Green and the closed link

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Under Green’s character input and the listed geometric, counting and compatibility assumptions, hosts on power windows and a Selmer bound supply families satisfying the tied inequalities. The division and loss exponents are fixed before the small parameter varies. The closed link is what relates the weighted local errors to the localization lengths on those families.

Statement

Assume the finite-field Green character statement. For any family class $\mathcal A$, take the data and hypotheses of the alternative shape: existence from the Minkowski division input, the family duties, and a twist selection with spectral, level, height, column and bounded-row realisations. Impose its twist, primitivity, division, point, size, literature, volume, decay and count conditions; its definite rows and indefinite selections; its point, period and complement conditions for the served twists; its closed count, projection and real-place ties; and its volume-ratio and genus-mass hypotheses. The two scalar predicates $V,NR$ in the shape are true. Its extra family premise is the following closed link on $D_3(3,\mathcal A)$: eventually there are common constants $C,x_0$ for a family so that, for each member at $x\ge x_0$ with finite prescribed localisation lengths and each lift $j$, summability of their weighted prime terms $v_\ell$ gives

$$\eta_j\le dZ_{0,j}\left[C\{fmbL^2+(\log x)^2N_c\} +\sum_\ell v_\ell\right].$$

Here $\eta_j$ sums weighted local errors at the varying places. The shape concludes that for every $\lambda>1$ and every request $G$, if hosts exist on $\lambda$-windows and every family in the request class eventually satisfies its Selmer bound, then there are $e_D,e_L$ and $\varepsilon_1>0$. For every $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_1$ that class contains a family with constants $K,c_{HH},e_K,C_L,x_0$ and tied data on every window scale $x\ge x_0$.

Assumptions

  • Assume Green's character statement and the alternative family shape with a common twist selection, its division input, duties, size and realization conditions.
  • Use the definite and indefinite selections, served periods and points, counts, projections, real ties, mass ratios and genus-mass inputs.
  • On the enriched class require the eventual closed Selmer-to-lattice inequality for every eligible lift with the prescribed summable prime terms.
