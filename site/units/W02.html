W02 · Isobaric base change for the definite unitary group

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

For an automorphic representation on the specified definite unitary group, this gives one family of isobaric constituents with compatible local parameters and archimedean weights. The Selmer-window construction uses this base change as a field of its automorphic input.

Statement

Let $E/E^+$ be CM, unramified at every finite prime, $n\ge1$, and $\pi$ an automorphic representation of the specified definite unitary group. There is a partition $n=\sum_i N_i$ with positive parts and a discrete $\mathrm{GL}_{N_i}(\mathbb A_E)$ constituent $V_i$ for each part. Each is an ascending constituent built from a normalized unitary conjugate-self-dual cusp form. There are local components $\sigma_{iw}$ and infinitesimal characters $m_{i\tau}$ of $V_i$. At each finite $w$ there is an irreducible subquotient $P_w$ of the local induced product of the $\sigma_{iw}$. It is spherical when $\pi$ is unramified there, has the base-changed Satake multiset for each inert unramified datum, and realizes the unitary local component at split places. At each embedding $\tau$, the sum of the $m_{i\tau}$ equals the infinitesimal character attached to each weight of $\pi$ at $\tau$.

Assumptions

  • Take a CM extension unramified at every finite place.
  • Use positive rank and an automorphic representation of the specified definite unitary group.
