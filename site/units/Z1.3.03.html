Z1.3.03 · Equivariant Hecke projection

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

At an allowed odd prime, a compatible Hecke splitting and projector give an integral equivariant projection. Scaling the finite functional by a prime power supplies its required normalization, allowing it to be used on the chosen rank-two component.

Statement

For ordinary rank-two data at odd $\ell$ prime to $N\ge5$, a compatible Hecke splitting and admissible projector polynomial, construct an integral Galois- and prime-to-level Hecke-equivariant projection and scale the given finitely supported functional by an $\ell$-power to satisfy IsLamFR2.

Assumptions

  • Fix rank-two ordinary data at an odd prime away from $N\ge5$.
  • Use the compatible Hecke splitting and an admissible projector polynomial.
