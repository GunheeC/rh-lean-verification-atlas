P013 · The Selmer window from the polarised row

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

The polarised row is the premise for the uniform Selmer-window assertion. The conclusion uses the interval from Y to its square and the twist cutoff with exponent 401. Its quantifier order determines which constants and intervals must work across later choices.

Statement

Assume $\mathcal R$. Then $V$ holds with the window $[Y,Y^2]$, the indicated quantifier order and the specified exponent $401$.

Assumptions

  • Assume the full polarized Galois row $\mathcal R$.
  • The window is tested for each primitive character, admissible page function and allowed positive exponent.
