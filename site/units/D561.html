D561 · Orthogonality under the translate average

AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Averaging the per-unit gap bound over subgroup translates bounds a normalized character-band contribution by the given error and an explicit width correction. The density factor in front of the contribution is needed when removing the normalization to obtain a prime-sum estimate.

Statement

For the fixed host, character data, positive b, and x at least 3, impose the size, thinness and cost windows, T2 inside the subgroup, and the per-unit gap bound Err. If |TT|Y is bounded by the TT sum of the subgroup-band character terms, then |TT| m b (delta2(T2)/2) Y is at most Err + 2|TT| m b/x.

$$|TT|\,m b\,\frac{\delta_2(T_2)}{2}Y\le\mathrm{Err}+\frac{2|TT|mb}{x}.$$

Assumptions

  • Assume the host prime facts, $x\ge3$, $b>0$, the size and thinness conditions, cost windows, and $T_2\subseteq A_0$.
  • Bound the gap by the same error for every $u\in A_0$ and give the subgroup-band sum a lower bound of $|TT|Y$.
