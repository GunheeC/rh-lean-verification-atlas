Levent Alpöge’s Lean development states the Riemann hypothesis for zeta and four further assertions about zeros of zeta or Dirichlet L-functions. A third-party audit records kernel acceptance, and this project did not reproduce that run. No human has read or checked the mathematical material in this atlas. The written deduction assumes V, F, Green’s finite-field character input, 31 local implications and the further inputs listed in v6, and V and F have no complete human-readable derivations in this atlas, although the Lean repository supplies terms for both. “Full” means the argument is written under the assumptions shown on its page. An outline still has intermediate reasoning to supply. A statement-only unit gives the claim and its source. Each grade records the evidence for that unit. The dashboard keeps AI review, independent corroboration and human checks in separate columns. These evidence levels are not cumulative prerequisites. L4 concerns the local written argument under its stated inputs. It gives no conclusion about V, F or RH. For each L4, read the number of readers, whether one is the maintainer and their declared AI assistance. Substantial assistance does not qualify as independent human reading. A second reader other than the maintainer or a text revision that supersedes the target can close a dispute. Reader names are self-declared and the repository does not verify identity.
