AI-written, AI cross-reviewed; no human sign-off; not a claim that RH is proved

Copy the fictitious worked YAML example into a new local file. Remove the example marker and replace all example values, including the fictitious non-attestation, with your own reading. Copy the edition from the unit checklist.

FieldContent
readerYour name, field and level (faculty, postdoc, phd-student, other). Affiliation and a checksum-valid ORCID are optional.
unit, edition_sha256The unit ID and the full 64-character edition hash.
scope, verdictThe steps checked, whole-note, or statement-vs-lean, with one verdict per target as described in the rules.
what_i_did, time_spent_hoursWhat you personally derived, followed or compared. Give hours as a nonnegative number.
reservations, conflicts_of_interestDescribe each limitation or conflict, or enter none.
ai_assistance_used, ai_assistance_descriptionOne of none, some, substantial, and what the tool contributed.
date, attestationYour reading date in YYYY-MM-DD format and the exact sentence “I read the edition identified above myself.”
