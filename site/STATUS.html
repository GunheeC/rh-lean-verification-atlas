The documents

Gunhee Cho’s paper, Toward a human verification of Alpöge's Lean proof of the Riemann hypothesis, I: the conditional chain from Selmer windows to zero-free regions, maps 775 Lean statements and follows the conditional deduction from the Selmer window to zero-free regions. The companion blueprint builds up the 20 original inputs from definitions and lemmas. With each edition, the atlas links steps in both documents to Lean declarations and review records so readers can check them by hand. AI systems drafted the texts under Gunhee Cho’s direction and cross-checked passages against the Lean source. No human, the author included, has yet checked the arguments. When a Lean result has a classical name

The Lean repository defines propositions under names taken from the literature and supplies them with its own terms. B04 restricts the stronger local parameter laws to specified classes. W11 gives a bounded-rank variant for a tower. T12 uses a particular special-value normalization. T17 uses this development’s arithmetic volume and intersection comparison.
