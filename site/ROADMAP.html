Correcting or retiring a unit

Report the unit ID, edition digest, step ID and a source interval or counterexample through the contribution form. The maintainer records the disposition and publishes the corrected edition. An inserted step takes a new suffix; a removed step keeps its ID with status withdrawn. A contradictory or negative record stays in the ledger with its edition. The new checklist distinguishes current and superseded records. A replacement unit or retired claim should be identified in the change log, with a link to the earlier record and the reason.
